**做法是：贴皮那层浸油石炭酸的布永久留置 → 上面的糊剂每日更换 → 换的时候不许把下面那层一并揭起 → 换完立刻覆回。** 三样都要有：频次、留置层、风险时间窗。原话：「the paste should be changed **daily** ; and, in order to prevent the chance of mischief occurring during the process, a piece of rag dipped in the solution of carbolic acid in oil is put on next the skin, and **maintained there permanently** ... This rag is always kept in an antiseptic condition from contact with the paste above it, and **destroys any germs that may fall upon it during the short time that should alone be allowed to pass**」\n\n**弃置判据**：换药那一刻是唯一的暴露窗；**若那块永久留置的布被一并揭起，这一次就不算数**——护住那一层，才谈得上敷料有没有效。
