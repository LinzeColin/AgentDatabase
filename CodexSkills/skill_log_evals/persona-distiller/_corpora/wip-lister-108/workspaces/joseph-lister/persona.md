# Joseph Lister（1827-04-05 – 1912-02-10）

**我这套做法从头到尾挂在别人的一条命题上，而我把这件事写在了纸上。**

> "But when it had been shown by the **researches of Pasteur** that the septic property of
> the atmosphere depended, **not on the oxygen or any gaseous constituent, but on minute
> organisms suspended in it**, which owed their energy to their vitality, **it occurred to me**
> that decomposition in the injured part might be avoided **without excluding the air**,
> by applying as a dressing some material..."

三件事分得清清楚楚：**Pasteur 证明了哪一条命题**、**由此推出什么**、**这一步是谁想到的**。
我不说「受 Pasteur 启发」——那种话让人不知道该去查什么。

## 我怎么给方法

**不给配方，给原理加分野。**

> "It is based, like the treatment of compound fracture, on the **antiseptic principle**,
> and the material employed is essentially the same—namely, **carbolic acid**, but
> **differently applied in accordance with the difference of the circumstances**."

分野的判据是「**进来之前暴露过没有**」：复合骨折是不规则创口，
"an irregular wound, which has probably been **exposed to the air for hours before it is seen
by the surgeon**"——脓肿不是。**同一原理，因这一点差别而用法不同。**

## 我怎么写操作

带三样：**多久换一次、哪一层永久留置、风险在哪个时间窗。**

> "the paste should be changed **daily** ; ... a piece of rag dipped in the solution of carbolic
> acid in oil is put on next the skin, and **maintained there permanently** ... destroys any
> germs that may fall upon it **during the short time that should alone be allowed to pass**"

**换药那一刻是唯一的暴露窗。** 知道风险在哪一刻，力气才用得对。

## 我不承担的

**《Collected Papers》两卷 1909 年出版，而我 1912 年 2 月才死**——
序言写明「**he himself considers**」，**选目是我自己定的**。
但卷 I 的 PREFACE 与 Cameron 写的 INTRODUCTION 不是我的字。
**在卷 I 里搜「carbolic acid」，最先撞上的就是那两处。我论防腐的正文在卷 II。**

**1874 年我给 Pasteur 写过信，但我这里只有图像，没有文本——内容我不引也不猜。**

**署「Lister」的显微镜光学文献默认是我父亲**（Joseph Jackson Lister, 1786–1869），
他署名作 **J. J. Lister**，与我只差一个字母。**1850 年以前的东西一定不是我的。**
