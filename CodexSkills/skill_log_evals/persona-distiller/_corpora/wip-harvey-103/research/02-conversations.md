# 02 · 问答路：九封书信、两篇驳论，**而每一封都隔着一层译者**

## 必须先说的一层

`src-9b38209bff18` 的九封书信是 **Willis 1847 的英译**——他自己在序里写
「The Letters, though frequently quoted, have never appeared in English before」。
**他的英文是他自己的译文，是你与哈维之间的一层。**

更远的还有：**Hofmann 那封只存于纽伦堡的一部印本书信集**（*Spicilegium Illustrium
Epistolarum ad Casp. Hofmannum*），Willis 是从那部印本转录的，**不是亲笔**。

## 真正的往复：具名对手、原话、以及他的回应方式

**Hofmann 的指控**（哈维逐字引出来再驳）：说他把自然指为
「a most clumsy and inefficient artificer」——一个最笨拙无能的工匠，
让血反复回心「uselessly spoiling the perfectly-made blood, merely to find her in something to do」。

**哈维的回应形态值得记下**：他**拒绝为一个自己从未提出的主张辩护**——
请对方去看「my eighth and ninth chapters」，那里他「purposely omitted to speak of
the concoction of the blood, and of the causes of this motion and circulation」。
落款 **Nürnberg, 20 May 1636**。

**Riolan** 承认有限的循环但让血穿过室间隔。哈维一句话打掉：
「I have nowhere assumed such a basis for my doctrine; for there is a circulation in
many red-blooded animals that have no lungs.」
他还诊断了对方的动机是制度性的：Riolan 身为巴黎学院院长「was bound to see the physic
of Galen kept in good repair … He has been playing the part of the advocate,
therefore, rather than of the practised anatomist.」

## 他对批评者的公开政策

`src-c587092e8f64`：「**Detractors, mummers, and writers defiled with abuse, as I
resolved with myself never to read them … so have I held them still less worthy of
an answer.**」后面接一句：「It cannot be helped that dogs bark and vomit their foul
stomachs, or that cynics should be numbered among philosophers.」

**这条政策是可核的**：`src-15ca17edb1d0` Primrose 1630 年——**出版仅两年后的第一部公开攻击**
——**哈维终身没有回应**。
