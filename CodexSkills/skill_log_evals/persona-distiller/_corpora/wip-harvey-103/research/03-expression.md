# 03 · 语体路：两篇献词、一部正文，以及 Aubrey 记下的口语

## 献词：政治比喻与优先权声明写在同一处

`src-9dc7dc8736b1` 致查理一世的献词把心与王并置：
「The heart of animals is the foundation of their life, the sovereign of everything
within them, the sun of their microcosm … The King, in like manner, is the foundation
of his kingdom, the sun of the world around him, the heart of the republic.」

致 Argent 院长那篇同时装着**优先权**与**政治计算**：
「having now for **nine years and more** confirmed these views by multiplied
demonstrations in your presence」；以及
「I was greatly afraid lest I might be charged with presumption did I lay my work
before the public at home, or send it beyond seas for impression, unless I had first
proposed its subject to you.」

> **「nine years and more」是本工作区认定循环说年份的唯一可靠出处。**

## 正文：他把归谬写成一串可以自己算的数

`src-0bcbf1b80d2b` 第九章：左室容量「two ounces, three ounces, one ounce and a half」；
每搏排出四分之一、五分之一、六分之一或八分之一；半小时逾一千次搏动，
「in some as many as two, three, and even four thousand」。
由此推出**十磅五盎司／二十磅十盎司／四十一磅八盎司／八十三磅四盎司**四组。

**论证不是任一数字为真，而是每一个都超过全身血量。** 并附他自己实测的对照：
「a fact which I have myself ascertained in the case of the sheep」。

## 口语：Aubrey 从 1651 年起记的

`src-a598fddaad3d`。论培根（他当过其医）：
「'He writes philosophy like a Lord Chancelor,' said he to me, speaking in derision;
'I have cured him.'」
论读书：「he bid me goe to the fountain head, and read Aristotle, Cicero, Avicenna,
and did call the neoteriques shitt-breeches.」
自评：「He was wont to say that man was but a great mischievous baboon.」

## 三种语体，三种用法

**献词体**用于对上与立优先权；**正文体**用于归谬与实验描述；
**口语**只见于 Aubrey 的转录——**而 Aubrey 论其为人一流、论其书目不可靠**，
用口语材料时须记住这条分界。
