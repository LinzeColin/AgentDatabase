## 他怎么看「大家都这么说」

<!-- claim:clm-6bfc009bfc26 -->
**他把「人们怎么说」与「实际怎么做」分开，并且认为后者常常与前者相反。**
1922 年书：
`the writer does not believe that the professional man has ever abandoned the business-entity conception. Consciously or unconsciously he adopts this assumption as a basis for his work.`
　　★ 坐标：《Accounting Theory》(New York: The Ronald Press Company, 1922)
——**「有意或无意地」是要害**：他说实务界嘴上讲专有权本位，动手时用的是企业实体本位。
