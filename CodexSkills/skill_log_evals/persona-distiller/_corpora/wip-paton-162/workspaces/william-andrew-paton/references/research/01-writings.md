## ★★★★ W1：他的立场是一个可辩论的主张，不是教科书叙述

来源：src-d24b0b5393b4

1922《Accounting Theory》全书围绕一件事：**专有权本位（proprietorship）会计对公司不成立，
应改用企业实体本位**。他在书里点名反驳 Sprague 与 Hatfield：

> It is the opinion of the writer that these doctrines of proprietorship, as propounded by
> Sprague, Hatfield, and others, are not an entirely adequate statement of the theory of accounts

> It is at this point, the writer believes, that the doctrines of proprietorship have led to serious error.

★★ **他知道自己是少数派，并且说出来**：

> Many would disagree with this statement, but the writer believes it is essen. tially sound.

★ `essen. tially` 是 OCR 把断字印成了**句点＋空格**，**照录未改**——
  我第一版写成 `essentially`，是把它悄悄修好了。

★★★ 而对另一类做法，他不留余地：

> Needless to say, perhaps, the writer views all such efforts as hopeless and the resulting rules
> as misleading and inadequate.

★ **注意分寸的差别**：对同行的理论他说「不够充分」「导致了严重错误」；
对「给估值定死规则」这类他说「毫无希望」。**同一个人，两种火力。**

## ★★★★ W2：1926 年那份会议发言——**全库唯一大量第一人称的一份**

来源：src-2e661bbd496a

《The Accounting Review》创刊号，他对 D. R. Scott 论文的现场答辩。**逐字记录，第一人称密集。**

他自述的教学法：

> I often tell my students that they must t 30 The Accounting Review distinguish between three
> things: (1) what I tell them, which is of the least importance; (2) what public accountants say

★★★ **照录未改**：`must t 30 The Accounting Review distinguish` 中间那段是**页眉插进了句子**
（OCR 把页码与刊名并进正文）。**不许把它抹平当成干净引文。**

他对实务与理论关系的态度：

> However, I think that it as quite possible to get an unduly narrow idea of what constitutes
> practice. Accoounting practice in the large should not be confused with accounting practice as
> it takes shape sole

（`it as` 与 `Accoounting` 是 OCR 讹字，照录。）

而他对同一场的对手：

> I am very much in agreement with Professor Scott, except that I go con- siderably further in
> condemning the cost or market whichever is lower proposition.

★★ **「我基本同意你，只是我走得比你远得多」——这是他和同行相处的典型姿态**，
与 W1 里对 Sprague/Hatfield 的写法是同一个人。

## Candidate Claims

**W-A（fact）**：**他主张用「properties／equities」两维取代「资产＝负债＋业主权益」**，
并在 1922 年书中点名反驳 Sprague 与 Hatfield。
- 证据 A：1922 原书三处 `the writer` 判断句（P1）
- 证据 B：Lawton 1923 书评的独立转述（S1，见 `04-external.md`）
- 语境：**专著 / 同时代书评**，两个语境

**W-B（heuristic，pattern 候选）**：**他把「我说的」放在证据序列的最低位。**
- 证据 A：1926 发言 `(1) what I tell them, which is of the least importance`（P1，逐字记录）
- 证据 B：同一场 `must apply all lines of theoretic reasoning with caution`
- ★★ **两条出自同一份来源**——按本项目口径**只算一处**，
  除非在别的作品里找到同形状的说法。**本轮没找到，故不立为 pattern。**

**W-C（boundary）**：**他的自称是 `the writer`，按 `I` 量会得到 0。**
- 证据：498 个 `I` 全量分类，属于他的 0 个

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **`the writer` 是 1920 年代学术散文的通行做法**，不是他个人的特征。
  ★ 本道用它来定位声口，**不把它当成关于他的断言**。
- **1926 发言是逐字记录，不是他写的文字**——口语与笔头的分寸可能不同。
  ★ 产物里引它时要注明是**发言记录**。
