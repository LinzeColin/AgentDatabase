# Work System / 工作系统

## 计划和分解

**定任务之前先测量，而且他明说「估」不算数。**

<!-- claim:clm-e6a806b50008 -->
1910 年《Work, Wages, and Profits》第 15 页：
`neither employer nor employee knows exactly in detail the best way of doing a piece of work, and,
as far as my own experience goes, they nm^er know ex-] actly how long it should take a good man]
fitted for the work, and provided with proper implements. Before intelligent action can be taken in
any case these facts must be known.`
（`nm^er` 即 never，`ex-] actly` 即 exactly，`man]` 即 man，**按 OCR 原样照录**）
同书第 74 页把「估」直接判掉：
`Estimates of a busy foreman as to how long it should take to do a new job must necessarily be
inaccurate, and rates set by his estimates are practically guesses.`
1914 证词里是同一条的现场做法：
`We do that before we set the task. We find out how much time it should reasonably take, and we do
not set the task for four hours unless we find that four hours is a reasonable time, and ample
time, to do the work`
以及公开性：`We particularly object to getting off in some comer and doing it.`
（`comer` 即 corner，**按 OCR 原样照录**）

★ 顺序是硬的：**测量 → 定任务 → 公开** 。跳过第一步的任务在他的口径里就是猜。

## 工具、执行和检查

**奖金制是三段一套，缺一段就不是它。**

<!-- claim:clm-6191f959a526 -->
1902 年 ASME v23 逐字：
`ll the work laid out for him, as consti- tuting his proper task for the day, he is, paid a definite
bonus in addition to the day rate which he always gets. If, however, at the end of the day, he has
failed to accomplish all of the work laid out, he does not get his bonus, but simply his day rate`
配套的是**信息给到工人手上**：
`As the time for each deta‘! operation is stated on the instruction card, the workman can see
continually whether he is earning his bonus or not`
（`deta‘!` 即 detail，那个撇号在语料里是 U+2018 不是直撇，**按 OCR 原样照录**）
1914 证词里是执行侧：
`We allow them a criticism in detail, and if there is any objec- tion to it in any way we go over
it part by part with them`（`objec- tion` 是跨行连字符）。

三段：**日薪永远给** → **完成全部任务才另给奖金** → **每道工序的时间印在指导卡上，工人自己看得见**。
★ 两处相隔 12 年、跨体裁（论文／宣誓作证）。
★★ **本条只说这套机制是什么，不说它有效**——见 `boundaries.md`。

**任务没完成时，第一步是把原因写下来查清楚；他给出的实测是原因更常在管理方。**

<!-- claim:clm-3cc3f6874fd9 -->
1914 证词描述的是一条硬规程：
`he must find out the cause of the loss and write it on the back of that card. We find that those
causes are very much more apt to be the fault of the management than the workman.`
1919 年《Organizing for Work》是同一条的结果侧：
`By systematically attempting to remove the obstacles which stood in the way of complete
accomplishment, we have secured a remarkable degree of co-operation, and developed in workmen
possibilities which had been unsuspected.`

★ 规程的形状是**记录 → 归因 → 由管理方去除障碍**，**不是「未达标即扣罚」**。
★★ 「原因更常在管理方」是**他自己口径下的自述**——
**本库没有任何卡片、报表或工厂侧记录能核这个比例。**

## 质量标准与交付定义

- **一件活的「该用多久」必须是量出来的**，工头的估计不算数。
- **量要当众量**，且允许对方逐条挑错。
- **未达成先记录原因**，且默认先查管理方（材料、工具、维护）。
- **给工人的信息要印在卡上，让他自己随时看得见**——不是事后通报。
- ★ **本节讲的全是他描述的制度。** 本库**没有任何一份工厂侧的实施记录**
  （没有合同、备忘、内部报告、往来书信）。**「这套办法在实际车间里怎样」，本库判不出来。**
