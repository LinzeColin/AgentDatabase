# Capabilities / 能力

★ 本页只列**有逐字证据的**能力，且每条注明证据落在哪一年、哪一种体裁上。

## 判一家厂效率高不高：换尺子，不换算法

<!-- claim:clm-33c8d161cabd -->
1914 年 ASME v36：
`the first step to be taken before introducing 7 a modern system of management is to eliminate the
ratio of “non- productive” to “productive” labor as a measure of efficiency.`
（`7` 是印本的节号混进正文，**按 OCR 原样照录**）
同篇：`total cost is the only reliable guide, will do much to pave the way for an improved system
of management.`
1915 年 Annals of the AAPSS v61：
`The old theory that it must be borne as a part of the cost of the articles produced is rapidly
giving way to the theory that it is a business expense and not chargeable to the articles pro-
duced.`

★ 两条是一套：**尺子换成总成本**，因此**闲置产能的钱不能藏进单件成本里**。
★★ Annals 那句用的是 `is rapidly giving way to`——**他在描述当时会计界的转向**，
不是单说自己的主张。**据此只能说他站在后一侧，不能说这个理论是他提的。**

## 把费用算到每一台机器上，并把闲置追到责任源头

<!-- claim:clm-3b82887f0cf1 -->
1917 年 ASME v39：
`we are able to determine for each ” machine in the factory both an idle- and an
operating-expense rate.`（`”` 是 OCR 噪声，**原样照录**）
1919 年《Organizing for Work》：
`In every case the responsibility for a condition is traced directly to its source. More- over, as
it is entirely possible to determine the expense incurred by idleness, such expense may be
allocated directly to the responsible parties.`

★ 落点是**责任可指名**，不是**成本更精确**——这是他与成本会计师的分歧所在。
★★ v39 那篇他自己写明这套办法**尚未到可详细发表的程度**，
**所以这是主张，不是成熟实践。**
