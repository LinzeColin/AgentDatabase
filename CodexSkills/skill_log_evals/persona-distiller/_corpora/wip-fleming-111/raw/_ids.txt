# ══ ★ HAZARD 1 — PENICILLIN ATTRIBUTION MUST STAY LAYERED ★ ══
#   The danger with this man is NOT a namesake, it is that the popular story deletes an
#   entire Oxford team. Both sides are in this corpus as PRIMARY material:
#     HIS half  — the 1928 observation and the 1929 paper really are his:
#         penicillin-1929-bjep        Br J Exp Pathol 10(3):226-236, full 13-page text
#         penicillin-letter-1941      he claims that merit himself, in his own words
#         nobel-lecture-fleming-1945  his own account of the early days
#     NOT his half — isolation, purification, clinical proof, 1939-1945, Oxford:
#         s1-oxford-purification-1942     Abraham, Chain, Holiday — the purification itself
#         s1-oxford-heatley-assay-1944    Heatley — the assay that made it measurable
#         s1-oxford-florey-survey-1944    Florey's own survey
#         nobel-lecture-florey-1945 / nobel-lecture-chain-1945
#     The adjudication, from the 1945 Nobel record itself:
#         nobel-summary-1945          prize share 1/3 EACH — Fleming / Chain / Florey
#         nobel-ceremony-speech-1945  Liljestrand states who did which part
#         nobel-banquet-fleming-1945  Fleming himself calls Florey and Chain "my partners"
#   So: neither "he alone invented penicillin" NOR any denial that 1928/1929 were his.
#

#     s1-sulphapyridine-fate-1940     its pharmacology, as understood at the time

#   challenge — recorded in _EXCLUDED.txt as blocked, NOT circumvented. One caution the
