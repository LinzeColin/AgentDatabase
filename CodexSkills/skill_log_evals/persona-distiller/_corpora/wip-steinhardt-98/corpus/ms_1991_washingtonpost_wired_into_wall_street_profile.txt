More than
34 years ago
Make us preferred on Google
By Brett D. Fromson
NEW YORK -- In most ways, it's been a great year for Michael H. Steinhardt, the head of a private investment firm that bears his name.
He stands to take home about $52 million this year -- spectacular compensation even by the standards of Wall Street -- and his investors, or limited partners, which include Yale University, the Carnegie Corporation endowment and Ameritech Corp., should earn another $450 million.
