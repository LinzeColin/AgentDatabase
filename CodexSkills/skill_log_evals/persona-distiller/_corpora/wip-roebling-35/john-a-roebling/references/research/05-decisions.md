## Source-linked observations

- **加劲方案决策**："But these do not only stiffen, they are also a great assistant to
  the cables."（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）——加劲梁/拉索与主缆协同的设计决策。
- **锚固决策**："Their number being limited, and the cables possessing an abundance of
  strength, I did not continue them over the towers to the anchorage."
  （`src-96ebd80c6e66`、`src-46dc13c375e1`）——以强度富余为前提简化锚固。
- **速度限制决策**："The first great object of this limitation of speed is safety."
  （`src-5d14356c3f4f`）——限速是安全考量。
- **材料检验决策**："I have tested them thoroughly by cutting up a number of extra bars
  and pins, and forging them over into various shapes."（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）——
  用破坏性试验验证材料。
- **风毁预防决策**：引用风毁案例（"destroyed by the momentum acquired by its own dead
  weight...wind"）作为加劲设计的依据（`src-46dc13c375e1`）。
