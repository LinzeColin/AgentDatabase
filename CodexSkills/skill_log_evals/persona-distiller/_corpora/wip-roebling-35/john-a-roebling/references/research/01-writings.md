## Source-linked observations

- **悬索桥刚度观**：Roebling 反复论证悬索桥可以做到与石桥/铸铁桥同等刚性——
  "I have built five such works, and two of them of large capacity and great extent, which
  have all proved successful, and are to all intents and purposes as rigid as stone or
  cast iron aqueducts."（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）。他对「悬索桥是松散织物」的流行看法
  明确反驳："Suspension bridges have generally been looked upon as loose fabrics swung up
  in the air, as if for the very purpose of swinging."（`src-46dc13c375e1`）。
- **试验与实测**：用加重的实验列车实测挠度——"The experimental freight train, which
  passed over the bridge on the 18th of March, and covered its whole extent, weighed about
  326 tons"（`src-46dc13c375e1`）；"The great experimental train, which covered the whole
  Bridge with loaded cars, propelled by two engines, produced a deflection of ten inches"
  （`src-5d14356c3f4f`）。
- **钢丝绳自产自用**：钢丝绳在其 Trenton 工厂制造——"These as well as the suspenders are
  all made of wire rope, manufactured at my works at Trenton"（`src-96ebd80c6e66`、
  `src-46dc13c375e1`）。
- **温度与缆索**："The camber of the floors and the deflection of the cables, as you well
  know, depend upon the temperature of the atmosphere."（`src-5d14356c3f4f`）；
  "I have made with wire of 1000 feet long, their expansion caused by an increase of
  temperature of one degree is 1-146000"（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）。
- **风致失效教训**："That Bridge was destroyed by the momentum acquired by its own dead
  weight, when swayed up and down by the force of the wind."（`src-46dc13c375e1`、
  `src-96ebd80c6e66`）——指早前悬索桥的风毁案例，是他加劲设计的直接动因。
- **成本与混合材料**："The mixed application of timber and iron in connection with wire,
  renders it possible to put up so large a work at so small a cost."（`src-46dc13c375e1`）。
- **测试方法**："I have tested them thoroughly by cutting up a number of extra bars and
  pins, and forging them over into various shapes."（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）。

## Candidate Claims

- 悬索桥可通过加劲梁/拉索达到与刚性桥同等刚度（`src-96ebd80c6e66`、`src-46dc13c375e1`）。
- 钢丝绳自产（Trenton 工厂）是工程实现的关键支撑（`src-96ebd80c6e66`）。
- 试验列车与实测数据是设计验证的第一手段（`src-5d14356c3f4f`、`src-46dc13c375e1`）。
- 温度效应是缆索桥设计必须量化的变量（`src-5d14356c3f4f`、`src-96ebd80c6e66`）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

Roebling 的自评带有工程辩护立场——1860 报告是给两家桥公司的正式陈述，对安全与刚度的
措辞偏乐观；1892 再版（其身后）保留了 1855 Final Report 原文。与 1857 年 Niagara 桥
风振事故（`src-46dc13c375e1` 提及的 "destroyed by...wind"）对照时，注意其「已解决」
叙事与后来 Brooklyn 桥（1883）实际仍受风振困扰之间的张力。

## Unknowns and source gaps
