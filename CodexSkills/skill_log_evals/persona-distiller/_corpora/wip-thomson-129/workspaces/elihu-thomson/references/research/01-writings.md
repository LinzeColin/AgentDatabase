## Source-linked observations

### 一、★★ 他给一个领域下判断时，**同时给出它的上界与下界**

`science-1899-field-of-experimental-research` 开篇：

> `THE FIELD OF EXPERIMENTAL SESEASOH* Physical research by experimental meth- ods is both a broadening and a narrowing field.`

（`SESEASOH` 是 `RESEARCH` 的 OCR 讹字，`meth- ods` 是原文的跨行连字符——**照录，不改**。）

同一篇里他把「限度」讲了四遍，每一遍都指向可测的东西：

> `We can imagine no limit to possible increase of temperature, such as is the absolute zero a limit of decrease.`

> `These limits are, however, already «et for us by the vaporization of all known substances.`

> `The limit of possible strength of the containing receptacle, or some unforeseen factor, would probably set the new bounds.`

> `Investigations of the efiects of low temperature upon the properties of bodies must, from the present outlook, be forever limited`

**「能到哪里」与「到不了哪里」是同一句话的两半。**

### 二、★★★ 他把「我没做完」写进正文

> `Some six or seven years ago I endeav- ored, while working upon the subject of high frequency, to fill the gap.`

> `The result was an apparatus which, with its modifica- tions, deserves more study and experiment than I have been able to give to i`

**自己的装置、自己承认研究得不够。** 这与他在讨论环节的形态是同一个（见 `02-conversations` 第三节）。

### 三、限制知识主张的措辞是显式的

> `We are, however, not justified in going beyond the proved facts which can only be the result of experimental work and close observ`

> `Whether these phenomena will ever be brought within the field of research by experimental methods is an open question.`

★ 注意后一句的形态：**不是「不可能」，是「是个未决问题」。**

### 四、成本被当作研究的实际边界，而不是抱怨

> `The expense or cost, if not the difQculty of the work, may become so great as to effectually bar further progress in some fields.`

> `He must also learn to render available to science the resources of the larger workshops and industrial es- tablishments.`

**「学会调用大工场的资源」被他列为研究者要学的本事之一**——
这与他本人同时是发明家兼企业技术负责人的位置一致，**但语料里没有一句自述这层关系，不许替他补。**

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **W-4 不等于「他主张科学要商业化」。** 语料里那一句讲的是**研究者要能调用资源**，
  不是「研究要服务商业」。**两者不许混说。**
- ★ `aiee-v4-1887-novel-phenomena` 这一份的开头几行是**学会当天的会务记录**
  （`It was voted to take a recess for lunch, until 2 P. M.`），**不是他的话**——
  引用本件时须避开卷首会务段。
