## 一、说一个领域能到哪里时，同时说它到不了哪里

1899 年那篇综述里，「限度」出现了四次，每一次都指向可测的物理：

> `We can imagine no limit to possible increase of temperature, such as is the absolute zero a limit of decrease.`

> `These limits are, however, already «et for us by the vaporization of all known substances.`

> `Investigations of the efiects of low temperature upon the properties of bodies must, from the present outlook, be forever limited`

**上界与下界是同一句话的两半。** 全库 18 份语料里出现同类限度词。

★ **可能的另一种解释**：这也可能是当时科普写作的通行修辞，而非我的判断方式——
**本库分不开这两者。**
