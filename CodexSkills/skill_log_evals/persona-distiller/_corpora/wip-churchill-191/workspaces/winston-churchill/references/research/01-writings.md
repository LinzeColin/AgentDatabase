### O-1 · 把「宗教狂热」这个现成解释先否掉，再给自己的

`src-925ef102d33e`（1899，《The River War Vol I》）：

> `It is, I believe, an historical fact that the revolt of a great popiilation has never been caused solely or even mainly by religious`

「It is, I believe, an historical fact that the revolt of a great population has **never** been caused solely or even mainly by religious enthusiasm」——
他不是补充一个新因素，而是**先把当时最流行的那个解释整条划掉**。
判据：`I believe` 与 `an historical fact` 落在同一句里——**标明这是我的判断，同时把它当事实主张下**。

### O-2 · 用一句反讽收掉一场争论

`src-934c9771e8e3`（1900，《London to Ladysmith via Pretoria》）：

> `’ But if they will invent a system of inoculation against bullet wounds I will hasten to submit mysel`

谈接种争议时他不站队，最后一句是：**若他们能发明一种防子弹的接种法，我马上去打**。
判据：**争论以一个具体到荒谬的条件收尾**，而不是以结论收尾。

### O-3 · 把选择写成一句「负担不起」

`src-615074b71a84`（1923，《The World Crisis 1911-1918》）：

> `If Germany was going to create a Navy avowedly measured against our own, we could not afford to remain ‘ in splendid isolation ’ from the European systems. We must in these`

「we could not afford to remain 'in splendid isolation'」——
他讲外交转向时不谈是非，谈**负担得起负担不起**。
判据：战略判断用**成本语言**下，而且引号里那个现成说法**是被他拿来否掉的**。

### O-4 · 手段与目的分两半说

`src-925ef102d33e`（1899，《The River War Vol I》）：

> `It is the means by which we fight ; the end at whic`

「It is the means by which we fight ; the end at which we aim.」——
一句话拆成对仗的两半，**手段一半、目的一半**。判据：他给定义时爱用这种两半式。
