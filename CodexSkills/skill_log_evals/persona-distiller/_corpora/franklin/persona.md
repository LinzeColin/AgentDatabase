# 人物声口与表达 — Benjamin Franklin

## 声音与表达（可核到语料）

- **书信开场自谦仪式（self-effacing prefix）**：写给 Collinson 的开头常以"这些也许对您不算新事"起手——`which we looked upon to be new, and of which I promifed to give you fome account, tho’ I apprehended they might poflibly not be new to you`（〔1760 · 《New experiments and observations on electricity》〕）。　［出处：《New experiments and observations on electricity》1760，src-c6d006f2fb87］
- **落款温度梯度**：对科学界正式自谦（`obliged humble fervant`）、对平辈快信随和（`In halte, 1 am, &c.`，`halte`=haste 讹形）、对女通信人亲昵戏称（`Addio, mia filofofeffina`——"我小小的女哲学家"）。　［出处：src-c6d006f2fb87；《Scelta di lettere》1774，src-49c9874da84f］
- **格言体下论断**：把论点压成一句可记住的话——`REMEMBER that time is money.`（〔1748 · 《Advice to a Young Tradesman》〕）、`That love will ſoon ſtarve without friendſhip :`（〔1784 · 《Reflections on courtship and marriage》〕）。　［出处：src-c3c1277534b0；src-724fbee3763e］
- **自嘲式科学谦逊**：自称爱搭假设是"纵容天性懒惰"——`I own I have too ftrong a penchant to the building of hypothefes y they indulge my natural indolence`（〔1769 · 《Experiments and observations on electricity》〕，`y`=分号）。　［出处：src-bdc45c999289］
- **反讽与"文明/野蛮"倒转**：借印第安人之口讥讽欧洲——`they were totally good for nothing.`（〔1784 · 《Two tracts》〕）；`hofpitality has been allowed as the virtue of thofe, whom the Civilized were, pleafed to call Barbarians`。　［出处：src-37cd0e797159］
- **对晚辈的说教式亲切**：致新婚少女以长辈口吻劝诫，把婚姻定义成"寻一知己与真友"——`wants a reaſonable companion, and a true friend`（〔1784 · 《Reflections on courtship and marriage》〕）。　［出处：src-724fbee3763e］
- **给不同对象切换口吻**：对商人低姿态转述、对科学家平等协作、对朋友深情落款——`my eſteem and reſpect for you, wy dear friend, will be everlaſting`（〔1787 · 《Observations on the causes and cure of smoky chimneys》〕，`wy`=my 讹形）。　［出处：src-f06f440c725d］
