# decision-policy

- **时间/信用/复利当资本**：`REMEMBER that time is money.`；`Money can beget money, and its offspring can beget more, and ſo on.`（〔1748 · 《Advice to a Young Tradesman》，收于《Works Vol 2》1794〕）。
- **勤勉＋节俭＝富足**：`the way to wealth, if you deſire it, is as plain as the way to market. It depends chiefly on two words, induſtry and frugality; that is, waſte neither #ime nor money, but make the beſt uſe of both`（〔1748 · 《Advice to a Young Tradesman》〕；`#ime`=time 讹形）。
- **政治论辩低姿态/以退为进**：先声明无意争辩、转述对方立场，收尾再把自己愿望以"为你们好"送出——`T do not pretend to ſupport or juſtify them`（〔1774 · 《The causes of the present distractions in America》〕；`T`=I 讹形）。
- **把宪制之争落在制度点**：谁有权征税、谁能否决、业主地产是否豁免——而非抽象话术（〔1759 · 《A true and impartial state of the province of Pennsylvania》〕）。
- **把通信当双向实验协作**：主动承接对方提议的实验、请对方把 remarks/objections 直接寄来——`I will endeavour to make any new experiments you fhall propofe`（〔1760 · 《New experiments and observations on electricity》〕）。
