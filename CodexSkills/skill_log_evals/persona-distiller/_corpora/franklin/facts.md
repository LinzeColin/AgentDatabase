**风筝实验验证"电与雷同质"（lightening=electric fire）**：1769《电学实验与观察》卷（1747 年起致 Collinson 书信汇编）自述费城用风筝把云中电引下来——`As foon as any of the thunder clouds come over the kite, the pointed wire will draw the eledric fire from them`，并断言 `the fame- nefs of the eledric matter with that of lightening completely demonftrated.`（`fame- nefs`=sameness、`demonftrated`=demonstrated 的 OCR 形，`fame`=same）；1760《New Experiments》Letter X 复述同一实验"已在费城用更简便的不同做法成功"——`it may be agreeable to inform the curious that the fame experiment has fucceeded in Philadelphia, though made in a different and more eafy manner.`（`fame`=same、`fucceeded`=succeeded、`eafy`=easy 讹形）。　［出处：《Experiments and observations on electricity》1769；《New experiments and observations on electricity》1760］

**殖民地人口-经济因果链（地贱→敢结婚→每二十年翻番→劳动贵→无廉价制造→殖民地是市场非对手）**：1760《论大不列颠的利益》附《Increase of Mankind》（正文署 *Written in Penſylvania, 1751*），从"地贱"推起——`Land being thus plenty in America, and ſo cheap as that a labouring man, that underſtands huſbandry, can in a ſhort time fave money enough to purchaſe a piece of new land ſufficient for a plantation, whereon he may ſubſiſt a family; ſuch are not afraid to marry`，再推到 `our people muſt at leaſt be doubled every twenty years.`（美洲人口每二十年至少翻番）与"制造源于贫困"定理：`Manufactures are founded in poverty. It is the multitude of poor without land in a country, and who muſt work for others at low wages or ſtarve, that enables undertakers to carry on a manufacture`；1784《两篇短论》从劳动稀缺侧复现同一判断——美洲工匠求学徒、甚至倒贴钱：`fo defirous of apprentices, that many of them will even give money to the parents.`（`defirous`=desirous 讹形）。　［出处：《The interest of Great Britain》1760；《Two tracts》1784］

**电学正负（plus/minus）命名与尖端引放电效应（避雷针原理的实验基础）**：1769《电学实验与观察》卷首书信里，他用"盈/亏"给实验命名——`B is eledrifed plus-, A, minus. And we daily in our experiments eledrife bodies plus or minus, as we think proper.`（`eledrifed`/`eledrife`=electrified 的 OCR 讹形，`-,` 为页折行伪影）；并把尖端的"引/放电"效应记为首要现象——`The firft is the wonderful effedt of pointed bodies, both in drawing off and throwing off the eledtrieal fire.`（`firft`=first、`effedt`=effect、`eledtrieal`=electrical 讹形）。同一套正负术语在 1758 德文译信《Briefe von der Elektricität》里复现：`plus, weniger aber negativ oder minus`——正负命名跨语言稳定。　［出处：《Experiments and observations on electricity》1769；《Briefe von der Elektricität》1758（德文译本）］

> **何时作废**：若档案证明正负命名先见于他人著作（如 Nollet 或更早欧洲文献）⇒ 本条须改为"富兰克林书信中最早的 plus/minus 用法之一"
