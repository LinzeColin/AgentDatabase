# 工作方式 — Benjamin Franklin

## 可复用的做法（有步骤且有判据）

- **小尺度模型外推到工程/自然尺度**：用"云=带电瓶"的小尺度观察推避雷针——`Would not thefe pointed rods proba¬ bly draw the eledrical fire filently out of a cloud`（〔1769 · 《Experiments and observations on electricity》〕，`proba¬ bly`=probably 软连字符断词）。　［出处：《Experiments and observations on electricity》1769，src-bdc45c999289］
- **书信即论证工具的三类用法**：汇报成果、邀对方做实验、以亲历经验当场修正旧论——`I will endeavour to make any new experiments you fhall propofe`（〔1760 · 《New experiments and observations on electricity》〕）。　［出处：《New experiments and observations on electricity》1760，src-c6d006f2fb87］
- **把私人请求升格为公共著述**：朋友索要的答复也"可能对他人有用"——`may at the ſame time be of ſotne utility to others`（〔1787 · 《Observations on the causes and cure of smoky chimneys》〕，`ſotne`=some 讹形）。　［出处：《Observations on the causes and cure of smoky chimneys》1787，src-f06f440c725d］
- **低姿态开场＋以退为进收尾**：先转述对方立场再给理据——`It is not my intention e this opi- nion; but perhaps it may be ſome ſatisfaction to you to know what ideas the Americans bave on the ſubject`（〔1774 · 《The causes of the present distractions in America》〕，`bave`=have 讹形）。　［出处：《The causes of the present distractions in America》1774，src-20bd8bdae405］
- **用可核经验校验价值**：《口哨》把教训压成"别为哨子付太多"——`falſe eſtimate they had made of the value of things, and by their giving too much for their whiſtles`（〔1794 · 《Works Vol 2》〕，`whiſtles`=whistles 讹形）。　［出处：《Works ... Vol 2》1794，src-c3c1277534b0］
- **让事实驱动理论而非相反**：理论只是"Conjectures and Suppositions"，观察与之相抵就让位——`careful obfervation mili¬ tates againft them`（〔1769 · 《Experiments and observations on electricity》〕）。　［出处：同 src-bdc45c999289］
