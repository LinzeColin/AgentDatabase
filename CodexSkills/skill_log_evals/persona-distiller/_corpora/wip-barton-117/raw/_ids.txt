# ══ HOW THIS LEDGER WAS PRODUCED ══

# ══ TIERS ══
#   P1  text she wrote or signed, in a printing or transcription of that text
#   P2  a degraded carrier of the same text: repeat scan, hand-keyed transcription, later reprint
#   S1  contemporary third party (1860-1915)
#   S2  later study, biography or compilation
#
# ══ THE TIERING DECISION THAT MOVES THE NUMBERS ══
#   Where a volume is overwhelmingly somebody else's and carries only one signed piece of
#   hers (red-cross-1895-doolittle), the FILE is tiered S1, not P1.  Her section is still
#   usable and its exact line range is in _BOUNDARIES.json.  The two big biographies
#   (Epler 1915, W. E. Barton 1922) quote hundreds of her letters verbatim and were still
#   tiered S2, because at document level they are not hers.
#
# ══ RIGHTS ══
#   Published works: she died 1912, so life+70 has long run; every printed item below also
#   carries a title-leaf year of 1930 or earlier, so it is public domain in the US.
#   Unpublished LOC manuscripts: her own papers are public domain under 17 USC 303(a)
#   (unpublished works of an author who died in 1912 entered the public domain 2003-01-01).
#   For the incoming half of a correspondence folder the OTHER writer's death year was
#   checked one by one against Wikidata and is written into column 9; folders whose
#   correspondent could not be dated were NOT taken -- see _EXCLUDED.txt.
#
