## ★★ Source-linked observations 一：她在自己的报告里主动把功劳记在别人名下

她那份报告的开头与中段，逐字照录（**OCR 讹字保留原样**）：

> `To  the  People  of  the   United  States  of  America :`
>
> `Having,  by  official  invitation,  been  placed  upon  an  erpedition  to`
> `Andersonville,  for  the  purpose  of  identifying  and  marking  the`
> `graves  of  th«dead  contained  in  those  noted  prison  grounds,  it  is`
> `perhaps  not  improper  that  I  make  some  report  of  the  circumstances`
> `which  induced  the  sending  of  such  an  expedition,  its  work…`
>
> `During  a  search  for  the  missing  men  of  the  United  States  Army,`
> `commenced  in  March,  1865,  under  the  sanction  of  our  late  lamented`
> `President  Lincoln,  I  formed  the  acquaintance  of  Dorence  Atvvatcr,`
> `of  Connecticut,  a  member  of  the  2d  New  York  cavalry,  who  had`
> `beon  a  prisoner  at  Belle  Isle  and  Andersonville  22  months,  and`
> `charged  by  the  rebel  authorities  with  the  duty  of  keeping  the  Death`
> `Register  of  the  Union  prisoners  who  died…`
>
> `By  minute  inquiry,  I  learned  from  Mr,  Atwater  the  method`
> `adopted  in  the  burial  of  the  dead…`

**这是本人物最强的一条可核证据**：
安德森维尔一万三千座墓的名录，其底本是 **Atwater 冒险抄录并私藏的死亡登记册**，
**而她在自己署名的官方报告里，把他的姓名、部队番号、被囚 22 个月、
以及登记册是他的职责，逐项写了出来。**

**她没有把他的工作吸收成自己的。** 这也解释了为什么两人的文字装订在同一卷里
——**那一卷本来就是两个人的联合呈报，不是她的书里混进了别人的话。**

结尾同样值得记（第 722–728 行）：

> `and,  grateful  for  the  opportunity,  I  hasten  to  place  beside  it  this`
> `humble  report,  whose  only  merit  is  its  truthfulness,  and  beg  you  to`
> `accept  it  in  the  spirit  of  kindness  in  which  it  is  offered.`
>
> `CLARA  BARTON.`

`whose only merit is its truthfulness` —— **她给自己的报告只认领一项优点：属实。**
与 01 写作路的「报告体」、03 表达路的 `render my account` 同一条线。
