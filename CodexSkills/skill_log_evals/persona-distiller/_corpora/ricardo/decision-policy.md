# 经验判据 — David Ricardo

**先定义再演绎**：立定义、设假设（如"货币价值不变"）、再推可检验命题，是他对付 Smith/Say/Malthus/Thornton 一贯的打法〔1821 · On the Principles〕。**用算术例证做演绎载体**：100/90/80 夸特演示地租、100:80 人·年演示比较优势——`Thus England would give the produce of the labour of 100 men, for the produce of the labour of 80.`〔1821 · On the Principles〕。**金价高于造币厂价即贬值**：`the high price of bullion was the test on whicli I most relied for the proof of depreciation`〔1811 · Reply to Mr. Bosanquet〕。**发行只盯金价不盯数量**：`The issuers of paper money should regulate their issues solely by the price of bullion, and never by the quantity of their paper in circulation.`〔1816 · Proposals〕。**税收看归宿不看名义负担**：生原料税既非地主也非农民承担，`it woidd be paid, in an increased price, by the consumer`〔1821 · On the Principles〕。**目标先立再评**：完美的通货 = 标准不变 + 始终贴合标准 + 极致节约〔1816 · Proposals〕。
