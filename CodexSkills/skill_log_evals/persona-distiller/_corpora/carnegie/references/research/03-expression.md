## Source-linked observations

### ① 《A League of Peace》（1905 圣安德鲁斯校长就职演说，1906 刊）——把"废除战争"当学生必须投入的头等公民义务

`src-eabcbd209294` 是面向圣安德鲁斯学生的校长就职演说。**开场即把责任压到学生肩上**：

> you are busily preparing to play your parts in the drama of life, resolved, I trust, to oppose and attack what is evil, to defend and strengthen what is good, and, if possible, to leave your part of the world a little better than you found it
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

- **给战争定性**——人类文明的"最大污点"（借卢梭的话，`altho`=although 拼写照录）：

> there still remains the foulest blot that has ever disgraced the earth, the killing of civilized men by men like wild beasts as a permissible mode of settling international disputes
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> War is the foulest fiend ever vomited forth from the mouth of Hell
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

- **历史铺陈法**：从古希腊 Amphictyonic Council、Grotius、Cruce 一路讲到海牙会议——用"人类反战史"做论证，把和平主义写成文明进程的必然方向（改述衔接）：

> The Hague Conference was the first ever called to discuss the means of establishing peace without reference to any particular war
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

- **"和平联盟"方案**——明确提出"任何国家不得开战、一律交海牙仲裁"、拒绝者被"断绝往来"，并**保留联盟用武力的权利**（这不是无原则和平主义，而是"以联盟之力维持和平"）：

> no nation shall go to war, but shall refer international disputes to the Hague Conference or other arbitral body for peaceful settlement
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> the League agreeing to declare non-intercourse with any nation refusing compliance
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> of all the modes of hastening the end of war this appears the easiest and the best
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

- **给学生的行动指令**——"把和平置于政党之上"、必要时与自己的党决裂；并**把经营哲学的"集中"直接搬进政治**：

> demand at once that your Government offer to refer it to arbitration, and if necessary break with your party. Peace is above party.
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> It is by concentrating upon one issue that great causes are won.
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> Refusal to arbitrate makes war, even for a good cause, unholy
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

- **反战=反"战争是英勇"的叙事**——借林肯做"集中打击单一敌人"的榜样（`man-slaying`/`man-selling` 的对应是他最爱的修辞结构）：

> It is a melancholy fact that nations looking back have usually to confess that their wars have been blunders, which means they have been crimes.
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

> Let us, as he did, subordinate all other public questions to the one
<!-- src-eabcbd209294 -->

（收尾处华盛顿"我的首要愿望是看到战争这个人类瘟疫从地球消失"与林肯"像打击奴役一样打击战争"并列，改述。）

⇒ 声口/论点：校长训话的口吻——祈使句密集、把抽象议题落到"你们该怎么做"；把经营上"集中兵力"的策略与道德动员（林肯的"唯一的压倒性议题"）接在同一套心智上。**"集中于一役"是他从钢铁到和平运动贯穿始终的表达指纹。**

### ② 《Edwin M. Stanton》（1906 肯扬学院纪念演讲）——共和/联邦的"爱国主义声口"

`src-5a457dfece71` 是肯扬学院 Stanton 纪念日的纪念演讲，把 Stanton 塑造成"联邦的化身"。**演讲含 Carnegie 本人的童年记忆**（他亲见过 Stanton）——罕见的自传性瞬间：

> it was there in his early prime that I, as telegraph messenger boy, had the pleasure of seeing him frequently, proud to get his nod of recognition as I sometimes stopped him on the street or entered his office to deliver a message.
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

- **对"联邦永存"的绝对化表述**（他称这是"Stantonese"——以 Stanton 的措辞立其人的信条）：

> The Union is necessarily perpetual. No state can lawfully withdraw or be expelled from it.
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

> Patriot at eighteen, patriot always, the needle not truer to the pole than Stanton to the Union.
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

- **复述他人话语**——他大段转述 Stanton 在内阁的强硬表态、林肯的评语、Grant 的信念信、Horace Greeley 的悼文，用"他人之口"为 Stanton 背书：

> its surrender by the Government would be a crime equal to that of Arnold
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

（Greeley 悼文 "it is to Edwin M. Stanton, more than to any other individual, that these auspicious events are now due"——第三方赞美被整段征用，改述。）

- **道德收束**——把 Stanton 的榜样定为"为他人高服务"、反衬"以财富为目的"的时代风气；并**把美国联邦与欧洲常备军对比**（和平主义伏笔）：

> a career spent, not in pursuit of miserable aims, which end with self, but in high service for others
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

> Dispensing hundreds of millions yearly, he lived without ostentation, and he died poor.
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

> in contrast to Europe with its huge armies, organized not against foreign foes but for protection against each other
<!-- src-5a457dfece71 -->

⇒ 声口/论点：纪念体的"爱国主义叙事"——用最高级形容（"Kenyon 最杰出的儿子"）、用第三方悼文背书、用戏剧化引语（Arnold/Andre）渲染危机；同时夹杂私人记忆与和平主义的旁白。**"引他人之口"与"私人记忆点缀"是此演讲的两大表达特征。**

### ③ 《The Negro in America》（1907 爱丁堡哲学学会演讲）——"数据+渐进改良"的种族观声口

`src-4769b2a7c8a7` 是面向爱丁堡哲学学会的演讲，用人口/识字率/财产数据论证美国黑人的进步。**开篇定调"这是当下最有意思的种族运动"**：

> No racial movement in the world to-day is more interesting; few, if any, are more important.
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

- **"奴隶变公民"的慢功夫**——诗的乐观与政治家的耐心对照：

> The shackles fall, but the citizen fails to emerge.
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

- **教育=道德炸药**（名句，`mora`/`dyna-mite` 跨行断词已归一）：

> Education is moral dynamite which invariably explodes into rebellion
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

> Ignorance is the only possible foundation upon which dominion over others can rest.
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

> Intelligence forces equal rights
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

- **数据叙事**——识字率从 1870 的 83.5% 降到 1900 的 47.4%、农场/房产数据、银行与职业清单；把"数据"当最有力的说服工具（与《Triumphant Democracy》的"facts 糖衣药丸"同一方法，见 01 道）：

> The saving man is par excellence the model citizen — peaceable, sober, industrious and frugal. The magic of property works wonders indeed
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

> Home is the cradle of the virtues
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

- **私有财产=反社会主义的堤坝**（与 01 道《Problems》的边界观同族）：

> This is our bulwark in America against revolutionary or socialistic ideas.
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

- **对普选权的谨慎**——"立刻无条件普选"是 `a choice of evils`（选择两害之轻），更倾向"教育测试"式的渐进（改述衔接）：

> Perhaps the best defense of the measure is that it was a choice of evils.
<!-- src-4769b2a7c8a7 -->

（结尾引 Rev. Lyman Abbott 的话背书——`never in the history of man has a race made such educational and material progress in forty years as the American negro`，此句声口归 Abbott，非 Carnegie 亲述，须标注。）

⇒ 声口/论点：慈善家兼统计师的声口——数据铺陈+道德判断（"储蓄者是最佳公民"）+渐进改良处方（教育/财产/私人所有权）；对南方的"穷白人"与黑人关系、对普选保持审慎。**"以数据立论、以私有财产为解毒剂"是其种族议题的表达指纹。**

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **"废除战争"与"联盟可用武力"的并存**：他一边把战争称为"人类最大污点"，一边主张"和平联盟"对拒绝仲裁者可动用武力——这是他和平主义的**制度设计**（以集体武力防单边战争），不是主张无抵抗。引用时不能把他说成"绝对反战主义者"。
- **数据立论的"乐观滤镜"**：Negro 演讲里全是进步数字，他自陈"亮处只是黑暗中的小点"（改述），并引 Abbott 背书——声口上"乐观+审慎"并存；且卷首传记（Committee of Twelve 所写）把他捧为"THE MILLIONAIRE EXEMPLAR OF SERVICE WITHOUT RECOMPENSE"，那是编者溢美层。
- **引语归属必须分清**：Negro 卷首传记引的 `The man who dies rich, dies in disgrace` 与 `What a man owns is already subordinate in America to what he knows` 均出自编者传记，**不是演讲正文**；Stanton 演讲里 Greeley 悼文、林肯/格兰特信件是"他转述的他人话"。下游一律标声口层。
