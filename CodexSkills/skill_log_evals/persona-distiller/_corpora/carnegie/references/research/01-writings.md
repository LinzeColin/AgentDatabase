## Source-linked observations

### ① 《The Gospel of Wealth》（1901 卷）——散财哲学的总纲（1889 原文收于此卷）

`src-8a5d4e1793b6` 收 1889 年《财富的福音》等文。**核心命题：死时巨富即死时蒙羞**（OCR 把引号丢掉了，照录）：

> The man who dies thus rich dies disgraced.
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

围绕它，富人被定义为"社会盈余的受托人/代理人"——本段页边注噪声极重（行尾夹 `9%`、`(5` 等扫描伪影），只取可核片段，`tiustee`=trustee 的 OCR 讹形照录：

> a man of wealth thus becoming the mere—tiustee
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

> which proclaims him only a trustee of: the surplus
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

（同章以"死后留下巨富者将 `pass away unwept, unhonored, and unsung`"收束——`mil_ lions` 系 `millions` 的 OCR 讹形，`_` 是折行连字被打坏，照录：）

> the man who dies leaving behind him mil_ lions of available wealth, which was free for him to administer during life, will pass away unwept, unhonored, and unsung
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

- **反"无差别施舍"**——慈善做错比不做更糟（`eure`=cure 的 OCR 讹形，见文末注）：

> the millions of the rich were thrown into the sea than so spent as to encourage the slothful, the drunken, the unworthy
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

（句首 "It were better for mankind that" 在语料中跨行且首词受损，以改述衔接。）

> Of every thousand dollars spent in so-called charity to-day, it is probable that nine hundred and fifty dollars is unwisely spent
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

- **穷家孩子的优势**（`ean`=can 的 OCR 讹形照录）——为穷男孩辩护、认为"有钱人的儿子"反而更不幸：

> the poor boy who has in his father his constant companion, tutor, and model, and in his mother—holy name!—his nurse, teacher, guardian angel, saint, all in one, has a richer, more precious fortune in life than any rich man's son who is not so favored ean possibly know
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

- **最佳用途清单**——公共图书馆、大学、实验室、公园；强调"生前亲自散财"而非"死后遗赠"：

> a noble public library, where the treasures of the world contained in books will be open to all forever, without money and without price
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

> The man who builds a university, library, or laboratory performs no more useful work than he who elects to devote himself and his surplus means to the adornment of a park
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

- **竞争观的"接受"**（同卷《The Advantages of Poverty》一类段落；`beneftetal`=beneficial 讹形照录）——承认竞争带来的不平与阶级，但认为它"对种族有益且不可或缺"：

> the law of competition between these, as being not only beneftetal, but essential to the future progress of the race
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

> Rigid castes are formed, and, as usual, mutual ignorance breeds mutual distrust
<!-- src-8a5d4e1793b6 -->

⇒ 声口/论点：把财富伦理写成"责任"——富人是受托人，社会之善在于生前把盈余用于最有利于大众的方向；对直接撒钱与死后遗赠都持否定；贫富差距被当作竞争法则的代价而接受。这是"散财哲学"的一手主证。

### ② 《The Empire of Business》（1902 卷）——经营方法论与钢铁经济学

`src-b465964357a3` 收 1885 年《The Road to Business Success》、1891《The A B C of Money》、1900《Thrift as a Duty》、1901《The Bugaboo of Trusts》《The Three-Legged Stool》等。**给年轻人的劝诫是"集中、储蓄、不投机"**：

> IT is well that young men should begin at the beginning and occupy the most subordinate positions.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> I would not give a fig for the young man who does not already see himself the partner or the head of an important firm
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> The rising man must do something exceptional, and be- [版口：THE ROAD TO BUSINESS SUCCESS ii] yond the range of his special department. He must ATTRACT ATTENTION.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

（"be-"与"yond"之间横着一道页眉 `THE ROAD TO BUSINESS SUCCESS ii`，用 [版口] 标注、分段各自命中。）

> Always break orders to save owners. There never was a great character who did not sometimes smash the routine regulations and make new ones for himself.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> There is one sure mark of the coming partner, the future millionnaire; his revenues always exceed his expenditures.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

（"百万富翁=蜜蜂/未来百万富翁"一段，`millionnaire`=millionaire 的法式拼法照录；"第一笔攒下的一百美元最关键"：）

> It is not capital that your seniors require, it is the man who has proved that he has the business habits which create capital
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> Gentlemen, it is the first hundred dollars saved which tells.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **成功大秘密=集中**（与 League of Peace 的"集中于单一议题"同构，见 expression 道）：

> concentrate your energy, thought, and capital exclusively upon the business in which you are engaged
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> "Don't put all your eggs in one basket" is all wrong. I tell you "put all your eggs in one basket, and then watch that basket."
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **反投机**（投机者与商人走的是分岔路）：

> The speculator and the business man tread diverging lines
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **财富伦理在此复现**（"以财富本身为目的极端卑下"）：

> As an end, the acquisition of wealth is ignoble in the extreme
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **节约=文明的分界**（`civiHzed`=civilized、`aU`=all 的 OCR 讹形照录）：

> the habit of thrift constitutes one of the greatest differences between the savage and the civiHzed man
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> thrift is mainly at the bottom of aU improvement
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> expenses should always be less than income
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **钢铁经济学**——"最便宜钢铁"的国家优势论、原料成本核算（`potmds`=pounds 讹形照录）、竞争让消费者受益、费率"滑动制"与劳工共赢：

> the nation that makes the cheapest steel has the other nations at its feet so far as manufacturing in most of its branches is concerned
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> The cheapest steel means the cheapest ships, the cheapest machinery, the cheapest thousand and one articles of which steel is the base
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> three and a third potmds of raw material have been made into one pound of steel
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> In 1873, only twenty-seven years ago, the United States produced 198,796 tons of steel
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> What we must seek is a plan by which men will receive high wages when their employers are receiving high prices for the product, and hence are making large profits; and per contra, when the employers are receiving low prices for product, and therefore small if any profits, the men will receive low wages.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

> Wages should be based upon a sliding scale, in proportion to the net prices received for product month by month.
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

- **关税立场**（1891 年《The A B C of Money》）——不反一切关税，只反"超出保护本国市场所需的"关税：

> I am not in favour of protection beyond the point necessary to allow Americans to retain their own market in a fair contest with the foreigner
<!-- src-b465964357a3 -->

⇒ 声口/论点：面向年轻人的励志训话+面向公众的经济解释；把"集中/储蓄/不投机"当成功铁律，把"最便宜成本"当国家竞争力；同一套"把一切落到成本与价格"的心智贯穿全卷。此卷是"经营方法/成本观/节约观"的一手主证。

### ③ 《Triumphant Democracy》（1886）——共和优越论与"美国即未来"

`src-d1a85cb73073` 是 1886 年的政论巨著，献给美国共和政体。**序言自陈写作动机**——让英国人了解"共和国真正是什么"（自比"养子的感恩献礼"）：

> I have tried to coat the wholesome medicine of facts in the sweetest and purest sugar of fancy at my command
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> no liberties have been taken with facts, figures or calculations
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> the tribute of a very dutiful and grateful adopted son to the country which has removed the stigma of inferiority which his native land saw proper to impress upon him at birth
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

- **共和 vs 君主制的"无特权"论**：

> There is not one shred of privilege to be met with anywhere in all the laws. One man's right is every man's right.
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> No ranks, no titles, no hereditary dignities, and therefore no classes. Suffrage is universal, and votes are of equal weight.
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> Government of the people, for the people, and by the people is their political creed. The vote of an Emerson or a Lincoln weighs no more than that of the poorest negro.
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

- **共和与和平的联结**（这是 1886 年就已埋下的和平主义伏笔，与 1906《League of Peace》呼应）：

> The reign of the masses is the road to universal peace.
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> We stand for the rights of man, the day of kings and peers is past. Down, privilege, down. Ring in the reign of the people, the equality of the citizen.
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

- **移民观**——共和国把"臣民变公民、农奴变人"，并赐免费公共学校：

> They reach the shores of the Republic subjects (insulting word), and she makes them citizens; serfs, and she makes them men
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

> gives them, without money and without price, a good primary education as the most precious gift
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

- **进步主义基调**（劝美国人"更以制度为傲"）：

> they may be still prouder and even more devoted if possible to their institutions
<!-- src-d1a85cb73073 -->

⇒ 声口/论点：以统计+慷慨修辞论证"共和优于君主、美国优于旧世界"；"事实为体、修辞为饰"（facts 的"糖衣药丸"）是他政论的一贯做法。此卷是"民主观/移民观"的一手主证。

### ④ 《Problems of To-day》（1908）——财富、劳工与社会主义的晚年立场

`src-5512c8642a23` 是 1908 年文集（Wealth—Labor—Socialism 三题）。**开篇即承认"财富分配不均是社会主义活动的根源"**，并自陈这些观点早在成百万富翁前就已持有：

> The unequal distribution of wealth lies at the root of the present Socialistic activity.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> Multi-millionaires, a new genus, have appeared, laden with fortunes of such magnitude as the past knew nothing of.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> the writer held and expressed advanced views upon "Labor" and "Land" before he could be ranked as one of the
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

（后文 "multi-millionaires" 在语料中作 `multi-` 折行+`millionaires`，连字归一后成 multimillionaires，与引文写法不一致，故引文止于 "one of the"，余改述。）

- **对社会主义"取其公平诉求、拒其革命手段"**——累进遗产税"正当且严格公平"，但那是"亚当·斯密式的健全教义、无甚社会主义色彩"；唯有及时让步温和派的合理诉求才能击败革命社会主义：

> A heavy progressive tax upon wealth at death [版口：12 PROBLEMS OF TO-DAY] of owner is not only desirable, it is strictly just.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> revolutionary Socialism is successfully to be combated only by promptly conceding the just claims of moderate men
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> By taxing estates heavily at death the State marks its condemnation of the selfish millionaire's unworthy life.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

- **储蓄存款 vs 国家没收**——储蓄（"moth nor rust"）的危险全在社会主义要把它们转归国家：

> Their only danger lies in the Socialistic aim to remove them from present owners and transfer them to the State
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

- **劳工=资本的合伙**（1898 Homestead 图书馆开幕演说）——资本、经营才能、熟练劳工是"三足凳"；劳工上升的终极出路是"合伙/持股"：

> A partnership of three is required in the industrial world when an enterprise is planned
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> Labor, Capital, and Ability are a three-legged stool. There is no first, second, or last. There is no precedence! They are equal members of the great triple alliance which moves the industrial world.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> The wheels cannot revolve unless Skilled Labor starts them
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> In this, the writer believes, lies the final and enduring solution of the Labor question.
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

> The Carnegie Steel Company made a beginning by making from time to time forty-odd young partners, only one was related to the original partners
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

- **对"胡乱撒钱的慈善家"的辛辣反讽**（"一个钱比理智多的慈善家"分钱给穷人只会害人）：

> a philanthropist — which generally means a man with more money than sense — resolved to act upon that idea, and distribute his fortune among the poor of London or New York
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

- **人类进步论与"兄弟情谊"的乐观**：

> Never was there so much of the spirit of brotherhood among men, never so much kindness, never so much help extended by men
<!-- src-5512c8642a23 -->

⇒ 声口/论点：晚年文集的态度是"接受社会主义的公平批评、拒绝其革命处方"；劳工问题的答案不是分配革命而是"合伙制+教育+财产私有权"。与 1886《Triumphant Democracy》的乐观相比，此卷多了一层对社会主义运动的警惕（见 divergence-map）。

## Candidate Claims

- C-W1（fact）：散财哲学核心——"死时巨富即死时蒙羞"，富人是社会盈余的受托人（`The man who dies thus rich dies disgraced`；`a man of wealth thus becoming the mere—tiustee`；`which proclaims him only a trustee of: the surplus`，src-8a5d4e1793b6）。
- C-W2（fact）：生前散财优于死后遗赠——遗赠被讥为"抛下带不走的俗物"（`will pass away unwept, unhonored, and unsung`；图书馆"值得永存、不要遗赠"的 Tilden 例，改述），src-8a5d4e1793b6。
- C-W3（mental-model）：竞争观——"适者生存"是价格下降与消费者受益的机制，竞争对种族有益且不可或缺（`the great law of the survival of the fittest vindicates itself`，src-b465964357a3；`the law of competition between these, as being not only beneftetal, but essential to the future progress of the race`，src-8a5d4e1793b6）。
- C-W4（mental-model）：成本/规模观——"最便宜钢铁"的国家碾压别国、原料成本核算、垂直整合（`the nation that makes the cheapest steel...`；`three and a third potmds of raw material have been made into one pound of steel`，src-b465964357a3；自传《Law of the Surplus》同簇，见 06 道）。
- C-W5（mental-model）：共和优越论——美国无特权、平等投票、免费公共学校是最珍贵礼物、共和通向普世和平（`There is not one shred of privilege...`；`The reign of the masses is the road to universal peace`，src-d1a85cb73073）。
- C-W6（mental-model）：劳工=资本的"三足凳"合伙观+费率滑动制（`Labor, Capital, and Ability are a three-legged stool`；`Wages should be based upon a sliding scale`，src-5512c8642a23 + src-b465964357a3）。
- C-W7（boundary）：对社会主义——接受其公平诉求（累进遗产税"正当且严格公平"）、拒绝革命手段与没收储蓄（`revolutionary Socialism is successfully to be combated only by promptly conceding the just claims of moderate men`；`Their only danger lies in the Socialistic aim...`，src-5512c8642a23 + src-8a5d4e1793b6）。
- C-W8（value）：财富只是手段不是目的（`As an end, the acquisition of wealth is ignoble in the extreme`，src-b465964357a3）；节约=文明之分界（src-b465964357a3）。
- C-W9（heuristic）：成功法=集中+储蓄+不投机（`concentrate your energy, thought, and capital exclusively...`；`put all your eggs in one basket, and then watch that basket`；`The speculator and the business man tread diverging lines`，src-b465964357a3）。
- C-W10（fact）：1860s-1900 钢铁扩张的量化自述（1873 年 198,796 吨钢、原料成本链），src-b465964357a3。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **"接受竞争"与"反对无差别竞争后果"的并存**：同一位作者既说竞争法则"对种族有益且不可或缺"（Gospel），又在《Problems》里承认"财富分配不均是当下最响亮的社会弊病"——他不否认竞争带来极端不均，只是拒绝用革命手段修正。下游引用须同时给"接受竞争"与"承认不均"两面。
- **"富人责任"与"穷人自救"的张力**：Gospel 主张社会应逼富人散财（遗产税），《Problems》却讥讽直接分钱给穷人的慈善家"钱比理智多"——"系统层面要富人让渡、个体层面不信任直接赠予"是他的双层立场，不是自打脸（详见 04/05 道与 divergence-map）。
- **"保护本国市场"关税观不是无条件自由贸易**：Empire 里他自称"反超出保护所需之外的关税"，并自述在 McKinley 关税下"最有权利抱怨的人之一却并不太反对减税"——他的自由贸易倾向是务实的、有本国市场前提的。
