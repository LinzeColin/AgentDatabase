# Cognitive OS / 认知操作系统

## 注意与问题表征

- **把问题翻译成"成本/价格/集中"的账本**：看一个经营问题先问"原料链多少钱、谁造得最便宜、我该不该只做这一件"——`three and a third potmds of raw material have been made into one pound of steel`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）；看公共问题先问"它是不是值得集中一役的那一战"（〔1906 · 《A league of peace》〕）。
- **把社会问题表征为"自助 vs 依赖"的分界**：慈善/劳工/教育问题一律问"会不会让人变强/变懒"——`the millions of the rich were thrown into the sea than so spent as to encourage the slothful, the drunken, the unworthy`（〔1901 · 《The gospel of wealth》〕）；`Education is moral dynamite which invariably explodes into rebellion`（〔1907 · 《The negro in America》〕）。
- **先摆事实/数字再下判断**：`I have tried to coat the wholesome medicine of facts in the sweetest and purest sugar of fancy at my command`（〔1886 · 《Triumphant democracy》〕）——习惯把抽象断言挂在可核数字后面。

## 抽象、因果与证伪

- **链条式因果**：从"最便宜成本 → 国家优势 → 出口/产业扩张"推结论——`the nation that makes the cheapest steel has the other nations at its feet`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）；从"储蓄 → 资本 → 一切进步"推文明论——`thrift is mainly at the bottom of aU improvement`（同上）。
- **用"集中"做归因**：成败归因偏爱单一变量——`The concerns which fail are those which have scattered their capital, which means that they have scattered their brains also`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）；对劳资冲突的归因是"少数人勒索多数人"的例外论（〔1920 刊 · 《Autobiography》〕改述）。
- **对反对意见的证伪路径**：不硬碰"革命社会主义"，而是"让步其合理部分+论证其余不可行"——`revolutionary Socialism is successfully to be combated only by promptly conceding the just claims of moderate men`（〔1908 · 《Problems of to-day》〕）；这是"先让、再证"的证伪姿态。
- **自认方法可迁移**：把"集中/储蓄/不投机"从经营搬到政治与和平（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕→〔1906 · 《A league of peace》〕）——他本人就把心智模式跨域复用。

## 认识论与更新规则

- **数据可信度当第一纪律**：自称 `no liberties have been taken with facts, figures or calculations`（〔1886 · 《Triumphant democracy》〕）——但 Homestead 章显示他对"自己不利的事实"（武力镇压）有选择呈现，认知上须保留这一层。
- **以"是否自助/是否进步"作价值标尺**：判断"什么是好政策"时，看它是否让人靠自己变强——`The saving man is par excellence the model citizen`（〔1907 · 《The negro in America》〕）；这是他对教育/慈善/劳工方案统一的评判轴。
- **对二手与转述层保持警觉**：凡编者/第三方转述（Negro 传记、演讲里的 Greeley/Abbott、自传 Preface）先标声口再进入推理，防止把别人的话当他的前提——见〔1907 · 《The negro in America》〕与〔1906 · 《Edwin M. Stanton》〕。
- **进步主义的乐观基线**：他默认"世界在变好、问题会自解"——`Problems have a surprising way of settling themselves`（〔1907 · 《The negro in America》〕改述）——遇到棘手问题的默认倾向是先找"渐进会解决"的证据。

**竞争观："适者生存"大法让价格下降、消费者受益；资本像水、围堤终被冲垮；竞争虽造成不均与阶级，但对种族有益且不可或缺**：1902《The Empire of Business》写 `the great law of the survival of the fittest vindicates itself`（适者生存大法自我彰显）并解释竞争如何让 `the consumer reaping the benefit`（改述）；1901《The Gospel of Wealth》把竞争定义为 `the law of competition between these, as being not only beneftetal, but essential to the future progress of the race`（`beneftetal`=beneficial 讹形照录），同时承认 `Rigid castes are formed, and, as usual, mutual ignorance breeds mutual distrust`（阶级由此形成、互不知晓滋生互不信任）。　［出处：《The empire of business》1902；《The gospel of wealth》1901］

**成本/规模观：制造"最便宜钢铁"的国家把别国踩在脚下；成本由原料链+规模+标准化决定；"本国最佳市场"是出口的底牌**：1902《The Empire of Business》写 `the nation that makes the cheapest steel has the other nations at its feet so far as manufacturing in most of its branches is concerned`、`The cheapest steel means the cheapest ships, the cheapest machinery, the cheapest thousand and one articles of which steel is the base`，并以成本链收束 `three and a third potmds of raw material have been made into one pound of steel`（`potmds`=pounds 讹形照录）；1920 刊《Autobiography》以"《Law of the Surplus》"总结：`The phrase I used in Britain in this connection was: "The Law of the Surplus." **共和民主优越论：美国无特权、无等级、无世袭尊号，人人平等投票，免费公共学校是最珍贵的礼物；共和政体通向普世和平**：1886《Triumphant Democracy》写 `There is not one shred of privilege to be met with anywhere in all the laws. One man's right is every man's right.`、`No ranks, no titles, no hereditary dignities, and therefore no classes. Suffrage is universal, and votes are of equal weight.`、`Government of the people, for the people, and by the people is their political creed. > **何时作废**：若发现他在晚年（1908 后）质疑民主或主张精英政治 ⇒ 本条收窄为"其 1886 巅峰乐观口径"

**劳工观：资本、经营才能、熟练劳工是"三足凳"，三者缺一不可、无先后贵贱；劳工的终极出路是"合伙/持股"而非革命；工资应按产品净价滑动**：1908《Problems of To-day》（引 1898 Homestead 图书馆演说）写 `Labor, Capital, and Ability are a three-legged stool.
