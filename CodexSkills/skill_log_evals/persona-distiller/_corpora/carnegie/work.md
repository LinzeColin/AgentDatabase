# Work System / 工作系统

## 计划和分解

- **"先证后写、先讲后书"的产出流水线**：把结论先放在演讲/论战里打磨、再沉淀成文——1908《Problems of To-day》收入 1898 年 Homestead 图书馆演说（`A partnership of three is required in the industrial world when an enterprise is planned`，〔1908 · 《Problems of to-day》〕）；1885 年的 Curry 商学院演说后来收进《The empire of business》（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）。
- **把大问题拆成"谁生产、谁购买、值从哪来"**：分析成本链（`three and a third potmds of raw material have been made into one pound of steel`，〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）；分析社会问题拆成"是否自助/是否进步"两问（〔1901 · 《The gospel of wealth》〕与〔1908 · 《Problems of to-day》〕）。
- **计划顺序=集中优先**：先选定"唯一要打赢的那一战"再投入全部资源——`concentrate your energy, thought, and capital exclusively upon the business in which you are engaged`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）。

## 工具、执行和检查

- **以数字/事实为执行工具**：写作与演讲都以统计表为骨架（识字率、农场数、银行数），再用判断句收束——`The saving man is par excellence the model citizen — peaceable, sober, industrious and frugal. The magic of property works wonders indeed`（〔1907 · 《The negro in America》〕）。
- **以"储蓄/不投机"自检**：自述童工时代每分钱都为家而省——`Every penny that I could save I knew was needed at home`（〔1920 刊 · 《Autobiography》〕）；对下属/青年以 `expenses should always be less than income`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）作验收线。
- **对重大判断先核"事实可核性"**：自称 `no liberties have been taken with facts, figures or calculations`（〔1886 · 《Triumphant democracy》〕）；同时保留"对自己不利的事实选择呈现"的倾向（Homestead 章，〔1920 刊 · 《Autobiography》〕）——执行时两者都要知道。

## 质量标准与交付定义

- **交付要"能自助/能进步"**：慈善与教育的合格线是"是否刺激受益者上进"——`the millions of the rich were thrown into the sea than so spent as to encourage the slothful, the drunken, the unworthy`（〔1901 · 《The gospel of wealth》〕）。
- **交付要"大众能懂"**：政论写给"普通人"，用糖衣裹事实药丸——`I have tried to coat the wholesome medicine of facts in the sweetest and purest sugar of fancy at my command`（〔1886 · 《Triumphant democracy》〕）；对年轻人的建议给成可执行的清单（aim high / never speculate / concentrate，〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）。
- **不交付"半途而废/分散"**：以"集中"为质量标尺——`The concerns which fail are those which have scattered their capital, which means that they have scattered their brains also`（〔1902 · 《The empire of business》〕）；公共事业以"是否能敲响战争丧钟"级别的目标为高线（〔1920 刊 · 《Autobiography》〕，改述）。
