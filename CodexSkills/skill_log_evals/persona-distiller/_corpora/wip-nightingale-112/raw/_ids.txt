# ── #112 Florence Nightingale (1820-05-12 – 1910-08-13, English nursing reformer & statistician; wrote in ENGLISH) corpus ledger ──
#
# columns (TAB separated, 9 of them):
#   short-id <TAB> URL <TAB> title <TAB> year <TAB> edition / where-it-sits <TAB> language <TAB> tier <TAB> flags <TAB> note
#   column 8 (flags) ALWAYS carries an attribution token — see the block below.
#
# ══ HOW THIS LEDGER WAS PRODUCED ══
#   Every URL below was read back out of the '# URL:' header of the .txt that was actually
#   downloaded, so no URL can appear here that was not fetched. Attempts that failed, and
#   material that was fetched and then discarded, are in _fetch.log — not here.
#
# ══ TIERS ══
#   P1  text she wrote or signed, in a printing of that text
#   P2  a degraded carrier of the same text: repeat scan, hand-keyed transcription,
#       translation, or posthumous reprint
#   S1  contemporary third party — Royal Commission colleagues, contemporary reviews and
#       obituaries, and volumes by OTHER authors that merely carry a preface of hers
#   S2  later study, biography or criticism
#
# ══ THE ONE TIERING DECISION THAT MOVES THE NUMBERS ══
#   Where she contributed only a preface or introduction to somebody else's book, the FILE is
#   tiered S1, not P1 — because the file is overwhelmingly not her. Her signed section is still
#   usable and its exact line range is in _BOUNDARIES.json. Tiering those eight files P1 would
#   have lifted the reported primary share by about seven points on a technicality. It was not
#   done. The single exception is infection-jervoise-1882, where her 'Remarks' is a named,
#   titled work printed complete and announced on the title leaf — not a preface.
#
# ══ FLAGS ══
#   HAS-OWN-STATS   the file contains numbers SHE computed and published, not merely numbers
#                   she quoted. Every one of these was confirmed by reading the table captions
#                   and rows out of the file itself. Two plausible candidates were deliberately
#                   NOT flagged — hospital-statistics-plans-1862 and surgical-operations-stats-1863
#                   — because they carry her method and her blank forms, not filled data.
#   FULL-PAGE-SCAN  the .txt also contains other people's text; see _BOUNDARIES.json
#   DUPLICATE-SCAN / TRANSCRIPTION / TRANSLATION / POSTHUMOUS / OCR-POOR   as named
#
#
# ══ ATTRIBUTION TOKEN — every row carries exactly one of these in column 8 ══
#   HER-OWN                signed by her, or printed as her text (letters, addresses, memoranda)
#   COMMISSION-COLLECTIVE  a collectively signed committee/department paper — NOT hers to speak in
#   CO-AUTHORED            produced jointly with a named collaborator
#   THIRD-PARTY            somebody else's work (all S1/S2), including volumes that merely carry
#                          a preface of hers — her section is then given in _BOUNDARIES.json
#   ATTRIBUTION-UNCLEAR    the document itself carries NO byline; do not quote in her first person
#                          without independent confirmation. Six rows. The verbatim catalogue
#                          creator field and the title-leaf evidence are written into each note.
#
#   WHY THIS MATTERS HERE: notes-british-army-1858 (853 pp., privately printed) DOES carry
#   'FLORENCE NIGHTINGALE.' on its title leaf and is hers to speak in. The Royal Commission
#   report of the same inquiry is a collectively signed public document and is NOT — even though
#   she drove the inquiry and her written evidence sits inside it at lines 83676-89650.
#   The two overlap heavily in content and are routinely co-cited; this column keeps them apart.
#
#   NO CO-AUTHORED ROWS. The brief anticipated Nightingale/William Farr collaborations. Farr was
#   checked across her statistical works and he appears as a CITED AUTHORITY whose disease
#   classification she adopted — 'arranged according to Dr. Farr's proposed new Classification'
#   (mortality-british-army-1858), 7 further mentions in notes-british-army-1858 — never as a
#   co-signatory. No item in this corpus bears a joint byline, so the token is unused rather than
#   guessed at.
#
