# 工作方式 — Charles Babbage

## 可复用的做法（有步骤且有判据）

- **先立尺度、再摆证据、最后下判断**：政策写作也带数学家的结构（《英格兰科学之衰败》序言先撇清与差分机的关系、再摆证据链，最后给改革纲领）。　［出处：《英格兰科学之衰败》1830，src-39cf3746b17b］
- **把论证外包给第三方"公证"**：请 Sir Harris Nicolas 从自己全部文件起草差分机案情陈述（第六章 Statement，1843），用他人执笔的方式把自己的遭遇叙述成可供第三方核验的文件——`The following statement was drawn up by the late Sir Harris Nicolas ... from papers and documents in my possession relating to the Difference Engine.`　［出处：《Passages from the Life of a Philosopher》1864，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **借体制权威背书**：对"机器能算数"这种外行难信的命题，主动向皇家学会会长等权威求助作证——`I was naturally anxious, in introducing it to the public, to appeal to the testimony of one so distinguished in the records of British science.`　［出处：《A Letter to Sir Humphry Davy》1822，src-6c2ab02ae2db］
- **信息公开优先于项目顺利**：明知分析机新原理可能推翻已开工的差分机，仍决定如实上报——`To withhold those new views from the Government ... would have been improper.`　［出处：《Passages from the Life of a Philosopher》1864，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **为单一目标献身**：1839 年为分析机辞去 Lucasian 讲席——`the demands of the Analytical Engine upon my attention had become so incessant and so exhausting ... I therefore sent in my resignation.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **把"能雇人做的绝不自做"立为原则**：`I at length laid it down as a principle — that, except in rare cases, I would never do anything myself if I could afford to hire another person who could do it for me.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **用机器消灭"人的环节"**：让机器自己取字模排版、同时取代排字工与计算员，并内置自校正机制防止误差累积——`any small error that may arise from accident or bad workmanship is corrected as soon as it is produced.`　［出处：《A Letter to Sir Humphry Davy》1822，src-6c2ab02ae2db］
