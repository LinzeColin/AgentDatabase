**他处理工厂、社会这类复杂对象时，先拆成尽可能多的问题，再每次只让一个变量变、其余保持不变（控制变量法）**：《1851 年博览会》引言把这一点写成一切探究的核心过程——`One of the most important processes in all inquiry, is to divide the subject to be considered into as many different questions as it will admit of, and then to examine each separately, or in other words to suppose that each single cause successively varies whilst all the others remain constant.`；同一思路用在工厂现场观察：预先印好带空白的问卷（skeleton forms），并警惕观察者改变被观察者——`if the observer stands with his watch in his hand before a person heading a pin, the workman will almost certainly increase his speed, and the estimate will be too large`。　［出处：《1851 年博览会》1851；《机械与制造业经济》1832］

> **何时作废**：若他在别的领域明显用整体印象而非分变量 ⇒ 收窄为"在其方法论写作中如此"
