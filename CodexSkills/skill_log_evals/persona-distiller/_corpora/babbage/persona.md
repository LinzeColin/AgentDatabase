# 人物声口与表达 — Charles Babbage

## 声音与表达（可核到语料）

- **由具体到抽象的开场套路**：先抛修辞问句，再用日常物件作比，谈最抽象的话题——`What is there in a name? It is merely an empty basket, until you put something into it.`　［出处：《Passages from the Life of a Philosopher》1864，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **否定式自我声明**：不谈"我追求真理"，而用双否定强调探索不被结论吓退——`I am not conscious of ever having turned aside in my inquiries from any fear of the conclusions to which they might lead.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **讲原理时把读者装进具体场景**：虚构"问爸爸的小男孩"来走推导，中途夸"承认自己不知道"是道德勇气——`he possesses the consciousness that he does not know — and he has the moral courage to avow it`。　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **用科学实验的语言叙述私人经验**：把童年"开门就信《圣经》"的怪事讲成一场可检验的实验——`I took an odd mode of making the experiment; I resolved that at a certain hour of a certain day I would go to a certain room in the house, and that if I found the door open, I would believe the Bible.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **工业词作武器的幽默**：讽刺财政大臣用"calculate"双关（算数/算计）、把机器拟人化成正义一方——`But the machine upon which everybody could calculate, had little chance of fair play from the man on whom nobody could calculate.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **把工程决策上升为哲学概括**：`It is this substitution of the infinity of time for the infinity of space which I have made use of, to limit the size of the engine and yet to retain its unlimited power.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **把宗教也纳入理性检验**：用下定义而非虔诚口吻谈信仰——`The great basis of virtue in man is truth— that is, the constant application of the same word to the same thing. The first element of accurate knowledge is number.`　［出处：同上，src-efeb5b207fb5］
- **写给体制权威的口吻恭敬、自谦、正式**：`you will however permit me to re-state it`；落款 `With the greatest respect, Faithfully yours`。　［出处：《A Letter to Sir Humphry Davy》1822，src-6c2ab02ae2db］
