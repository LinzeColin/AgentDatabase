## 一、1875 年：他当众撤掉了自己用了三千次的试法

`src-e7403b89a239`：

> Sir  J.  Whitwobth  mentioned  that  his  first  experiments  on  the
> use  of  fluid  compressed  steel  for  guns  had  been  made  by  subjecting
> the  material  to  a  gunpowder  strain,  using  small  tubes  like  the
> specimens  shown  (see  Figs.  3  to  8,  Plate  47),  and  measuring  the
> strength  of  the  metal  by  the  number  of  grains  of  gunpowder
> required  to  burst  the  tubes ;  as  many  as  3000  of  these  tubes  had
> been  burst  experimentally  in  that  way.  Now  however  he  had
> entirely  given  up  that  mode  of  testing,  and  had  adopted  in  preference
> the  more  definite  test  of  tensile  strength  and  ductility

★★ 这是本批语料里最有分量的一条：**三千次试验之后，他自己把那套试法废掉了**，
理由只有一个词——`more definite`。
与他在 1858 年那批论文里的做派完全一致：**判据要能被别人重复，不能只在他手里成立。**

## 二、同一场里，他不许别人把他的结果读窄

> Sir  J.  Whitwobth  said  he  had  not  made  any  trial  of  applying
> pressure  to  cast  iron  in  a  fluid  state.  It  should  be  distinctly
> understood  that  the  value  of  the  process  of  compressing  fluid  steel
> under  the  heavy  pressure  employed  was  not  in  proportion  to  the
> ductility  alone  of  the  metal  so  produced,  but  consisted  in  obtaining
> high  tensile  strength  in  combination  with  ductility.

★ 注意开头那半句：**没做过的就说没做过**（`had not made any trial`），
然后才纠正对结果的误读。

## 三、被问到机理时，他把「知道的」与「相信的」分开

> Sir  J.  Whitworth  remarked  that,  when  the  pressure  of  6  tons
> per  sq.  in.  was  applied  to  the  fluid  metal  in  the  mould,  a  column  of
> flaid  metal  of  8  ft.  height  was  reduced  1  ft.  in  less  than  five  minutes.
> No  doubt  there  was  a  great  deal  of  gas  expelled  during  the
> compression,  but  he  believed  nine-tenths  of  it  was  common  air ;

**量出来的给数（8 ft 压掉 1 ft、五分钟内），推测的说 `he believed`。**

## 四、他答问的方式是**把东西端出来**

> Sir  J.  Whitworth  exhibited  the  iron  plate  referred  to  in  the
> paper,  and  the  three  projectiles — flat-headed,  ronnd-headed,  and
> pointed — which  had  been  fired  against  it  obliquely  through  80  in.  of
> water

与 1856 年那次把比长机带到会场（见 `01-writings.md` 第二节）是同一个动作。

## 五、1847–49 年 IMechE：最早的两条，一条薄一条实

`src-7ddafdc7c7bc`：

> Mr. Whitworth seconded the motion, and it was passed.

★ **这一条是程序性的，不承载任何内容**——写下来是为了不把 2 条说成 2 条实质发言。

> Mr. Whitworth said it was very simple, it was a worm
> working in two wheels, and counted up as high as 30,000.

被问到他那台转数计时的回答：**先说「很简单」，再给结构与量程。**
