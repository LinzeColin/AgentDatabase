## Source-linked observations

- Hadfield 自述自 1888 年在土木工程师学会宣读锰钢论文以来，已向国内外科学和技术学会提交了 139 篇论文，体现了他作为多产科学作者的身份。原文："Since reading my first paper on Manganese Steel before the Institution of Civil Engineers, in 1888, I have presented 139 papers to Scientific and Technical Societies at home and abroad."（1923，src-dc603260fff5）

- Hadfield 明确承认 Percy 的经典著作和 Faraday 的早期合金实验是激励他从事铁合金研究的两大智识来源，将 Percy 与 Faraday 并列为"两位伟人"，并将自己置于这一谱系之中。原文："As Faraday's records show, his master mind was also attracted in this same direction. The early studies of these two great men have happily matured to the enormous benefit of the world at large. I consider it a matter of no little satisfaction and encouragement to have worked in this same direction."（1923，src-dc603260fff5）

- Hadfield 在 1925 年的著作中系统梳理了从古代到近代的冶金史脉络，将 Roger Bacon、Dud Dudley、Robert Boyle、Priestley、Faraday、Bessemer 等人纳入同一叙事框架，明确列出 Faraday 在 1842 年即开展了铁与其他元素的合金实验。原文："Faraday himself, who carried out important experiments of great promise on alloys of iron with other elements, it was not until the decades 1870 to 1890 that there occurred the great advances and tremendous burst of activity in metallurgical science."（1925，src-f94b66599392）

- Hadfield 在 1925 年著作中对 Faraday 有专门传记段落，记述其 1821 年即从事钢合金研究，并称其为"世界上最伟大的实验哲学家"。原文："In 1820 Faraday published his discovery of two new compounds — chlorine, carbon and iodine; carbon and hydrogen — and in 1821 he was working on alloys of steel." 以及 "He was probably the greatest experimental philosopher the world has ever seen."（1925，src-f94b66599392）

- Hadfield 在 1922 年的冶金化学家演讲中，强调化学分析必须绝对精确、不容"做假账"，并自述其个人实验记录可追溯至 1882 年且至今无误。原文："There can be no 'cooking' of figures; accuracy must reign supreme. During my life I suppose that hundreds of thousands of analytical results have come before me and I am delighted to say that I never remember a dishonest one being presented."（1922，src-9d9f22cec4f5）

- Hadfield 在 1922 年演讲中回忆了自己的化学启蒙经历——在 Sheffield Collegiate School 每周六上午听 William Baker 的化学课，并于 1875 年获得"Natural Science"双奖。原文："In 1875, to my great delight, after having struggled hard, I obtained both the prizes offered in 'Natural Science,' as it was termed, that is, one for Practical Chemistry and the other for the best paper on the Lectures of that year."（1922，src-9d9f22cec4f5）

- Hadfield 在 1925 年著作中对锰钢发明的叙事极为详细，记录了 1882 年 9 月 7 日实验笔记本中的原始条目，其中他作为 22 岁青年预言该实验"可能在某种程度上彻底革新冶金界对铁合金的看法"。原文："The experiments have led to some very curious, perhaps most momentous, results that may to some extent entirely revolutionise metallurgical opinions as regards alloys of iron and steel."（1925，src-f94b66599392）

- Hadfield 在 1925 年著作中援引多位世界顶级冶金学家的评价来确立锰钢发明的历史地位，包括 Osmond 将其与碳钢淬火发现相提并论，以及 Pourcel 将其与 Bessemer、Mushet、Martin、Siemens、Gilchrist 的贡献并列。原文："the discovery and invention of manganese steel was not only the discovery of a new alloy, curious, of great scientific value and yet useful, but in the history of the metallurgy of iron it ranked as a discovery equal in importance to that of the effect of quenching carbon steel."（1925，src-f94b66599392）

- Hadfield 在 1922 年演讲中特别展示了 Sir Henry Bessemer 1897 年写给他的亲笔信及其四代家族肖像，体现了他对冶金传承的重视。原文："Plate 4 shows a personal letter which I received from Sir Henry under date January 11th, 1897."（1922，src-9d9f22cec4f5）
