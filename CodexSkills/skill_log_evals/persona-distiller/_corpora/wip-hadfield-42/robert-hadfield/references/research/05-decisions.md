## Source-linked observations

- Hadfield 以自身企业的实际数据为缩短工时立场提供经验证据：他在 Hadfield's Steel Foundry Co. 将日工时从 9.5 小时减至 9 小时（周工时从 54 减至 51 小时），涉及 450-500 名工人，结论是"减少工时实际上并未增加生产成本"，产量未降而工人守时率大幅提升。原文："practically the reduced hours have not added to the cost of production. The management on its side has perfected better methods, and the workers on their side have shown more intelligent interest in carrying out the work to be done, the result being that as much work has been done as in the former long hours."（1892，src-63800b6e6147）

- Hadfield 将缩短工时的核心动力归结为"工人士气与信任"而非单纯的经济算计，认为人对信任会做出积极回应，并将此与企业管理的实际改善并列。原文："one of the chief factors has been the better tone and morale amongst the men. It is the old tale, that human nature is not irresponsive to more trust and confidence being placed in it."（1892，src-63800b6e6147）

- Hadfield 以具体数据说明缩短工时后守时率的显著改善：1891 年 1 月约 500 名工人中有 72 人平均迟到半小时、22 人 9 点才开始工作；1892 年 1 月全部工人中仅日平均 19 人未准时，准时率从约 80% 升至 96%。原文："In January 1892, the whole of the men, except a daily average of nineteen, were in at work punctually at the starting-time — 6.30 a.m."（1892，src-63800b6e6147）

- Hadfield 在磁力-机械分析实验中做出关键技术决策：选取同一化学成分（C 1.25%, Si 0.43%, Mn 12.20%）但不同热处理的两根锰钢棒（No. 2 非磁 vs No. 5 磁性），通过对比 Joule 效应与 Villari 效应来关联磁性与机械性能，揭示热处理而非化学成分是决定锰钢磁性的关键变量。原文："one differing from the other only in heat treatment." 以及 "Rods Nos. 2, 3 and 4, were non-magnetic; hand magnet just pulled at 1/16 inch. Rods 1, 5 and 6 were magnetic; hand magnet pulled well at 3/8 inch."（1920，src-b558bfde4e73）

- Hadfield 通过磨削实验做出重要判断：锰钢棒的微弱磁性来源于表面氧化层（"skin trouble"）而非材料本体，从而排除了化学成分偏差的可能性。原文："The slight magnetic qualities noticed in bar No. 2 in fig. 3 were therefore entirely due to the condition of the surface material." 以及 "manganese steel is very readily oxidised, and consequently bars of it, unless carefully ground, are liable to what is termed 'skin' trouble, that is, slight magnetic susceptibility."（1920，src-b558bfde4e73）

- Hadfield 提出磁力-机械分析方法具有"无损检测"潜力，并呼吁大型钢铁企业与研究实验室合作系统研究这一领域，将 X 射线方法和磁力方法列为当时仅有的两种无损检测手段。原文："X-ray methods, and the methods indicated above, seem to be about all the means we have at present to test the mechanical properties of a substance without injuring the sample tested. There are some advantages in the magnetic method."（1920，src-b558bfde4e73）

- Hadfield 对全球钢铁腐蚀损失做出宏观量化判断：估算每年因腐蚀损失超过 4000 万吨钢铁，总经济损失超过 5 亿英镑，并据此提出应鼓励发展耐腐蚀合金钢。原文："Careful estimates appear to show that there is a present annual loss of over 40 million tons of iron and steel under corrosion and consequent removal of material rendered unserviceable. Taking into account the cost of protecting the material, the author estimates that the aggregate annual loss due to the effects of corrosion is probably well over 500 million pounds sterling."（1922，src-db7d79db2c67）

- Hadfield 设计并实施了对含铜钢与普通钢耐腐蚀性的系统对比实验，结论是即使仅含 0.25% 铜（1/400）的钢在硫酸中的耐腐蚀性也远超普通钢，腐蚀速率比为 9:1。原文："the author's experiments, therefore, show that even so small an amount of copper in mild steel as 0.25 per cent., or only 1 part in 400, can exert a very marked inhibiting influence against corrosion by sulphuric acid."（1922，src-db7d79db2c67）

- Hadfield 在结论中保持审慎态度，指出铜的耐腐蚀效益仅在裸金属暴露于含硫大气条件下成立，对于涂层金属尚无证据表明铜含量有益。原文："a small copper-content, say 0.16 to 0.25 per cent., is beneficial, provided the condition is that of bare metal exposed to atmospheric corrosion, especially in a sulphurous atmosphere. But no recorded tests have yet shown that coated metal would be benefited by a copper-content."（1922，src-db7d79db2c67）
