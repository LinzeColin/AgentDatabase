## Source-linked observations

- Hadfield 在 1877 年即以作者身份出版了一部关于美国冶金工业家兼政治家 Elijah Ward 的传记，由纽约 G. W. Carleton & Co. 和伦敦 S. Low, Son & Co. 同时出版，署名"Robert Hadfield"，此时 Hadfield 仅约 19 岁，早于其锰钢发明（1882）五年，是其最早的可考出版行为。原文题名页："ELIJAH WARD [OF NEW YORK.] A BIOGRAPHICAL SKETCH BY ROBERT HADFIELD. A NEW EDITION. NEW YORK: G. W. CARLETON & CO., PUBLISHERS. LONDON: S. LOW, SON & CO. M.DCCC.LXXVII."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）

- 该传记的写作时间可追溯至 1874 年 12 月（版权登记年份），当时 Hadfield 约 16-17 岁，但内容已显示出对商业、法律和政治事务的成熟理解，序言将 Ward 的"公众服务"定位为值得记录的对象。原文："The honorable career of Elijah Ward, his earnest support of the Government during the late civil war, and his sound views on leading financial, commercial and other questions, would seem to render a sketch of his public services not inappropriate."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）

- Hadfield 在 Ward 传记中展现了将钢铁贸易置于宏观商业与政策框架中分析的视角，引用官方统计数据论述美国对加拿大的钢铁出口规模，反映出他早期即以"工业家-贸易政策观察者"的身份思考冶金工业的全球格局。原文："The exports of our cotton manufactures to Canada in the same year amounted to $1,591,844, and those of iron and steel to no less than $0,833,649."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）

- Hadfield 在 Ward 传记中记录了 Ward 的出生地（Sing Sing, Westchester County）和约 56 岁的年龄，以及其家族从英国经新英格兰迁至纽约的谱系，体现了 Hadfield 对盎格鲁-撒克逊工业家谱系和跨大西洋联系的持续关注。原文："Elijah Ward was born at Sing Sing, Westchester County, and is about fifty-six years of age. He is the son of Israel Ward, now deceased."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）

- Hadfield 在 Ward 传记中引用了 Gladstone 关于工人自我管理的论述，显示他早在 1877 年（15 年后即出版缩短工时论著）就已在思考劳动者自主性与工作时间的关系问题。原文 Gladstone 引言："the mainspring of a working-man's life has been placed, for the most part, as it has with a young child; as with a slave… For very large numbers of working-men it has now gradually shifted to a point lying within his base."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）

- Hadfield 在 Ward 传记中记录了 Ward 从 1833 年到纽约谋生、1840 年入法律事务所学习、1843 年取得律师资格、1845 年获军法法官任命的完整职业轨迹，这种对"从学徒到专家"生涯阶段的记录方式，与 Hadfield 后来在自述中反复使用的人生阶段叙事高度一致。原文："In February, 1840, he entered, as a student, the law office of the Honorable William W. Campbell. In May, 1843, having completed the prescribed term of legal study, he was admitted to practice in the Supreme Court and Court of Chancery."（1877，src-abb82d8164df）
