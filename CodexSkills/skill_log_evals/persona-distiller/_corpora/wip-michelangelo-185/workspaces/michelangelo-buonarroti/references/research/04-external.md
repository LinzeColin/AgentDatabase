## Source-linked observations

### O-1 · 19 世纪的英语传记把他放进**佛罗伦萨政治史**里讲

`src-1a70dbd068ca`（1857，《The Life of Michael Angelo Buonarroti》）：

> In the year 1497, a conspiracy for the restoration of the Medici was discovered and frustrated,
> and five citizens of high consideration implicated in it were, after trial by a commission
> specially appointed for…

### O-2 · 摹本册的编者从素描里读出的是「**准备工作有多用心**」

`src-2141d0967120`（1862，Fisher 摹本册的目录说明）：

> This Study is most interesting, as **proving the care of this illustrious Master
> in preparing for his Pictures**.

同册另一条（`src-bde8403de0f8`，1852）著录《最后的审判》里的一个人物：

> One of the Figures in the celebrated Fresco of the Last Judgment in the Vatican —
> an admirable Study: highly finished in black chalk, and touched with surprising truth
> as to anatomical knowledge.

★ **这两条是 Fisher 的话，不是他的**。本道的用途正是「别人怎么看他」，
但写进产物时必须标明说话人是编者。

## ★ 一件要写下来的

`src-5a9c6f4bef17`（Lester 1845）里有大量第一人称段落，**说话的不是米开朗基罗**——
那是书中写到的 19 世纪美国雕塑家（有一段说「一尊我接了 **300 美元**的胸像」）。
**这一份已从一手改判为二手**，但它仍在语料里，取引文时**必须逐条判说话人**。
