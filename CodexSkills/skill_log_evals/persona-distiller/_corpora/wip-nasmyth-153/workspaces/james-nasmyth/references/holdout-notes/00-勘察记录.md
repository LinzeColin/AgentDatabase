### 2.1 ★ 自传的体例——它算不算一手？

**书目事实（已核，不照抄任务描述）：**
- 首版：*James Nasmyth, Engineer: An Autobiography*, edited by Samuel Smiles. London: John Murray, 1883, xvii+456 pp.
  archive.org 扫描件 https://archive.org/details/jamesnasmythengi00nasmiala （著录：John Murray, London, 1883）
  其他 1883 扫描件：https://archive.org/details/jamesnasmytheng01smilgoog 、https://archive.org/details/jamesnasmythengi0000nasm
- **PD 依据：出版年 1883 < 1931。**
- Project Gutenberg #476，著录 author `Nasmyth, James, 1808-1890`，editor `Smiles, Samuel, 1812-1904`。
  https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/476 ；正文 https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/476/pg476.txt
  ★ Gutenberg 文本自述取自 **1897 年 John Murray「popular edition」**，且其序言落款是 **"S.S. LONDON, October 1885"**。
  ★ 而 Grace's Guide 转录的 1883 版序言，同一篇文字落款是 **"S. S. LONDON, January 1883"**。
  https://www.gracesguide.co.uk/James_Nasmyth_by_James_Nasmyth:_Preface
  → **同一篇序言在不同版次上落款不同**；引用时必须连版次一起写，否则会引出一个不存在的日期。

**体例（依 Smiles 本人的序言，逐字取自 Grace's Guide 1883 版序言页）：**
- "Mr. Nasmyth has been prevailed upon by some of his friends … to note down the reminiscences of his life … **He has accordingly spent some of his well-earned leisure during the last two years in writing out his numerous recollections.**"
- "Mr. Nasmyth has furnished me with abundant notes of his busy life, and he has requested me, in preparing them for publication, to '**make use of the pruning-knife**.'"
- "I must add that **everything has been submitted to his correction and received his final approval**."

**据此的判读（本轮只陈述证据，不裁定）：**
- **正文 21 章是 Nasmyth 亲笔写的回忆稿，Smiles 的动作是删削与编排，且成稿经 Nasmyth 逐处校订认可。** 章目见
  https://www.gracesguide.co.uk/James_Nasmyth_by_James_Nasmyth （I 我的祖先 … XXI Active Leisure）。
  第 1 章开头是第一人称叙事："Our history begins before we are born…"
- **序言（Preface）是 Smiles 写的第三人称文字，不是 Nasmyth 的话**——序言里凡带引号的句子才是 Nasmyth 的原话（如 1863-04 的回信、"make use of the pruning-knife"、"Drawing is the Education of an Eye"、他给工程学下的定义 "common sense applied to the use of materials"）。
- 两个附录里，**"Chronological List of Mechanical Inventions and Technical Contrivances"（43 条，1825–1862）** 与 **"Sun-Ray: Origin of the Pyramids, and the Cuneiform Character"** 都被序言明确归给 Nasmyth 本人。
  https://www.gracesguide.co.uk/James_Nasmyth_by_James_Nasmyth:_Chronological_List
- ⚠️ 未做的核对：我**没有**逐章比对 1883 版与 1897 版正文差异，也**没有**找到 Nasmyth 交给 Smiles 的原始手稿（无法量化 Smiles 到底删改了多少）。见最后一节。
