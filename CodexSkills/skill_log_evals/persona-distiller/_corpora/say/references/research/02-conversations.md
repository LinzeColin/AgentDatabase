## Source-linked observations

本道只有 1 份：1821 年 Richter 英译的《Letters to Mr. Malthus》（法文原版 1820）。全卷由 6 封致马尔萨斯的信组成，是对马尔萨斯《政治经济学原理》"一般过剩"论的直接论战——这是本语料库里**唯一一份以"对话/往复反驳"为形态的一手文本**。注意卷首《Preface》署名 `J. R.`（即译者 John Richter），是译者对 Say 的定位，不是 Say 自己的话。

### ① 译者 Preface（二手视角）：把 Say 定位为"把政治经济学提到精密科学之列的第一人"

> Mr. Say was the first writer who attempted to raise Political Economy to the rank of the exact sciences
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（译者 J.R. 的定位，非 Say 亲述；同段他还概括 Say 的主张"markets for commodities are created by and depend upon the production of commodities which can only be purchased by producers, or their representatives — not by unproductive consumers"，改述。）

### ② 第一封信：礼貌的开场——"你的权威若被采纳会使科学倒退"

> I find in your doctrines some fundamental principles which, if admitted under the imposing sanction of your authority, would occasion a retrograde movement in a science of which your extensive information and great talents are so well calculated to assist the progress.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（`J regret`=I regret 的 OCR 形；"我并非来挑剔你书中亮点，也不愿纠缠无益之争，但你的几条基本原理若因你的权威被接纳，会让这门科学倒退"——先用谦辞垫场再直陈分歧，是 Say 书信论战的开场范式。）

### ③ 第一封信：把马尔萨斯的问题翻译成三个提问

> What is the cause of the general glut of all the markets in the world, to which merchandize is incessantly carried to be sold at a loss? What is the reason that in the interior of every state... there exists universally a difficulty of finding lucrative employments? And when the cause of this chronic disease is found, by what means is it to be remedied?
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（OCR 折行已按版面处理；`lucrative employments`=有利可图的就业，`chronic disease`=慢性病——他把"市场普遍滞销"称作一种"慢性病"。）

### ④ 第一封信：销售法则的自我陈述（他说"我的结论让你吓了一跳"）

> As each of us can only purchase the productions of others with his own productions — as the value we can buy is equal to the value we can produce, the more men can produce, the more they will purchase.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

> if certain goods remain unsold, it is because other goods are not produced; and that it is production alone which opens markets to produce.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（引号内是 Say 复述自己在《概论》里的原话，`which opens markets to produce` 疑为 `produce` 与 `products` 的排印混，OCR 照录不补。）

> in short, that the superabundance of goods of one description arises from the deficiency of goods of another description.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（`IT ore`=more 的 OCR 形，见"could provide IT ore of them"，改述时注明。）

### ⑤ 第一封信：引 Sismondi 的反对意见来一起反驳（借力打力）

> "Europe," says that ingenious author, "has in every part arrived at the point of possessing industry and manufacturing power superior to its wants."
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（Say 主动引 Sismondi"欧洲工业能力已超过其需要"的原文，说"我不想剥夺你任何优势、好一起答复你和 Sismondi"——`I am happy to quote here his strongest expressions, that I may not deprive you of any of your advantages`，改述。论战技巧：把对手与第三者的最强表述摆出来再逐一拆。）

### ⑥ 第二封信：核心命题复述——"生产为生产开出市场"

> I THINK I have proved in ray first letter that productions can only be purchased with productions: I do not therefore yet see any reason to abandon the doctrine, that it is production which opens a market to production.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（OCR 讹形照录：`ray`=my。）

### ⑦ 第二封信：用"100 袋谷物/100 匹布"的可感例证替对手立论再驳

> Mr. Malthus will readily allow that 100 sacks of corn will buy 100 pieces of stuff... but if the same society should happen to produce 200 sacks of corn and 200 pieces of stuff... he will maintain that a part of each of them will no longer find purchasers.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（Say 先把马尔萨斯的反对翻译成具体数字例，声明"我首先得证明：无论产量多大、价格压得多低，一种产品的产量总足以让生产者买到另一种产品的产量"——改述衔接。）

> how the superabundance of productions creates a demand for it, for the purpose of consumption.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（"生产的过剩如何为消费创造需求"——他正面论证"生产越多→互相买得起→消费越多"。）

### ⑧ 第二封信：对"自由生产假设"的手法让步

> the hypothesis of unrestricted production is more favourable to your cause, because it is much more difficult to dispose of unlimited ... produce than of that which is restricted
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（句中夹页眉"26 LETTER II."，以省略号跳过。）

（连论战假设都主动让到对对方最有利的极端——"即便在最不利的假设下我也要证明我的结论"。）

### ⑨ 第二封信：储蓄与资本积累观——反驳"储蓄减少需求"

> capital can never augment with a rapidity capable of producing sudden revolutions in industrious pursuits.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

> If they save, I say, that they promote industry and production, and that this increase of production is distributed in the most advantageous manner to the public.
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

> Now what is this but an increase of prosperity?
<!-- src-0d7cfabc38d1 -->

（`ray`=my、`enn ployment`=employment 的 OCR 形，见"a greater number of the industrious find enn ployment"，改述。他对马尔萨斯说：资本每年增加一点、下年多雇一点劳动、这些劳动者再消费掉他们的工资——"这难道不是繁荣的增长吗？"）

⇒ 声口：书信论战体的三个特征——①谦辞垫场再陈分歧（②）；②替对方把反对意见立到最强（⑦⑧）；③以可感的数量例与"繁荣增长"的口语化反问收束（⑨）。这与他著作里的书面论证是同一套逻辑、但节奏更像对话。

## Candidate Claims

- C-C1（fact）：与马尔萨斯论战的供给过剩立场——"如果某些商品卖不掉，是因为别的商品没被生产出来；是生产为生产开出市场"（src-0d7cfabc38d1 `if certain goods remain unsold, it is because other goods are not produced`，与 writings 道 C-W1 同簇）。
- C-C2（mental-model）："产品只能换产品"——购买力来自已生产的价值（src-0d7cfabc38d1 `productions can only be purchased with productions`；src-1695e00c1f47 需求=另一种产品的供给）。
- C-C3（fact）：一般过剩不可能、只可能有局部过剩/相对滞销（src-0d7cfabc38d1 `the superabundance of goods of one description arises from the deficiency of goods of another description`）。
- C-C4（heuristic）：论战手法——先立强对手再驳、让假设到对对方最有利（src-0d7cfabc38d1 ⑥⑦⑧，`the hypothesis of unrestricted production is more favourable to your cause`）。
- C-C5（mental-model）：储蓄促进生产与繁荣、反对"挥霍维持需求"（src-0d7cfabc38d1 ⑨，`If they save, I say, that they promote industry and production`）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **Preface 层 ≠ Say 层**：`J. R.` 的 Preface（"把政治经济学提到精密科学之列的第一人"）是译者的尊崇定位，不是 Say 的自评；同卷"unproductive consumers are mere representatives of others"等概括也是译者对 Say 教义的转述，引用时须标声口。
- **Say 自己承认的对手理据**：他明确复述 Sismondi"欧洲工业能力超过需要"与 Malthus"可能全面过剩"的实例（1818 开普敦、加尔各答仓库堆满欧洲货），说明他对"市场饱和的现实"不是无知，而是用"他物生产不足"来解释——这条"承认现象、否认结论"的张力是理解其立场的钥匙。
- 与 writings 道③（Prinsep 脚注）一致：Say 在论战中把"不生产者（神父/公职/医生）"也算进需求链（`unproductive consumers are mere representatives of others`，Preface 概括；writings 道④神父例），这一扩展是后来"无形产品"学说的种子。
