# 事实 · John Marshall（1755–1835）

## 他判过的案子——判词与要旨

- **Marbury v. Madison**：他写下 `It is emphatically the province and duty of the judicial department to say what the law is.`（`src-8c46f27be355` @69739）——法院有权说什么是法。★ 该句前一句他先把对方读法称作 `an absurdity too gross to be insisted on`，**却仍写「不过还是要更认真地考虑它」**。 <!-- claim:clm-775fcc3110c7 -->
  - **反证条件**：若该段被证为编者转述，本条须撤
- **Marbury v. Madison** 里他给成文宪法的理由是**书写本身的目的**：`To what purpose are powers limited and to what purpose is that limitation committed to writing, if these limits may at any time be passed by those intended to be restrained ?`（`src-8c46f27be355` @67529） <!-- claim:clm-c71af2bf6e38 -->
  - **反证条件**：若属编者转述须撤
- **McCulloch v. Maryland** 里他把「这是宪法」当成解释的前提：`In considering this question, then, we must never forget that it is a Constitution we are expounding.`（`src-8c46f27be355` @483554） <!-- claim:clm-24d32a113c5d -->
  - **反证条件**：若同案另有相反段落须并列
- **McCulloch v. Maryland**：联邦政府的授权直接来自人民——`The government proceeds directly from the people`（`src-8c46f27be355` @477007）。前文他先承认反方最强的一点：各州人民**是在各自州内**行动的（`when they act, they act in their States`）。 <!-- claim:clm-d708527610db -->
  - **反证条件**：若属辩护词须撤
- **McCulloch v. Maryland** 的手段合宪判准是四条并列条件：`Let the end be legitimate, let it be within the scope of the Constitution, and all means which are appropriate, which are plainly adapted to that end, which are not prohibited, but consist with the letter and spirit of the Constitution, are constitutional.`（`src-73eaf91e64a8` @511536） <!-- claim:clm-772111a5c435 -->
  - **反证条件**：若 1903 版正文也有该段，版本说明须更正
- **McCulloch v. Maryland**：`That the power to tax involves the power to destroy ;`（`src-8c46f27be355` @523989）——原文继续 `that the power to destroy may defeat and render useless the power to create`。 <!-- claim:clm-f655afb48156 -->
  - **反证条件**：若被标为引述他人须改写
- **Gibbons v. Ogden**：`Commerce, undoubtedly, is traffic, but it is something more; it is intercourse.`（`src-8c46f27be355` @789020）他拒绝把 commerce 限成买卖，理由是那样等于 `restrict a general term, applicable to many objects, to one of its significations`。 <!-- claim:clm-4e30de63c8f4 -->
  - **反证条件**：若属辩护词须撤

## 案件要旨（取自编本的要旨行，不是判词本身）

- **Fletcher v. Peck** 的要旨（编本标题页逐字）：`The Constitution of the United States forbids a State from impairing` ——契约条款禁止州破坏既成契约。 <!-- claim:clm-be1d36cd7eb5 -->
  - **反证条件**：若要旨行为编者概括而非判词，须标明
- **Cohens v. State of Virginia** 的要旨：`Constitutional supremacy of the Supreme Court over` ——最高法院对州法院判决的复审权。 <!-- claim:clm-58547b7f7548 -->
  - **反证条件**：同上
- **Osborn v. Bank of United States** 的要旨：`State laws taxing the franchises or functions` ——州法对合众国银行特许与职能征税的效力。 <!-- claim:clm-1be5811bc1ae -->
  - **反证条件**：同上
- **Brown v. Maryland** 的要旨：`Paramount power of Congress to regulate commerce — The States cannot` ——国会贸易管制权优先于州。 <!-- claim:clm-7aaf2296c588 -->
  - **反证条件**：同上
- **Worcester v. Georgia** 的要旨：`The National authority over Indian tribes as against the States is s[upreme]` ——对印第安部落的权限属联邦而非州。 <!-- claim:clm-04321aa8db3c -->
  - **反证条件**：同上
- **Ogden v. Saunders** 的要旨：`Constitutional validity of State insolvent and bankrupt laws` ——州破产法的合宪性。★ 该案是他任内**少数发表异议**的宪法案件之一，本轮语料只取到要旨行，**未核他的意见立场**。 <!-- claim:clm-2cdb4b0ec791 -->
  - **反证条件**：读完该案他的意见后，本条须补立场
- **Sturges v. Crowninshield** 在该编本里被称为 `has always been ranked as among the great judgments of Chief Ju[stice Marshall]`——编者对该判决的评价。★ 这是**别人对他的评价**，不是他的话。 <!-- claim:clm-c9a7dc5d9f9f -->
  - **反证条件**：若该句实为他人引述须改写
- **Craig v. Missouri** 在四年后的 `Byrne v.` 一案中被复审并重申（`Craig v. Missouri was reviewed and reaffirmed four years later in Byrne v`）。 <!-- claim:clm-acb374639ce8 -->
  - **反证条件**：若该复审关系记错须撤

## 他写的东西与他的私信

- **他 1804–1807 年写的《The Life of George Washington》是他篇幅最大的著作**，而那本书的第一人称大多是**华盛顿的**：书中转录华盛顿书信时带引导语，例如 `he writes in a letter of the 17th, to general Arnold`（`src-2e4088bec901` 前文）、`said he in a letter to … governor Clinton`。⇒ 从该书取「马歇尔说过的话」默认是错的。 <!-- claim:clm-1ada6e16e012 -->
  - **反证条件**：若找到他以自己名义发议论的整段须并列
- **私信里他把挨骂降级为自己不够格**：`I must bear that newspaper scurrility which I had hoped to escape, and which is generally reserved for more important personages than myself.`（`src-23baaf7130ab` @249905） <!-- claim:clm-e466ce161fcf -->
  - **反证条件**：若该信为他人代笔须撤
- **1833 年 7 月 22 日于 Richmond**，他回信谈共济会，开头逐字为 `My Dear Sir, I have received your favor of the 16th, enclosing a printed copy of your letter respecting Masonry to Mr.`（`src-21deee17627c` @1756）。★ 本条只断言该信存在并被 1840 年编本收录，**不断言其立场**。 <!-- claim:clm-2e5d5c4b01c1 -->
  - **反证条件**：若该编本为伪托须撤
- **他称赞一本书时挑的是对本方最不利的一段**：`But I have been still more touched with your notice of the red man than of the white.`（`src-a0776cc4e7ae` @251071），后文接 `The conduct of our forefathers in expelling the original occupants of the soil grew`。⚠ 该行前紧跟页眉 `DOCTRINES OF JOHN MARSHALL 125`，逐字引用不能从句首整段取。 <!-- claim:clm-fe6eaaba99e7 -->
  - **反证条件**：若该信为他人所作须撤
