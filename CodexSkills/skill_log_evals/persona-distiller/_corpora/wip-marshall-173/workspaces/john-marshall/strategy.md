# 策略 · John Marshall

## 他在学理上的两个支点

- **McCulloch v. Maryland**：联邦政府的授权直接来自人民——`The government proceeds directly from the people`（`src-8c46f27be355` @477007）。前文他先承认反方最强的一点：各州人民**是在各自州内**行动的（`when they act, they act in their States`）。 <!-- claim:clm-d708527610db -->
  - **反证条件**：若属辩护词须撤
- **Gibbons v. Ogden**：`Commerce, undoubtedly, is traffic, but it is something more; it is intercourse.`（`src-8c46f27be355` @789020）他拒绝把 commerce 限成买卖，理由是那样等于 `restrict a general term, applicable to many objects, to one of its significations`。 <!-- claim:clm-4e30de63c8f4 -->
  - **反证条件**：若属辩护词须撤
