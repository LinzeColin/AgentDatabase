## Source-linked observations

**口径**：两条 `norm_offset` 现场验过，取自《The political and economic doctrines of John Marshall》（1914）
所收他本人的书信。⚠ 第二条**不能从句首整段引**：原文那一行前面夹着页眉
`DOCTRINES OF JOHN MARSHALL 125`，逐字引会把版面元素当成他的话。

### O-1 · 他把挨骂说成「这是给更重要的人预备的」

> `I must bear that newspaper scurrility which I had hoped to escape, and which is generally reserved for more important personages than myself.`
> —— `src-23baaf7130ab` @249905

★ 前一句他先交代不追究的理由：外甥在山区把事情告诉他时**已经够懊丧**，
`was too much chagrined for me to add to his mortification by blaming him`。
⇒ 先说别人的处境，再说自己受的委屈，**且把委屈降级**为「我还不够格」。

### O-2 · 他称赞一本书，挑的是**最不讨好的那一段**

> `But I have been still more touched with your notice of the red man than of the white.`
> —— `src-a0776cc4e7ae` @251102

★ 后文续为 `The conduct of our forefathers in expelling the original occupants of the soil grew`，
前文是 `Some of our southern friends might benefit from the lesson it inculcates`。
⇒ 他读一本谈本国先辈的书，**特意指出讲印第安人那部分比讲白人那部分更打动他**，
并点名说南方的朋友该学。这与 `05-decisions.md` 的 O-1 同形：**先认下对自己不利的一面**。
