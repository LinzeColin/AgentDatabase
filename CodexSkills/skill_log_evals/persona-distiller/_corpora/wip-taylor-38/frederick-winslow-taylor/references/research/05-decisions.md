## Source-linked observations

- **《混凝土成本》（1912）是 Taylor 将时间研究方法系统应用于建筑行业的成果，其方法论核心
  是"分析→合成→验证"三段式**：Taylor 在序言中记述了该方法——"(1) Analysis, i.e.,
  analyzing all of the work in a given trade into its small elements; separating the
  efficient from the inefficient movements of the workmen; and then studying with a
  stop-watch the proper time for making each elementary movement. (2) Synthesis, i.e.,
  making this time study practically useful by grouping together the proper series of
  movements for doing each class of work; summing up these 'unit times,' and adding the
  proper time allowance for unavoidable delays and accidents, etc.; and, finally,
  classifying and tabulating these data so as to place them in the simplest and most
  convenient form for practical use. (3) Proof, i.e., testing the value of the data and
  tables contained in the book by computing from the book the time it ought to take to
  build a structure about to be erected, and then comparing the 'book time and cost'
  with the 'actual time and cost' of the structure"（1912，src-b91d41aca463）。他还指出
  "the time required for 'Analysis,' i.e., direct time study, is but a small fraction of
  the time afterward spent in 'Synthesis' and 'Proof'"（1912，src-b91d41aca463）。

- **《混凝土成本》导论记录了 Taylor 从 Midvale 到建筑行业的方法迁移决策**：Taylor 记述了
  从工业到建筑行业的时间研究迁移——"In from six to eight years the application of this
  time study to a large range and variety of work had resulted in such great economy in
  the many trades practised in the Midvale Steel works that the writer decided to give
  his whole time to systematizing other companies along similar lines"（1912，
  src-b91d41aca463）。他还记载了与 Thompson 的合作决策——"in 1894 Mr. Sanford E. Thompson,
  who had been cooperating with the writer during much of this period, joined him in an
  effort to apply these principles to the building trades"（1912，src-b91d41aca463），
  并坦承初判有误——"We were sure that the application of motion and time study to the
  building trades would be followed by the same useful results as had been obtained in
  industrial work; and it was our judgment that the necessary 'time study' could be
  quickly made. In the latter supposition, however, we were wrong"（1912，
  src-b91d41aca463）。

- **《混凝土成本》展现了 Taylor "分解为基本动作→测定单位时间→合成标准工时"的工程决策
  方法论**：Taylor 论述其方法——"He made a careful analysis of the movements of workmen
  in one job after another, eliminated all of the useless motions, and substituted fast
  for slow and inefficient movements. And then he studied with a stop-watch the time
  which a first-class man should take to make each of the elementary movements into
  which all kinds of work may be sub-divided"（1912，src-b91d41aca463）。他将合成后的
  时间称为"unit times"——"By adding together the proper series of these 'unit times'
  (as they are called), the correct speed for doing any kind of work was obtained"
  （1912，src-b91d41aca463），并指出此方法"almost invariably"使工人效率大幅提升且
  "with but little greater effort to themselves"（1912，src-b91d41aca463）。

- **Taylor 的决策逻辑以"工人加薪 30% 以上同时公司获利"为经济基础**：他在《混凝土成本》中
  论证经济可行性——"This enabled us to pay them a substantial premium or bonus (an
  increase of 30% or more in their wages) whenever they did the tasks which were assigned
  them in the proper times, and still leave a good profit for the Company"（1912，
  src-b91d41aca463）。他还指出工人态度的转变——"When these men found that we were
  deliberately planning to give them a large increase in pay, instead of trying to cut
  down their wages, they ceased to be our antagonists and cooperated most heartily in
  introducing the new system"（1912，src-b91d41aca463）。

- **《混凝土论》（1905）中 Taylor 制定了高钢规格，这是将科学方法应用于材料标准的决策
  实例**：Taylor 在合著中制定了适用于钢筋混凝土的高钢规格——"Specifications for First-class
  or High Steel, drawn up by Mr. Taylor, are, we believe, the first recommendations which
  have been made to safely adapt this important material to reinforced concrete
  construction"（1905，src-28ad6346694f）。该书还强调科学配比优于经验法则——"The method
  is exact and scientific and not 'rule-of-thumb'"（1905，src-28ad6346694f），以及
  "more accurate and scientific to measure the aggregates by weight than by volume"
  （1905，src-28ad6346694f），展现了 Taylor 将"科学取代经验法则"的原则延伸至材料配比。

- **《科学管理原理》中生铁搬运案例展示了 Taylor "科学选人→设定任务→激励配合"的决策链**：
  Taylor 记述了选人决策过程——"We therefore carefully watched and studied these 75 men for
  three or four days, at the end of which time we had picked out four men who appeared to
  be physically able to handle pig iron at the rate of 47 tons per day"（1911，
  src-c864253fe201）。选人的标准包括性格、习惯与野心——"We looked up their history as far
  back as practicable and thorough inquiries were made as to the character, habits, and
  the ambition of each of them"（1911，src-c864253fe201）。最终选中的"Schmidt"获得 60%
  加薪——"he received 60 per cent. higher wages than were paid to other men who were not
  working on task work"（1911，src-c864253fe201）。

- **《车间管理》中 Taylor 将计划室定位为"工厂大脑"，并将日常运营决策权从管理层转移到
  计划部门**：Taylor 论述——"The shop, and indeed the whole works, should be managed, not
  by the manager, superintendent, or foreman, but by the planning department"（1919，
  src-cb1c3263e778）。他甚至提出"the works could run smoothly even if the manager,
  superintendent and their assistants outside the planning room were all to be away for
  a month at a time"（1919，src-cb1c3263e778），并以 Bethlehem Steel 为例——
  "The large machine shop of the Bethlehem Steel Company was more than a quarter of a
  mile long, and this was successfully run from a single planning room"（1919，
  src-cb1c3263e778）。

- **《计件工资制》中 Taylor 论证了"基本动作时间研究→差別计件率→自动选优"的薪酬决策链**：
  Taylor 论述基本动作研究的优越性——"While this method seems complicated at the first
  glance, it is, in fact, far simpler and more effective than the old method of recording
  the time required to do whole jobs of work, and then, after looking over the records
  of similar jobs, guessing at the time required for any new piece of work"（1896，
  src-92e733171b0a）。他还论证差別计件率的自动筛选功能——"It automatically selects and
  attracts the best men for each class of work, and it develops many first-class men
  who would otherwise remain slow or inaccurate, while at the same time it discourages
  and sifts out men who are incurably lazy or inferior"（1896，src-92e733171b0a）。

## Candidate Claims

- Taylor 的工程决策方法论为"分析→合成→验证"三段式（src-b91d41aca463）。
- 时间研究方法从 Midvale 工业场景迁移至建筑行业，初判快速完成但实际耗时 17 年（src-b91d41aca463）。
- "单位时间"（unit times）合成法是 Taylor 成本估算的标准化工具（src-b91d41aca463）。
- Taylor 的经济决策逻辑以"工人加薪 30% 以上 + 公司获利"为双赢基础（src-b91d41aca463）。
- Taylor 制定高钢规格是材料标准化的早期实践（src-28ad6346694f）。
- 生铁搬运案例展示了"科学选人→任务设定→激励配合"的完整决策链（src-c864253fe201）。
- Taylor 将计划室设为工厂决策中枢，甚至主张管理层缺席一个月工厂仍可运转（src-cb1c3263e778）。
- 差別计件率制度具有自动筛选优秀工人的功能（src-92e733171b0a）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

《混凝土成本》与《混凝土论》均为 Taylor 与 Thompson 合著，其中混凝土成本的大量数据与表格
工作主要由 Thompson 完成（Taylor 在序言中记载"During the past seventeen years Mr. Thompson
and his able assistants have devoted practically their whole time to a minute,
painstaking study of the building trades"，见 src-b91d41aca463）。Taylor 的角色更多是
方法论提出者与初始推动者。Thompson 文集（src-fc8570b0f7ad）中也记录了 Taylor 与 Thompson
合著此书的事实，但指出此书的核心方法来自 Taylor。

## Unknowns and source gaps

《混凝土成本》中大量表格数据（Tables 16-31 为实际结构成本，Tables 165-172 为材料成本等）
未在文本层面逐条引用，其数据质量取决于 17 年时间研究的覆盖范围。Taylor 在 Concrete Costs
导论中提及 Thompson 已积累数据准备出版砖砌、抹灰、木工等行业的后续著作，但这些后续出版物
不在 train 语料中。Taylor 个人在 Midvale 与 Bethlehem 的具体工程决策（如金属切削参数选择）
散见于 Shop Management 和 Principles 中，但以案例叙述为主，缺乏完整的决策记录文件。

## Handoff to adjudication

decisions 道以《混凝土成本》为核心，辅以《混凝土论》及 P1 管理著作中的决策段落，支撑
Taylor 作为"以时间研究为统一方法论、跨行业实施工程与管理决策的系统实践者"的 persona。
其决策模式高度一致：始终以基本动作分解→单位时间测定→标准化合成→激励配套为链条，从
工业制造延伸至建筑施工。关键决策的经济逻辑——"高效率=高工资+低成本"——贯穿从 1895 年
计件工资制到 1912 年混凝土成本的全部著述。
