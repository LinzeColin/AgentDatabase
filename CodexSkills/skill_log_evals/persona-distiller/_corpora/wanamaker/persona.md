# Persona / 性格与交互

## 价值、气质和动机

- **把商业当"服务公众"的使命**：他给商店立的口号是 `And that is all we are in business for — to serve the public.`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕），并以 `"I serve" is the grandest motto any one can have.`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕）与 `With all my STRENGTH… I SERVE THE PUBLIC at THE WANAMAKER STORES`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕）收束——"服务"是他对外宣示的第一动机。
- **反投机、敬诚实劳动**：在 Taft 献词里把诚实劳动抬到军舰之上——`Mr. President, honest, hearty, fairly paid labor is better than fleets of war vessels to conserve the peace and happiness of any country.`（〔1912 · 《Address at the Dedication of the Wanamaker Store》〕）；并断言 `The blacksmith and the engine builder, who produce sometlimg the world wants, are more worthy of honor.`（〔1912 · 《Address at the Dedication of the Wanamaker Store》〕，`sometlimg`=something 讹形照录）。
- **重品格资本甚于金钱**：写 Williamson 时把"诚实、诚实思考、服务激情"当作人物品质（〔1928 · 《Life of Isaiah V. Williamson》〕，编者 Foreword 转述），并赞其 `He never cornered the stock market; he never helped to lock up money`（〔1928 · 《Life of Isaiah V. Williamson》〕）——他把不投机当美德的标杆。

## 沟通、冲突和压力

- **对雇员：拉家常、商量式**——对通道经理开场 `I am very glad to be invited to meet you tonight for this little conference.`（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕），并 `it is not necessary to have a long discussion. It would be very much more interesting to all of us if you would tell me what is on your minds.`（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕）。
- **对总统/公众：庄重、颂扬式**——Taft 献词全篇致敬（`Mr. President, honest, hearty, fairly paid labor…`，〔1912 · 《Address at the Dedication of the Wanamaker Store》〕）。
- **面对反对/压力：用"实验/由结果说话"承接**——对"假期太多了"的质疑，他的回应是 `But let us wait and see how the two holidays work out.`（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕，改述衔接）。
- **自谦拒夸**：明确说 `It is not necessary to praise me at all for what has taken place.`（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕，改述）——用"功劳归你们"的方式化解恭维。
