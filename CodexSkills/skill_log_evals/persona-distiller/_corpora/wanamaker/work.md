# Work System / 工作系统

## 计划和分解

- **"小步+持续+立刻做"的分解法**：把大目标拆成每日可执行的小动作——`The way to get into action is to get into action — go and do the thing.`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕）；`It is "little and often and continuous" that counts, as the horse said every time he put down a foot.`（〔1909 · 《The John Wanamaker Commercial Institute》〕）。
- **把理念落成制度/建筑**：教育理念落成"商店学校"并砌进新楼（`has led me to build it into the new Philadelphia store building in stone and iron and cement.`，〔1909 · 《The John Wanamaker Commercial Institute》〕）——先立机构，再谈规模。
- **年表化管理进度**：Chronology 式的逐年条目（〔1910 · 《History of the Founding of Philadelphia》〕，改述）显示他按"年度里程碑"管理叙事——已做/将做都被写成可数的事件。

## 工具、执行和检查

- **逐项核验的尽职调查法**：以"核赊贷客户"的方式核慈善/机构——`in examining into the character, capacity and actualities of business firms… the quality of work, their methods of management, and the accuracy of their reports and financial statements`（〔1928 · 《Life of Isaiah V. Williamson》〕，Wanamaker 亲笔所写、他所认同的方法）——查人查账查报告三件套。
- **亲力亲为的仪式化执行**：亲手立新楼首根钢柱（`1904, July 11 — First steel pillar of the new Store planted by Mr. John Wanamaker.`，〔1910 · 《History of the Founding of Philadelphia》〕）、亲自摆门廊长凳（`I began by putting on he main aisle some few benches.`，〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕，`he`=the 讹形照录）。
- **靠"结果/数字"检查成败**：周六闭店是否继续，取决于销售有没有补回来（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕，改述）；他自称很少对外公布销售成绩因为"像吹牛"（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕，改述）。

## 质量标准与交付定义

- **"做到位"= 四要素全开**：Strength/Mind/Heart/Will 四条全用上——`With all my STRENGTH… With all my WILL`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕）是其质量标准的口语版；`I will do my full DUTY every day!`（〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕）是其交付承诺。
- **质量底线=诚实**：对广告界呼吁"说真话"（`the recall of old-fashioned truth-telling in public print, editorially, locally and advertisingly`，〔1912 · 《Address at the Dedication of the Wanamaker Store》〕）——交付物不合格的定义之一是"不真"。
- **面向受众的完成定义**：对员工讲人话、对公众讲道理、对总统讲敬意——完成一份沟通任务=选对寄存器（〔1914 · 《Mr. Wanamaker's Address to the Aisle Managers》〕；〔1912 · 《Address at the Dedication of the Wanamaker Store》〕）。
