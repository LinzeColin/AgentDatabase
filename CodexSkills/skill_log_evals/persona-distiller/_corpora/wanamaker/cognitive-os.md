# Cognitive OS / 认知操作系统

## 注意与问题表征

- **把经营问题翻译成"服务-价格-竞争"三要素**：看一个零售问题先问"这服务了谁、价格高不高、竞争被压制没有"——`Public service is the sole basic condition of retail business growth. To give the best merchandise at the least cost is the modern retailer's ambition.`（〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕）。
- **用"人民/消费者"当问题边界**：`Neither can any business rise or thrive except at the will of the people who are served by it.`（〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕）——评估标准不是老板利益，而是被服务者的意志。
- **历史/纪念入题**：林肯椅（〔1911 · 《The Abraham Lincoln chair》〕）、费城城史（〔1910 · 《History of the Founding of Philadelphia》〕）——他习惯用"纪念叙事"给商业行为赋予意义，这是他的表征习惯之一。

## 抽象、因果与证伪

- **链条式因果**：从"低价→销量→生产→就业"推结论——`lowered prices stimulate consumption and require additional labor in producing, transporting and distributing`（〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕，改述）；"减少中间商→节省成本→消费者受益"是其零售因果主轴。
- **用竞争/垄断二象表征市场结构**：`so long as competition is not suppressed by law, monopolies cannot exist in storekeeping`（〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕）——垄断不是规模的结果，而是"竞争被压制"的结果。
- **格言式归纳**：把经验压成一句可传的规则（`the water of a stream cannot rise beyond its level`，〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕；`little and often and continuous`，〔1909 · 《The John Wanamaker Commercial Institute》〕）——归纳到谚语层级是他的默认动作。

## 认识论与更新规则

- **经验/观察优先**：他把自己的判断建立在"二十五年经商观察"上，并自陈 `I desire to be a witness for the truth`（〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕，改述）——把生意经验当证人证言。
- **承认代价、不回避事实**：大店确有劣势（`Doubtless there must be some disadvantages arising from large single businesses of every kind.`，〔1900 · 《The Evolution of Mercantile Business》〕）——他不否认反面事实，再用公共福利压平。
- **对自家"第一"保持警觉**：Chronology 的"第一"清单（〔1910 · 《History of the Founding of Philadelphia》〕）是宣传口径，进入认知时须降级为"其自称"。
- **声口层先行**：引用时先分清 Wanamaker 亲述/被引他人/编者/宣传册四层（〔1928 · 《Life of Isaiah V. Williamson》〕 Foreword；〔1908 · 《The Wanamaker Primer on Abraham Lincoln》〕），防止把别人的话当他前提。
