## Source-linked observations

- **《Field System》展示了 Gilbreth 将系统化管理方法应用于建筑承包业务的决策框架，核心是"无账簿会计"系统**：John P. Slack 在评述中记载"The Gilbreth system of accounting has been utilized under all kinds of local conditions in nearly all the states, and in the provinces of Canada as well. It provides for no cash book, journal nor ledger, but in their place substitutes what is in fact a systematic set of memoranda. Their completeness is such that the owner may see at a glance each Saturday what the total cost of a given structure has been up to the previous Thursday. The system shows furthermore, what the cost of materials will be for the completion of the job"（1908，src-512232eb4755）。该系统还实现了材料采购的流程化管控——"When materials are to be purchased, the regular form"订单流程、"the job clerk gives the person furnishing such materials"收据机制、"required to forward duplicate bills to the"总部核账（1908，src-512232eb4755），确保多工地远程并行管理的成本可控。

- **《Field System》记录了 Gilbreth 通过系统化管理实现"整镇速建"的决策成果**：Slack 记载"three complete industrial towns which the Gilbreth organization erected, each in the phenomenal time of a few months; one at Sprague's Falls, Maine, one at Piercefield, N. Y., and another at Canton, N. C. On each of these contracts the system of accounting without books, fully described below, was used, and the size of the contracts alone furnishes eloquent testimony to the excellence of the system"（1908，src-512232eb4755）。Slack 还指出该系统的保密级别——"For several years previous to its recent publication the Gilbreth 'Field System' was open to the inspection only of the men in the employ of that organization. Only a limited number of copies of the volume were in existence, each being numbered, and the possessor of each being accountable for its return even to the extent of being bonded in a small sum to cover its loss"（1908，src-512232eb4755）。

- **《Field System》阐述了承包商面临的远程多工地管理挑战与系统化对策**：Slack 论述承包商与制造商的根本差异——"The manufacturer as a rule groups his tools, human and mechanical, at one location, possibly under one roof, in any case in one plant. His forces, under effective direction, may work as a unit"，而承包商"One structure is erected in one state and another perhaps a thousand miles distant. The one building may be a factory, the other a city sky-scraper"（1908，src-512232eb4755）。Gilbreth 的 Field System 正是针对这一挑战设计的——"A notable instance of the application of a working system through which field work may be executed from beginning to end is found in the organization of Frank B. Gilbreth of New York"（1908，src-512232eb4755）。

- **《Corrugated Concrete Piles》记录了 Gilbreth 在混凝土桩工法中的工程决策：以波纹截面替代传统圆桩，兼顾表面摩擦力与排水功能**：Gilbreth 记载其波纹设计的目的——"the corrugations, which serve the purpose of: (a) Increasing the surface for skin friction. (b) Outlets for the water from the jet"（1906，src-84fb8b78b859）。他还记录了桩的几何设计决策——"the pile tapered from sixteen inches across at the larger end to eleven inches at the smaller, lower end"，以及中心孔道的设计——"The hole in the centre is made 3½ inches in diameter at the top of the pile, and 2 inches at the bottom of the pile. This hole is made tapering for two reasons: 1. So as to have a large quantity of concrete in the lower end of the pile. 2. So that the tapering plug that is used to cast the hole in the pile can be easily withdrawn"（1906，src-84fb8b78b859）。

- **《Corrugated Concrete Piles》记录了 Gilbreth 在基础方案选择中的工程经济决策逻辑——以混凝土桩替代木桩以节约成本和时间**：Gilbreth 论述方案比选——"Two or three times as many wooden piles as concrete piles would have to be used, owing to the inferior supporting power of the wooden piles. Again the wooden piles would have to be cut off at a grade as low or lower than the low-water level, while the concrete piles could extend up to the reinforced concrete footings"，以及经济分析——"With the concrete piles it was possible not only to save this cost, but also to save so much time that the rent available would come near to covering the cost of the piles"（1906，src-84fb8b78b859）。他还记录了质量管控决策——"One of the fine features of this pile is that there is no guesswork regarding its construction, or regarding the location of its reinforcement"（1906，src-84fb8b78b859），以及分层浇筑的工序设计——"Figure X shows how the piles were cast in tiers to economize space in a city lot"（1906，src-84fb8b78b859）。

- **《Process Charts》系统定义了工艺图表方法的目标——将整个流程可视化以便在改动前审视全局**：Gilbreth 论述"The Process Chart is a device for visualizing a process as a means of improving it. Every detail of a process is more or less affected by every other detail; therefore the entire process must be presented in such form that it can be visualized all at once before any changes are made in any of its subdivisions"（1921，src-4613a3b8b48f）。他还指出"In any subdivision of the process under examination, any changes made without due consideration of all the decisions and all the motions that precede and follow that subdivision will often be found unsuited to the ultimate plan of operation"（1921，src-4613a3b8b48f）。

- **《Process Charts》将工艺图表定位为"发现最佳方法的可视化第一步"，并指出其在防止"向下发明"中的作用**：Gilbreth 论述"The process chart serves as an indicator of profitable changes. It assists in preventing 'inventing downward,' and stimulates invention that is cumulative and of permanent value. It is not only the first step in visualizing the one best way to do work, but is useful in every stage of deriving it"（1921，src-4613a3b8b48f）。他还记录了工艺图表的广泛适用性——"The process chart lends itself equally well to the routine of production, selling, accounting and finance"（1921，src-4613a3b8b48f），以及标准化的渐进路径——"Standards in writing should be made, even if there is not the managerial mechanism necessary to enforce and maintain them. Standards made even with enforcing mechanism absent will hasten the day when the enforcing and maintaining mechanism will be installed and continuously operated"（1921，src-4613a3b8b48f）。
