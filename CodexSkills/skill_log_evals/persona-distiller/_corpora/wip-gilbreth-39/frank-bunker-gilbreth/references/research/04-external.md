## Source-linked observations

- **Lillian Gilbreth 在《管理心理学》中将"管理心理学"定义为"指导工作的心智对被指导工作的影响，以及这种被指导和未被指导的工作对工人心智的影响"**：她写道"The Psychology of Management, as here used, means, — the effect of the mind that is directing work upon that work which is directed, and the effect of this undirected and directed work upon the mind of the worker"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。这一定义将 Frank 的动作研究方法提升至心理学层面，论证管理学不仅涉及工程效率，还涉及对工人心理的理解与回应。她还指出心理学长期被排除在工程教育之外——"Psychology, in the popular phrase, is 'the study of the mind.' It has for years been included in the training of all teachers, and has been one of the first steps for the student of philosophy; but it has not, usually, been included among the studies of the young scientific or engineering student"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。

- **《管理心理学》系统论证科学管理不仅不扼杀个体性，反而是建立在"对个体的识别"这一基本原则之上**：Lillian 在结论部分明确反驳"科学管理扼杀个体性"的普遍误解——"Contrary to a widespread belief that Scientific Management kills individuality, it is built on the basic principle of recognition of the individual, not only as an economic unit but also as a personality, with all the idiosyncrasies that distinguish a person"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。她进一步指出"Scientific Management fosters individuality by functionalizing work"以及"Measurement, in Scientific Management, is of ultimate units of subdivision"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575），将 Frank 的动作分解方法与个体化管理直接关联。

- **《管理心理学》将动作研究定义为"确定最少浪费方法"的核心工具，并系统阐述 micro-motion study 作为精确测量手段**：Lillian 论述"Motion Study Is Determining Methods of Least Waste. — Motion Study is the dividing of the elements of the work into the most fundamental subdivisions possible; studying these fundamental units separately and in relation to one another; and from these studied, chosen units, when timed, building up methods of least waste"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。她还描述了 micro-motion study——"Micro-motion study is timing sub-divisions, or elements of motions by carrying out the principles of motion study to a greater degree of accuracy by means of a motion picture camera, a clock that will record different times of day in each picture of a moving picture film together with a cross sectioned background and other devices for assisting in measuring the relative efficiency and wastefulness of motions"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575），为 Frank 的技术发明提供了心理学维度的理论正当性。

- **《管理心理学》从心理学视角论述疲劳与工人福利的关系，主张科学管理必须以"公正对待"为前提**：Lillian 论述疲劳的累积效应——"no man is capable of doing his best work permanently if he is worried by the idea that he will not receive the square deal, that someone stronger than he will be allowed to cheat or to domineer over him, or that he will be sped up to such an extent that while his work will increase for one day, the next day his work will fall down because of the effect of the fatigue of the day before"（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。她还论述动作研究与时间研究在科学管理中的测量功能——"Motion Study and Time Study Are the Method of Measurement Under Scientific Management. — Under Scientific Management, much measuring is done by motion study and time study, which measure the relative efficiency of various men, of various methods, or of various kinds of equipment, surroundings, tools, etc."（1914，src-8ae7cf6e0575）。
