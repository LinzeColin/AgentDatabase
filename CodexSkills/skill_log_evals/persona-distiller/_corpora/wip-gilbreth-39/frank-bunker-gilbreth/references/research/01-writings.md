## Source-linked observations

- **Gilbreth 在《Motion Study》序言中将动作研究的目标定义为"发现并永续完美方案"，并提出三阶段方法论**：他写道"The aim of motion study is to find and perpetuate the scheme of perfection. There are three stages in this study: 1. Discovering and classifying the best practice. 2. Deducing the laws. 3. Applying the laws to standardize practice, either for the purpose of increasing output or decreasing hours of labor, or both"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。他将动作研究定位为标准化的首要因素——"Standardizing the trades is the world's most important work to-day, and motion study is the first factor in that work"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。

- **《Motion Study》将所有影响工作产出的因素系统归为三大类"变量"——工人变量、环境变量、动作变量——构成其分析框架的核心**：Gilbreth 将变量划分为"Variables of the Worker"（含 Anatomy, Brawn, Fatigue, Habits, Skill, Temperament 等十五项）、"Variables of the Surroundings, Equipment, and Tools"（含 Appliances, Clothes, Lighting, Quality of material, Special fatigue-eliminating devices 等十四项）、"Variables of the Motion"（含 Acceleration, Direction, Length, Speed, Cost, Necessity 等十三项）（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。他论述道"Every element that makes up or affects the amount of work that the worker is able to turn out must be considered separately; but the variables which must be studied in analyzing any motion, group themselves naturally into some such divisions as the following"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。

- **《Motion Study》以砌砖为例论证动作研究的巨大节约潜力——已有研究将砌砖工的动作减少了三分之二以上**：Gilbreth 写道"There is no waste of any kind in the world that equals the waste from needless, ill-directed, and ineffective motions. When one realizes that in such a trade as bricklaying alone, the motions now adopted after careful study have already cut down the bricklayer's work more than two-thirds, it is possible to realize the amount of energy that is wasted by the workers of this country"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。他还指出"Wherever motion study has been applied, the workman's output has been doubled"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。

- **《Motion Study》系统阐述疲劳的科学机理，将其分为三类并提出消除策略**：Gilbreth 引用医学研究论述疲劳的本质——"Fatigue is due to a secretion in the blood"，并引述"The toxin of fatigue is the phrase the physicians have given us with which to jar the attention of those who can only be stirred by harsh words. It has been demonstrated in the last few years that fatigue is due to an actual poison not unlike the poison or toxin of diphtheria"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。他将疲劳分为三类——"1. Fatigue due to coming to work improperly rested (fatigue brought to the job). 2. Unnecessary fatigue, due to unnecessary work, unnecessary motions, or uncomfortable positions, surroundings, and conditions of working. 3. Necessary fatigue, due to output"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。

- **《Motion Study》强调动作进步不能仅靠"消除"，而必须通过"标准化单元重构"实现**：Gilbreth 论述道"It is as well to recognize first as last that real progress from the best present method to the standard method can never be made solely by elimination. The sooner this is recognized the better. Elimination is often an admirable makeshift. But the only real progress comes through a reconstruction of the operation, building it up of standardized units, or elements"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。他同时强调科学研究的必要性——"the worker cannot, by himself, arrange to do his work in the most economical manner in accordance with the laws of motion study"（1911，src-d229be6683e5）。

- **《Bricklaying System》将动作研究定义为对动作价值、时间和序列的系统检验**：Gilbreth 在书末术语表中定义"Motion Study. — The examination of the vahie, time and sequence of motions for producing the greatest results in the least time with the least effort and fatigue"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92）。他指出"the motion study in this book is but the beginning of an era of motion study, that will eventually affect all of our methods of teaching trades. It will cut down production costs and increase the efficiency and wages of the workman"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92）。

- **《Bricklaying System》提出砌砖工成功的核心原则是"用最少的动作"而非"动作最快"**：Gilbreth 论述道"To be pre-eminently successful: (a) A mechanic must know his trade; (b) he must be quick motioned; and (c) he must use the fewest possible motions to accomplish the desired result"，并进一步指出"It is a fact beyond dispute that the fastest bricklayers, and generally the best bricklayers, are those who use the fewest motions, and not those who are naturally the quickest motioned"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92）。

- **《Bricklaying System》记录了 Gilbreth 的"packet system"（砖包系统）和可调式脚手架等工程创新**：Gilbreth 记载"The Gilbreth packet system consists of conveying bricks upon packets from the pile in the street to the top of the wall"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92），并通过分析传统砌砖工序图——"it is not probable that any one first class bricklayer would use all of the eighteen operations as shown on this chart"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92）——展示了通过标准化减少操作数量的路径。他还创新了可顶升脚手架——"Jacking Up the Scaffold While the Men Are Working on it"（1909，src-a105f1d59a92）。

- **《Fatigue Study》将疲劳消除定位为"消除人类最大的不必要浪费"，建立动作研究与疲劳研究之间的因果链**：Gilbreth 在序言中论述"No organization can continue to be of first quality whose workers are over-fatigued. Other things being equal, that country will be most happy and most successful whose workers have the least unnecessary fatigue"（1916，src-39dfef403efb）。在正文中，他将疲劳研究与动作研究直接关联——"In 'Motion Study' we stated: 'There is no waste of any kind in the world that equals the waste from needless, ill-directed, and ineffective motions.' It is an aspect of wasted motions that we are discussing here. Wasted motions mean wasted effort and wasted time. One of the results of this waste is unnecessary fatigue, caused by unnecessary effort expended during time that must, as a result, be wasted"（1916，src-39dfef403efb）。

- **《Applied Motion Study》系统记述了 Gilbreth 发明的 micro-motion 方法和 cyclegraph / chronocyclegraph 记录技术**：Gilbreth 描述 micro-motion 方法——"The micro-motion method of making motion studies consists of recording motions by means of a motion picture camera, a clock that will record different times of day in each picture of a motion picture film, a cross-sectioned background, and other devices for assisting in measuring the relative efficiency and wastefulness of motions"（1917，src-b84ad47069dd）。他还记述了 cyclegraph 的发明过程——"To overcome this lack we invented the cyclegraph method of recording motions. This consists of attaching a small electric light to the hand or other moving part of the person or machine under observation"（1917，src-b84ad47069dd），并进一步发展为 chronocyclegraph——"This time element is obtained by putting an interrupter in the light circuit, that causes the light to flash at an even rate at a known number of times per second"（1917，src-b84ad47069dd）。

- **《Measurement of the Human Factor》将测量定位为工业进步的前提，强调对人的测量比对机器的测量更具持久价值**：Gilbreth 论述"No definite and permanent advance is made in any kind of work, whether with materials or men, until use is made of measurement. This is especially true of advancement of the human factor in industry, which varies so much that unless we use measurement and abide by the results, there is no possibility of repeating the process accurately and efficiently at will, or of predicting and controlling the future conditions that assure that advancement"（1917，src-2019536f67f7）。他指出"Measurement on machines that are obsolete is of little value. Measurement of human beings is valuable forever"（1917，src-2019536f67f7）。

- **《Primer of Scientific Management》以教学为导向，系统引述 Taylor 的科学管理核心定义并加以通俗化阐释**：Gilbreth 在开篇即引用 Taylor 的定义——"'The principal object of management should be to secure the maximum prosperity for the employer coupled with the maximum prosperity for each employee'"以及"'Scientific Management has for its very foundation the firm conviction that the true interests of the two are one and the same'"（1912，src-51659308714b），将 Taylor 的四原则与自己的动作研究方法衔接，构建了一部面向初学者的科学管理入门读本。
