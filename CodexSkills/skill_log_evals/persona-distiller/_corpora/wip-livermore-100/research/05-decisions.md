# 05 · decisions（可核的具体决策）

## 一、1908 棉花：他当场否认自己在逼仓（src-da0a248f8373）

1908-05-15，他对记者说：「No, I simply sold out all my July cotton. I began to sell on
Wednesday and completed my sales this morning… **Corners are all very well in their way,
but I never will try to carry out a corner in cotton.** I have completed my July deal
and will now watch.」
**决策内容**：了结七月棉多头、拒绝把持仓推成逼仓、了结后转为观望。
**证据等级**：当日直引，最高。

## 二、1910 他为自己设了一道不可动用的底线（src-ac61de64d9b8）

「I don't expect I will ever need it, but I want to make sure that if I lose every other
dollar I've got, I will still have the means on which to live.」
**决策内容**：把一部分资产隔离在交易账户之外。
这条与他书中「The speculator has to be his own insurance broker」的原则一致（src-78d1f7f3fb9a）。

## 三、1923 Mammoth Oil 做市：宣誓证词里的自陈（src-40e2c4076059）

「I was employed to steady the market.」「I would not allow my name to be used unless
I had the privilege of buying back and creating a steady market so that the public
could sell.」结果他亲口报出：「there was a loss and I realized a profit of only
\$9,916 on the total transaction.」
**决策内容**：受雇做市、以「公众能卖出」为条件、并公开承认该役实际获利极小。
**这是本语料中唯一一份宣誓状态下的自陈。**

## 四、1924 小麦：他书里自陈的成功案例（src-78d1f7f3fb9a）

1924 年夏，小麦到达他所称的 Pivotal Point，他初次买入 500 万蒲式耳；
价格未跌破该点后再买 500 万，第二笔的均价比枢轴点高 1½ 美分。
他把「第二笔更难买到」读作**市场转强的证据**。
但他随后自陈了一个错误：赚到每蒲式耳 25 美分时就落袋，
「sat back and saw the market advance 20 cents more within a few days…
**Why had I been afraid of losing something I never really had?**」

## 五、1924 他对棉花那一役的公开定性（src-b6b01f04cda0）

「It was bad judgment.」「There I was on a falling market, collecting bales and bales
of the stuff.」——**对外定性与书中自陈一致**，不是事后美化。

## 六、书中自陈的最大一次失误（src-78d1f7f3fb9a）

他反复在同一笔棉花交易上进出五次、六周内亏掉约 20 万美元，
随后让人把棉花报价机搬走；两天后行情启动、上涨 500 点。
他自己给的归因是两条：**一是没等到价格到位就动手**
（「I did not have the will power to wait」），
**二是让恼怒影响了判断**。他把损失记为「实际亏 20 万 + 错过 100 万」。

## 七、1932 与 1934：破产与还债（src-995ce3754fc9、src-7d64414ec450、src-c4277b744fe3）

1932：「Gentlemen, I have paid them. All of them. A hundred cents on the dollar.
I have paid for my mistakes, too, and one of them cost me \$2,000,000.」
1934 破产后：「I've owed that much many times and I've always paid it off.」
「I intend to get right back to work, to restore my fortune and pay my creditors
as I have done in the past.」

## 八、1940-09-22 最后一次公开市场判断（src-3d772e864171）

他给出的是**带条件的判断**而非点位预测：
「If any one thinks Germany is going to be able to subdue England in a comparatively
short space of time, he should not own any stocks at all.」
以及一条对税负的算法性反问：「If earnings of various companies are increased from
100 to 200 per cent, what does it matter if 50 per cent of that increase does not
have to be paid out in increased taxes?」

## ⚠ 一条不得抹平的张力

他 1934 年说「我欠过很多次，每次都还清了」（src-7d64414ec450），
1940-11-29 的遗书写的是「I am not worthy of your love. **I am a failure.**」（src-5bcee03a75f1）。
**两句都是他的原话，产物中必须并陈，不得只取其一。**
