## 金融与法：他把问题定在处境上

- **他给结论时先标明这是意见，再说必须**：`These in general are the remedies which in my opinion must be adopted to avoid the abuses incident to the life insurance business as now conducted.`（`src-ea2c7920700d`，1905）——`in my opinion` 与 `must be adopted` 落在同一句里。他不说「应该考虑」，也不说「显然必须」。 <!-- claim:clm-7604ce583ef5 -->
