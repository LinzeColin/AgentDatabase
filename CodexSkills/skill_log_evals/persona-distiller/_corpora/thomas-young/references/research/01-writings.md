## Source-linked observations

- **弹性以"可测的列高"来定义（杨氏模量的操作式定义），并以定义—定理—证明推进**：他不满足于定性描述"有弹性"，而是把一个材料的弹性大小定义为一根同质柱子的高度——这根柱子底面的压力与"造成某程度压缩的重量"之比，等于"该物长度与其缩短量之比"，等于把抽象弹性变成可测量、可比较的数值量；同一节（Section IX）先立"完美弹性"定义再推出各定理，体现他自称的"从抽象数学的最简公理出发、对每个必要命题作严格证明"的方法。原文："The modulus of the elasticity of any substance is a column of the same substance, capable of producing a pressure on its base which is to the weight causing a certain degree of compression, as the length of the substance is to the diminution of its length."（1807，src-e1cfbdfb8e07）；"A substance perfectly elastic is initially extended and compressed in equal degrees by equal forces, and proportionally by proportional forces."（1807，src-e1cfbdfb8e07）

- **理论推导必须与实测数值对表，缺一个不认账**：他给"冷杉（deal）弹性模量高度约 0,328,000 英尺"这一数值时，既引 Leslie 的实验、又援 Chladni 对云杉发声的观测作交叉印证——同一结论要有两条独立证据线才写下来。原文："From an experiment made by Mr. Leslie on a bar in the circumstances, the height of the modulus of the elasticity of deal appears to be about 0,328,000 feet. Chladni's observations on the sounds of fir wood, afford very nearly the same result."（1807，src-e1cfbdfb8e07）

- **自我定位：把"眼睛调节、光的干涉定律、波动光学复兴"并列为三成就，且对自身理论被证伪持开放态度**：他在这段自述里明确宣布已"发现"干涉的一般定律，并把学说承续关系讲得很清楚——复兴 Hooke 与 Huygens 的理论、加上 Newton 关于色性的提示；同时并不宣称定论，而是把目标降为"引发对事实的充分而坦诚的讨论"，明说哪怕理论最终被驳倒也算有益于光学——以科学进步而非个人声望为归依。原文："I have myself attempted to attain a degree of certainty, in attributing the changes of the refractive powers of the eye to a variation in the form of the crystalline lens; I have discovered a general law of the mutual action of two portions of light interfering with each other, to which no exception has yet been shown; and by reviving a theory of light similar to that of Hooke and Huygens, with an improvement originally suggested by Newton, respecting the nature of colours, I have endeavoured to obtain a satisfactory explanation of many circumstances, which appear, upon a minute examination, to be in every other hypothesis difficulties absolutely insuperable."（1807，src-88da063192b4）；"It cannot be expected that all objections to such a system will at once be silenced, but if a full and candid discussion only of the facts, which I have advanced, should be excited, I trust that the science of optics will be essentially benefited, even if the theory should be ultimately confuted."（1807，src-88da063192b4）

- **双孔干涉条纹是他的标志性实验图示**：他描述的正是后来所谓双缝实验的形态——两股同源彩色光各经一个小孔射入，重叠处产生明暗条纹，条纹呈双曲线形；这表明他不仅提出理论，也亲手设计并描画可复现的实验图景。原文："The manner in which two portions of coloured light, admitted through two small apertures, produce light and dark stripes or fringes by their interference, proceeding in the form of hyperbolas; the middle ones are however usually a little dilated."（1807，src-88da063192b4）

- **把眼睛当作精密光学装置来读，并对"机制智慧"保持敬意**：他看眼睛的视角是机械师式的——每种透明介质如何按折射率收光、聚焦于视网膜——同时流露出对"每个部件被调准到其余部件"的赞叹，认为这是需要"艰苦研究"才能理解的造物。原文："The eye is an organ that exhibits, to an attentive observer, an arrangement of various substances, so correctly and delicately adapted to the purposes of the sense of vision, that we cannot help admiring, at every step, the wisdom by which each part is adjusted to the rest."（1807，src-88da063192b4）

- **调节机制之争：他认定靠晶状体曲率变化，而非眼球拉长或角膜变弯**：他排除了改变眼球长度、改变角膜曲率等备选假设，断言晶状体因内部原因增大凸度——这是他眼科工作的核心结论之一，论证方式是"先排除其他可能、再以折射与像差现象作正面证据"。原文："I apprehend that at present there is little further room for doubting, that the change is produced by an increase of the convexity of the crystalline lens, arising from an internal cause."（1807，src-88da063192b4）

- **科学方法：数学公理推演为骨干，实验证明为辅助而非替代**：他主张课程要以严格数学证明支撑全部命题，但面向听众时用实验来"展示理论与可见效果的重合"，并认为"实验与类比论证的结合是现代哲学的主要优点"。这显示他对数学与实验的层级观——数学是第一位的，实验服务于理解与教学。原文："Deeply impressed with the importance of mathematical investigations, both for the advancement of science and for the improvement of the mind, I thought it in the first place an indispensable duty to present the Royal Institution, in my Syllabus, with a connected system of natural philosophy... Here I propose to support the same propositions by experimental proofs: not that I consider such proofs as the most conclusive, or as more interesting to a truly philosophic mind than a deduction from general principles; but because there is a satisfaction in discovering the coincidence of theories with visible effects."（1807，src-88da063192b4）

- **厌恶"半吊子经验者"，强调彻底训练的价值**：他警告不要受一知半解的庸医干扰，并推崇"读定理、自己重推、再对照原证"的自学方法——认为"动用一定程度的发明力"才是把科学原理牢固装进头脑的可靠途径，这既是他自己的学习方法，也是他相信的教育法。原文："In physic, the wisest can do but little, and the ignorant can only do worse than nothing: and anxiously as we are disposed to seek whatever relief the learned and experienced may be able to afford us, so cautiously ought we to avoid the mischievous interference of the half studied empiric."（1807，src-88da063192b4）；"The exertion of a certain degree of invention is by far the surest mode of fixing any principle of science in the mind."（1807，src-e1cfbdfb8e07）

- **医学观：专著要穷尽"一个属"的全部事实，观察与文献并重**：他写肺痨专著时给自己的标准是——把"凡是能观察到或能找到记载的关于某一属疾病的性质与治疗的重要事实"尽量收全，既靠亲身观察也靠历代文献，二者并列为证据来源。原文："I have endeavoured to comprehend in this work every fact of importance, which I have been able to observe, or find recorded, with respect to the nature and cure of the diseases belonging to a single genus."（1815，src-2ed4144cd162）

- **临床分类务实：解剖证据服从"实用安排"**：他承认单看解剖可以把肺痨拆成很多种，但实用分类里只有"有咳嗽或胸痛的消耗（pulmonary consumption）"值得单列为种；负定义虽有缺点，但林奈和 Cullen 也都承认有时不可避免——分类的取舍标准是临床可操作，而非解剖学完备。原文："If we consulted the evidence of anatomy only, we might enumerate a great diversity of separate local affections... but there is only one part of the body, which, on account of the danger attending its chronic affections, as well as from the peculiarity of their symptoms, deserves to be thus distinguished in a practical arrangement."（1815，src-2ed4144cd162）

- **疾病分类学：批判 Cullen、转向林奈式严格分类，并强调"实用"= 病人在世可确证**：他原本准备沿用当时英国通行的 Cullen 疾病分类，深入后却发现问题重重、成为不可采用的障碍，转而要把自己的分类安排"套进林奈哲学的最严格规则"；同时强调 "practical" 不是空形容词——所有疾病定义都应"在病人生存期间、疾病持续期间"可以适用、可以被确证，以此同时反对纯解剖分类与纯形而上学/理论区分。原文："When I commenced the undertaking, I was disposed to acquiesce in the nosological arrangement of Dr. Cullen, as being the most generally approved and employed in this country: but upon proceeding further in the task, I found it replete with such irregularities and inconsistencies, as appeared to me to afford insuperable objections to its adoption."（1813，src-ed6b3adb3a5b）；"I have, in general, been able to accommodate the nosological arrangement, which I have ventured to propose, to the strictest rules of the Linnaean philosophy."（1813，src-ed6b3adb3a5b）；"In denominating this arrangement a system of practical nosology, the term practical has not been employed as an idle epithet... but as implying that all the definitions of diseases, which are introduced, are supposed to be applicable, and to be capable of being ascertained, during the life of the patient, and during the continuance of the disease."（1813，src-ed6b3adb3a5b）

- **术语观：精确用语是学科的防腐剂**：他把"正确使用术语"看成解剖学、数学、化学免于野蛮化的原因，反过来说医学正因缺这一条而受害——这是他作为博学家对语言精确性的执念，也是他把多门学科在"命名法"层面打通的方式。原文："A proper and correct use of terms has preserved anatomy, mathematics, and chemistry from barbarism: the want of it has been highly pernicious to medicine."（1813，src-ed6b3adb3a5b）

- **象形文字方法论：以罗塞塔三语碑为钥匙，编纂上"拒收注释与任意假说"**：他断言罗塞塔三语碑为整体解读象形文字带来前景，并在丛书体例里规定不收入任何评注、甚至不采用特定命名法，"以免引入任意假说和错误结论"——只呈现尽量忠实的材料本身，把解读留给读者。同一篇里他还认为单靠一个人力量太弱、一生太短，主张靠学会的持续协作完成。原文："The triple Inscription of Rosetta having afforded a prospect of the partial interpretation of the Egyptian hieroglyphics in general, it becomes a matter of high importance... For this purpose, the efforts of a single individual would probably be too feeble, and the duration of a single life might possibly be too short; but it may be effected with much more ease, and with far greater certainty, by the continued cooperation of a Select Society."（1823，src-c1504874ac13）；"In order to avoid the introduction of arbitrary hypotheses and erroneous conclusions, no commentaries, nor even any particular nomenclature, will be admitted into this series of hieroglyphics."（1823，src-c1504874ac13）

- **实验精神：坚持"尽量少靠别人协作，把实验做在自己眼睛上"**：他测量自己眼睛的尺寸与调节范围，公开声明"确信独自观察总有利，我努力把大部分实验限制在自己眼睛上，并一般按一个近似于我自己的眼睛来立算"——亲自充当被试，是自觉的选择而非不得已。同一来源里他叙述在牛眼晶状体里"发现一种结构，似乎扫清了长期笼罩该分支的困难"（晶状体的纤维结构），把解剖观察与调节理论互相印证。原文（法文）："Persuadé qu'il y a toujours avantage à faire ses observations avec aussi peu de collaboration que possible, je me suis efforcé de limiter la plupart de mes expériences à mes propres yeux, et, en général, j'établirai mes calculs dans l'hypothèse d'un œil à peu près semblable au mien."（1894，src-61782774d25c）；"En examinant avec soin, à l'œil nu, le cristallin de l'œil d'un bœuf, sorti de sa capsule, j'ai découvert une structure qui semble lever toutes les difficultés qui ont si longtemps obscurci cette branche de l'optique."（1894，src-61782774d25c）
