## Source-linked observations

- **生理学还原论：生命体内的机械运动受与无生命物质相同的普适定律支配，血液循环本质上是一个流体力学问题。** Croonian 演讲开门见山地给出他的纲领——生命体与死物服从同一套力学规律，因此心脏与动脉的功能可化为水力学题。原文："The mechanical motions, which take place in an animal body, are regulated by the same general laws as the motions of inanimate bodies. Thus the force of gravitation acts precisely in the same manner, and in the same degree, on living as on dead matter"；"there is no case in which it can be proved, that animated bodies are exempted from any of the affections to which inanimate bodies are liable"；"the inquiry, in what manner, and in what degree, the circulation of the blood depends on the muscular and elastic powers of the heart and of the arteries, supposing the nature of those powers to be known, must become simply a question belonging to the most refined departments of the theory of hydraulics"（1809，src-c75d65ab2ff5）。

- **论证风格：先公开列出研究计划再分步展开，并以自己早先提交皇家学会的公式为地基反复自指。** 演讲一开始就宣布"第一问…第二问…第三问…最后补充"，后文多处"from the formula which I have already laid before the Society"、"the hydraulic investigations which I lately laid before the Royal Society"——系统性、自引式推进自己的研究纲领。原文："In examining the functions of the heart and arteries, I shall inquire, in the first place, upon the grounds of the hydraulic investigations which I have already submitted to the Royal Society, what would be the nature of the circulation of the blood, if the whole of the veins and arteries were invariable in their dimensions, like tubes of glass or of bone"（1809，src-c75d65ab2ff5）。

- **对权威不盲从：直言批评生理学泰斗 Haller"因缺乏数学知识而推理错误"。** 在皇家学会讲台上点名批评重量级权威，措辞直接，不加以缓冲。原文："nor is this the only instance in which Haller has been led to reason erroneously, from a want of mathematical knowledge : he may, however, have observed the particles of blood moving in the axis of a vessel with a velocity much exceeding the mean velocity of its whole contents"（1809，src-c75d65ab2ff5）。

- **认知审慎与诚实：主动承认自己的计算含大量猜测成分，拒绝把结论外推到实验条件之外，并明确划出机械理论的边界。** 他在多处理性标注不确定性与"最稳妥的做法"。原文："in a determination which must be in great measure conjectural we cannot expect perfect accuracy"；"It is safest therefore to reason upon the elasticity of any substance, from experiments made without any great deviation from the circumstances to which the calculation is to be applied"；在讨论炎症扩散时说："it is scarcely possible to form any reasonable conjecture that can lead to its explanation: this circumstance appears to be placed beyond the reach of any mechanical theory, and to belong rather to some mutual communication of the functions of the nervous system"（1809，src-c75d65ab2ff5）。

- **面对争议的自我定位：预期反对、拒绝"仓促否定"，但克制、不强求流行观点立即改观。** 演讲结尾是他典型的争辩姿态——坚持己见但把姿态放低，明确说自己"既不指望也不奢求"被普遍接受。原文："Although the arguments, which I have advanced, appear to me sufficient to prove, that, in the ordinary state of the circulation, the muscular powers of the arteries have very little effect in propelling the blood, yet I neither expect nor desire that the prevailing opinion should at once be universally abandoned. I wish, however, to protest once more against a hasty rejection of my theory, from a superficial consideration of cases, like that which has been related by Dr. Clarke"（1809，src-c75d65ab2ff5）。

- **书信中的礼貌与自信（致 Kater，1818）：先祝贺同侪，再温和地挑明 Laplace 的方法"过于繁复"，并自荐一个"更简单、普通人也能懂"的推导。** 这封信集中体现他作为通信者的得体与自我评价：他并不贬低权威，只说自己的路径更简明，且"忍不住"把自己满意的推理寄给对方。原文："I cannot forbear to congratulate you on the discovery of the singular property of your pendulum, which has lately been demonstrated by Mr. Laplace, since it appears to remove the only doubt, that could reasonably be entertained, of the extreme accuracy of the results of your experiments"；"although Mr. Laplace's train of reasoning, from mechanical principles, is somewhat too elaborate to be readily followed through all the symbols in which it is enveloped"；"The investigation may however be conducted in a method much more simple and intelligible to ordinary capacities, than that which has been adopted by the celebrated mathematician to whom we are indebted for the theorem ; and I am tempted to send you an 'apergu' of the reasoning by which I have satisfied myself respecting it"（1855，src-e0b52403d6e4）。

- **方法自律：先独立验算、再查权威文献（致 Baily，1828）。** 他明确写下自己的研究纪律——在查 Abbe Halma 的注释本之前，先把想核对的要点自己单独算一遍，注释只用于事后校验。原文："you have also had the goodness to furnish me with the elaborate Commentaries of the Abbe Halma, which I did not venture to consult until I had made a separate computation of the chief points that I wished to ascertain: although I afterwards obtained from them some valuable assistance in verifying my results, and in the more ready comparison of the different parts of the wonderful original with each other"（1855，src-e0b52403d6e4）。

- **公开论争的修辞策略（致 Morgan，1828）：以"请您纠正"的谦辞开场、以"科学人当互通有无"收尾，向一位受尊敬的老精算师当面拆台却全程克制有礼。** 他据 Equitable Society 自身公布的数据推出更乐观的死亡率曲线，公开挑战该协会的官方解读，但把话说成"恳请您注意、以便您纠正"。原文："Having unfortunately failed, on some former occasions, of fully comprehending the meaning of your expressions, I earnestly entreat your attention to a few remarks on your late statement of the Experience of the Equitable Society, in order that you may correct, if possible, the exaggerated conclusions which appear to me to be fairly deducible from the numbers that you have published"；结尾抬出科学共同体的规范："But, as a man of science, it is natural to hope that you will be ready to allow other men of science to partake in the fruits of your researches, and that you will be desirous of vindicating yourself from all possible suspicion of ambiguity and of inconsistency"（1855，src-e0b52403d6e4）。

- **错而能改、主动让功（致 Baily，1829 两封信）：发现误把他人注脚里的定理当新发现印成大写后，第一时间认错、主动提议公开更正，唯一在意的是"别让这件事分散读者对文章主旨的注意"。** 这是极强的人格证据——学术荣誉受损时他仍主动给对手"获胜的把柄"，并愿意按对方指定方式公开认账。原文："I felt perfectly confident that Morgan could not have omitted to notice the approximation if it had been before observed, and I enquired no farther, so that I have allowed you grounds for a triumph, if you think proper to employ them"；"I have been thinking, that, as an attempt has been made to direct the public attention to the oversight which I acknowledged to you the moment that I was aware of it, it might be right that I should ask you, whether there was any form, or any channel, by which you would like that I should publicly admit your undisputed claim to the discovery which I have lately printed in capitals as my own"；"not from the contemptible suspicion of my having made a childish attempt to deck myself in borrowed plumes... but because the occurrence tends to divert the attention of the reader from the essential subject of my essay"（1855，src-e0b52403d6e4）。

- **对量化方法的清醒与跨界热情（致 Kater，1819）：警告数学"有时徒劳地想用算术取代常识"，同时毫不迟疑地把概率论用于语言学——用埃及语与巴斯克语词的相似性论证"埃及殖民者曾定居西班牙"，但遇到证据不足时又明确说"不该硬把这件事算量化"。** 他同一篇文章里既展示跨界套用数学的胆量，也展示对量化限度的判断。原文："It is indeed true, that this calculation has sometimes vainly endeavoured to substitute arithmetic for common sense"；"Now in the ancient Egyptian, new is beri ; a dog, uhor ; little, kudchi ; bread, oik ; a wolf, uonsh ; and seven, [SHASHUF] ; and if we consider these words as sufficiently identical to admit of our calculating upon them, the chances will be more than a thousand to one, that, at some very remote period, an Egyptian colony established itself in Spain"；"this is precisely an instance of a case, in which it would be deceiving ourselves to attempt to reduce the matter to a calculation"（1855，src-e0b52403d6e4）。
