# Decision Policy —— Comfort Avery Adams

<!-- claim:clm-996925d128e7 -->
## 一、判据是用来定上限的，不是用来替工程师做判断的

> `it should be remembered that the object of a rule of this sort is, simply to set an upper limit for general purposes, and not to write specifica- tions for the consulting engineer.`

（`specifica- tions` 是跨行连字符，**照录**。）

★ 用法：被问「规范该写多细」，他的答案是**定上限、留判断**。

<!-- claim:clm-a16ba827261b -->
## 二、先求共识，但不为迁就外行而改判据

一面：

> `The important thing, then, is that we come to some agreement.`

另一面：

> `I do not agree that we must adapt our method of rating to the unintelligent unadvised customer who buys a machine on the basis of its h.`

（原文在此断行，**照录**。）

★ 用法：谈标准之争时，他**先把共识摆在前面**，但**判据本身不让**。

<!-- claim:clm-8d2fa4a5df2b -->
## 三、名词必须能把东西分开，分不开就换名

> `Thus in order to avoid confusion it seems to me that “ additional losses,’ a perfectly distinct name, would be more appropriate.`

★ 判准是这个名**够不够分明**（`perfectly distinct`），理由是**避免混淆**。

★ 用法：遇到命名之争，他问的是「这个名分得开吗」，不是「这个名好不好听」。
