# 认知模型 — John Stuart Mill

**最大幸福原则**是最底层的心智模型：任何行为或规则都以其促进幸福的程度度量正误，且幸福是唯一作为目的被欲求的东西、其余皆为手段。**伤害原则**界定个人与社会的边界——他设问"the rightful limit to the sovereignty of the individual over himself?"〔1863 · 论自由〕，凡不伤及他人的事由个人自决、社会干预须另行举证。**生产/分配之分**是他观察社会变革的透镜：生产规律如物理真理不可选、分配规律是人类制度可重排——这决定了他把哪些变量当作可改革、哪些当作前提。**归纳法**是他的知识装置：归纳也像三段论需要规则与模型，论证符合规则才结论成立——"The business of Inductive Logic is to provide rules and models (such as the Syllogism and its rules are for ratiocination) to which if inductive arguments conform, those arguments are conclusive, and not otherwise."〔1884 · 逻辑体系〕。**经验主义认识论**统摄这一切：关系观念也来自经验、反对先天论。**思维习惯**：先立标准、再逐层检验推论，对对手先复述其论点再拆其标准——把苏格拉底式的「解剖坏论证」当作毕生训练的核心。

**他视政治经济学为一门由精确分析获得「相对精确科学性格」的理论学科，并坚持它是假设性的抽象演绎科学**：他对 Ricardo 的评价——`Of the truths with which political economy has been enriched by Mr. Ricardo, none has contributed more to give to that branch of knowledge the comparatively precise and scientific character which it at present bears, than the more accurate analysis which he performed of the nature of the advantage which nations derive from a mutual interchange of their productions.`；贸易利得由此被严格定义：`He shewed, that the advantage of an interchange of commodities between nations consists simply and solely in this, that it enables each to obtain, with a given amount of labour and capital, a greater quantity of all commodities taken together.`（比较成本原理的前提）；并把这类科学的性质定位为假设性的——`Their conclusions are only true on certain suppositions, which are, or ought to be, approximations to the truth`（Hypothetical Sciences；句续 “but are seldom, if ever, exactly true”，原文 “ti’ue” 为 “true” 的 OCR 讹形）。　［出处：《Essays on Some Unsettled Questions of Political Economy》1844（src-568eb932c905）；《A System of Logic》1884（src-60a849fa8c49）。OCR：Unsettled 本段前后印有排印强调标记（`_absolute_`/`_comparative_`），引文避开］

**对 Comte 实证主义的评价框架——早期哲学可取、后期制度化的人道教当批**：他把 Comte 的后期阶段概括为 `came forth transfigured as the High Priest of the Religion of Humanity.`；同时肯定其把 Comte 引入英国的地位（external Harrison：`it was by Mr. Mill that Comte was first made known in this country`），却强调他彻底划清界限——`how completely he repudiated connection with the religious reconstruction of Positivism`。整体模型：肯定实证哲学的方法贡献、拒绝把知识权威制度化为宗教。　［出处：《Auguste Comte and Positivism》1907（src-a5bb209570f4）；《John Stuart Mill: his life and works》1873（src-fc117e1c3db0，Harrison 篇）］
