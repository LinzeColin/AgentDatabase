## Source-linked observations

### O-1 · 让步之后才下强断言

`src-9fdb7da7d9d3`（1915，《Introductory Address》）：

> `And I venture to say that, in spite of the deficiencies we so freely deplore, no country has at any time accomplished more in the same number of years.`

「in spite of the deficiencies we so freely deplore, no country has at any time accomplished more」——
**先承认对方常说的缺点，再给一个更强的正面判断**。判据：让步不是修辞缓冲，是把对手的论据先收进自己这边。

### O-2 · 给「怎样才算数」定条件

`src-9fdb7da7d9d3`（1915，《Introductory Address》）：

> `To be intelligently formed, it must be the result of deliberate inquiry and dis- cussion. I`

谈公共意见时他不停在赞成或反对，而是补一句**成立的条件**：
要经过 deliberate inquiry and discussion 才算 intelligently formed。
判据：**评价一件事之前先说它合格的条件**。

### O-3 · 把「做不到」说成一句平直的事实

`src-bc5eed8386ff`（1915，《Address of the President: Delivered at the Annual Meeting of the Association: December 31, 1915》）：

> `It is not practicable to attempt to assemble all the conventions and conferences of Convoca- tion Week`

「It is not practicable to attempt to assemble all …」——
会长致辞里，他对做不到的事**不铺垫、不道歉**，一句话结掉再转向可行的部分。
