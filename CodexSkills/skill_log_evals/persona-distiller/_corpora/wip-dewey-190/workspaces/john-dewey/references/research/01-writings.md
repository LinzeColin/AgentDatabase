## Source-linked observations

### O-1 · 他先把「我要你看的是哪一件」单独说一句，再展开

`src-0ca65cad1b2c`（1900，《The school and society;》）：

> `It is to this, then, that I especially ask your attention : the effort to conceive what roughly may be termed the "New Education" in the l`

整句的主干是「It is to this … that I especially ask your attention」——
**先划定注意力的落点，再补充那件事是什么**。他不是先讲内容再总结，
而是把「请你看这里」做成一个独立动作。

### O-2 · 定义靠**排除**推进：先说不算什么

`src-e35552b6cd32`（1909，《How We Think》）：

> `Second, the term is restricted by excluding whatever is directly presented; we think (or think of) only such things as we do not directly see, hear, smell, or`

「the term is restricted by **excluding** whatever is directly presented」——
他给「思维」下定义的办法是一层层减：先把直接看到、听到、闻到、尝到的排除出去。
判据：**减法先于加法**，被排除的那一项每次都写明。

### O-3 · 用一个具体书名去驳一个抽象定义

### O-4 · 从「什么在顶回来」定义对象

`src-d2ba66e8dd88`（1922，《Human Nature And Conduct》）：

> `When we are attempting to control anything we are acutely aware of what resists`

「we are acutely aware of what **resists** us」——他定义「对象」不靠属性，
靠**它在你试图控制时顶回来的那一下**。判据：概念用「遇到阻力的经验」来锚，不用本质。

### O-5 · 书的立场在开篇就说成一句可反驳的断言

`src-e35552b6cd32`（1909，《How We Think》）：

> `This book represents the con- viction that the needed steadying and centralizing factor is found in adopting as the end of endeavor that atti- tude of mind, that habit of thought, w`

「This book represents the conviction that …」——他把整本书压缩成一句**可以被反对**的主张，
而不是一段主题描述。判据：**开篇给的是立场，不是范围**。
