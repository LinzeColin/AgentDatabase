## Source-linked observations

### 一、★★ 他写东西的单位是**问句与编号**，不是章节

`src-af673c29709f`（No.39，1927）正文一开头就摆出结构：

> `“HOW TO MAKE AND SAVE MONEY ON THE FARM” The winter season is fast approaching, and`
> `with the promised short- age of crops in many sections, it is highly important that every`
> `farm- er and his family look ahead, plan and work together with all their might to raise`
> `and save every possible thing on the farm, garden, orchard, etc., that can be used as food`
> `for either man or beast.`

紧接着**整篇按问答排**：

> `QUESTION 1 Should the farmer keep a cow? Answer — Yes, every farmer should keep one or`
> `two good cows; a good cow is half of any family’s living, as she will furnish all the butter,`
> `milk, cream, etc., that the ordinary family can use, and if properly cared for a surplus can`
> `be had to sell.`

**先给答案（`Yes`），再给理由，再给可核的后果（能自用、还有余可卖）。**

★ 同一形态在 No.31 的标题里就写着：**`105 WAYS OF PREPARING`**——
**他的产出单位是「几种做法」，不是「一篇论述」。**

### 二、写土壤时，他从**古人**起手，不从化学起手

`src-b37e96bfb234`（No.6，1905），题名页署名之后的第一句：

> `HOW TO BUILD UP WORN OUT SOILS G. W. Carver, M. S. Agr. Director The subject of soil`
> `improvement through natural agencies has been one of much concern to both ancient and`
> `modern agricul- turists. The ancient Egyptian knew that if he let his land lie idle, (rested)`
> `as he termed it, he was able to produce a much better crop`

**「古埃及人就知道」——他先立一个读者能接受的常识，再往上加。**
而那一句里已经埋着一个可测的量：

> `that crop would be in quantity and quality, all other things being equal, proportionate to`
> `the length of time this land had been rested.`

**`proportionate to`——他给的不是「地要歇」，是「歇多久与收成成比例」。**

### 三、他把「同一种东西有几个等级」当成常识来讲

`src-97f3515542b7`（No.37，1918）：

> `SWEET POTATO FLOUR There are several grades of this product and quite as many ways to`
> `manufacture them. Each one of these flours or meals (as most millers insist upon calling`
> `them) has a particular character of its own and is therefore adapted to certain uses`

★ 括号里那句 `(as most millers insist upon calling them)` 值得留意：
**他知道行业里的人怎么叫它，并且把这件事写出来。** 写的是给农民与磨坊主看的东西。

### 四、他也写过纯分类学的论文，而且是独著

`src-da062ea8a2ce`（PIAS Vol.7, 1900, pp.161–165）——真菌分类，本人第一人称：

> `I have consulted the following works`
> `I am also indebted to Prof. L. H. Pammel who has kindly examined some of the specimens for me`
> `I have arranged the specimens alphabetically.`

★★ **这一篇与那些「105 种做法」出自同一个人。**
一边是给农民的操作手册，一边是给学会的物种表——
**两种体例都在，且都在他自己的署名之下。**

★ 该篇后由 Bulletin No.4（`src-30f132430c2a`）**修订重印**，扉页明写：
`Revised and Reprinted from the Reports of the Iowa Academy of Science iSyy.`
（★ `iSyy.` 是 `1899.` 的 OCR 讹字——**照录，不改**。）
**两件同源，No.4 记 P2，不进去重后的一手计数。**

### 五、1899 年那篇是**宣读稿**，不是发言记录

`src-52f2e2df90ff`：

> `A FEW HINTS TO SOUTHERN FARJTERS BY FROF. G. W. CARVER, OF TUSKEGF.E INSTITUTE`
> `The virgin fertility of our southern soils and the vast amount of cheap and unskilled`
> `labor that has been put upon them, have been a curse rather than a blessing to`
> `agriculture; this exhaustive system of cultivation, the destruction of forests, the rapid`

（`FARJTERS`／`FROF.`／`TUSKEGF.E` 均为 OCR 讹字。）

★★ **开篇第一句就是一句判断，而且是逆着「南方土地肥沃」这个常识说的**：
`have been a curse rather than a blessing`——**先天的肥力加上廉价劳力，是诅咒不是恩赐。**
这与 No.6（1905）「古埃及人就知道要让地歇」是同一个关切的两种说法：
**耗地的耕作方式，是他反复回到的那件事。**

★ **归 writings 不归 conversations**：它是事先写好、当场宣读的论文；
会后 discussion 里发言的是 Troyer 与 Frissell，**他本人没有在讨论中开口**。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **W-1 不等于「他不做研究」。** PIAS 那三篇与 No.4 是分类学工作；
  **「产出单位是做法」说的是他面向农民的那一大类，不是他的全部。**
- ★ **W-4 的两篇 PIAS（1896）是合著**（`BY L. H. PAMMEL AND GEO. W. CARVER.`／
  `BY F, C. STEWART AND G. W. CARVER.`（`F,` 的逗号是 OCR，**原字如此，不改**）），**只有 Vol.7 那篇是独著**。
  **举「独著」时只许举 Vol.7。**
- **W-3 的 `proportionate to` 是他的措辞，不是实验结论**——
  本道没有任何一处给出该比例的数据。**不许渲染成「他量出了比例」。**
