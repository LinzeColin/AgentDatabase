## Source-linked observations

### 一、开场：先说自己知道多少，**再说自己不知道多少**

逐字照录（含 OCR 讹字）：

> `The Chairman. All right, Mr. Carver. We will give you 10 minutes.`
> `Mr. Carver. Mr. Chairman, I have been asked by the United Peanut Growers' Association`
> `to tell you something about the possi- bility of the peanut and its possible extension.`
> `I come from Tuske- gee, Ala. I am engaged in agricultural research work, and I have`
> `given some attention to the peanut, **but not as much as I expect to give.**`

**开口第一段就自设上限**：受谁之托、来自哪里、做的是什么，
**以及「我在这上面下的功夫还不够多」**。

### 二、★★ 被起哄时，他**不接那一句，只接实物**

> `Mr. TiLsON. Do you want a watermelon to go along with that 1`
> `Mr. Carver. Well, of course, you do not nave to have it. Of course, if you want a desert,`
> `that comes in very well, but you know we can get along pretty well without dessert.`
> `**The recent war has taught us that.**`

（`that 1` 是 OCR，原文是问号。）

**1921 年，一位众议员对一位黑人科学家说了这句话。**
他的回答**一个字都没碰那句话的言外之意**，只把「甜点可有可无」接下去，
**并用刚过去的战争把它转成一句关于节用的话。**

★★ **本条只陈述文本里发生了什么，不推断他当时的感受**——语料里没有一句支持那种推断。

### 三、报数与报未知，出现在同一句里

> `Mr. Carver. The sweet-potato products number 107 up to date. **I have not finished working**`
> `**with them yet.** The peanut products are going to beat the sweet-potato proaucts by far.`
> `I have jiist ]>egun with the peanut. **So what is going to come of it why we do not know.**`

三个层次：**已有的数（107）／未完成（not finished）／未知（we do not know）**。
**数是确的，前景是敞开的。**

### 四、被问机理时，用**对方熟悉的东西**作参照

> `Mr. Hawley. Is that made from the peanut i`
> `Mr. Carver. Made from the peanut milk; yes, sir.`

（同一轮稍后：）

> `the peanut milk has about the same quantity of curds in it as the cow's nailk has, and the`
> `whey settles out the same as it does from cow's milk, and leaves this clear curd.`

★ **上面两段中间有省略**，故分开引，**不合成一条带省略号的「逐字引文」**——
本项目今天刚记过一条规矩：**引一段非原样的字，就说明它是什么。**

不讲成分表，讲「**和牛奶一样**」。**对面坐的是议员，不是化学家。**

### 五、被问立场时，答得极短

> `Mr. Treadwat. Did the institute send you here or did you come of your own volition ?`
> `Mr. Carver. **The United Peanut Association of America, sir, asked me to come.**`

**问的是「谁派你来的」，他只答谁请的，不多说一个字。**

### 六、场面是热的，不是冷的

记录里有 `[Laughter.]`，主席两次维持秩序
（`Yes, let us have order in the room.`／`Yes. I will have to try to hold the room in order.`），
他自己也接了一句：

> `Mr. Carver. I am not ready to use them just now. They ^11 come later ii my 10 minutes`
> `are extended. [Laughter.]`

收尾：

> `Mr. Carver. Yes sir. You have seen, gentlemen, just about half of them.`
> `There is just about twice this many more.`
> `The Chairman. **We want to compliment you, sir, on the way you have handled your subject.**`

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- ★★ **C-3 不许被渲染成「他反击了」或「他忍下了」。**
  文本里**只有「他没有接那句话」**这一个事实。**关于他情绪的描写一律不许写。**
- **C-1 的射程是「公有领域可得的」，不是「存在过的」。**
  1897–1930 的塔斯基吉黑人大会年会记录**很可能记过他的发言**，只是没有数字化本
  （HathiTrust 记录 000063320 列出 v.1 1897／v.4 1900／v.8 1904 三卷全览，
  **被 Cloudflare 挡住未取到，未绕过**）。**取到之后本道要重做。**
- **C-4 的「107」是他当时的说法**，不是核实过的产品数——**引用必须带 `up to date`。**
