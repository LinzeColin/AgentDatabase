## 生卒与出身

Thomas Telford 1757 年生于苏格兰 Dumfries 郡 Eskdale——"The early part of my life was spent in employment as a mason, chiefly in my native district of Eskdale a mountainous tract of the County of Dumfries"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。1834 年 9 月初去世，Rickman 在 Preface 中记载："More than three years having elapsed since the death of Mr. Telford, in the beginning of September 1834"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000001 --> Telford 自述早年做石匠，建造农舍、小桥，这段手工经历被他视为工程师教育的根基——"Youths of respectability and competent education, who contemplate Civil Engineering as a profession, are seldom aware how far they ought to descend in order to found the basis of future elevation"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000013 -->

## 职业转型与主要工程

1793 年，Ellesmere Canal 委员会邀请 Telford 主持工程，他自此从石匠/建筑师正式转向土木工程——"from that time directed my attention solely to Civil Engineering"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000002 -->

Pontcysyllte 渡槽于 1805 年通航，以铸铁水槽取代夯土防水层，造价 £. 47,018。Telford 自述该工程"was unprecedented, and generally considered hazardous"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000003 --> Chirk 渡槽于 1796 年 6 月 17 日奠基，1801 年完工，造价 £. 20,898（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。

Caledonian Canal 于 1823 年末通航，但 Telford 坦承该运河"has not accomplished its primary and national object, in facilitating the conveyance of Baltic timber to the western ports of Great Britain and to Ireland"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000005 -->

Menai 悬索桥于 1819 年 8 月 10 日由驻地工程师 W. A. Provis 奠定第一块基石，1826 年建成通车。桥共 16 条链，每链 935 条铁杆，全长 1,710 英尺（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000004 --> 悬索桥设计源于 1814 年 Runcorn 桥实验——"I recommended a bridge of wrought iron, upon the suspension principle; to prove which, I tried several hundred experiments upon malleable iron"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。

Holyhead Road 改进工程从 1810 年勘测到 1820s 持续约十五年。Telford 自述："This road, established through a rugged and mountainous district, partly along the slope of rocky precipices, and across inlets of the sea, where the mail and other coaches are now enabled to travel at the rate of nine or ten miles an hour, was indeed an arduous undertaking, which occupied fifteen years of incessant exertion"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。

Parnell 记载 Telford 在苏格兰高地建造了 875 英里道路与 1117 座桥梁（src-a47f326e6786）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000009 -->

1834 年 2 月，Telford 向 Treasury 提交伦敦供水报告，排除 Thames 水源，选择 Verulam 河与 Wandle 河，总造价约 £. 1,177,840 16s. 5d.（src-01d9132416de）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000006 -->

## 荣誉与职位

1820 年 1 月 25 日，ICE（Institution of Civil Engineers）选举 Telford 为首任会长。决议原文称："a respectful communication be made to Thomas Telford, Esq., F.R.S.E., Civil Engineer, requesting him to patronize this institution, by taking on himself the office of President"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000001 -->

## 出版物

1795 年诗作 *Eskdale, a descriptive poem* 由 Shrewsbury 印刷出版，以英雄双行体描写苏格兰边境 Eskdale 山谷。序言署名 "THOMAS TELFORD"，自述该诗"was written in early Youth, when the Situation of the Author gave him little Opportunity of being acquainted with English Poetry"（src-9e354d032ce5）。<!-- claim:clm-000000000007 -->

1838 年自传 *Life of Thomas Telford* 由 John Rickman 身后编辑出版。书名页标注 "WRITTEN BY HIMSELF" 与 "EDITED BY JOHN RICKMAN, ONE OF HIS EXECUTORS; WITH A PREFACE, SUPPLEMENT, ANNOTATIONS, AND INDEX"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。DLI 副本（src-988d9ca66526）为主副本的数字扫描衍生。<!-- claim:clm-000000000008 -->

## 知识边界

- 1757-1793 年的童年与石匠学徒期缺少文件记录，自传以回忆性叙述而非日期锚点支撑。
- 私人书信未入语料；Telford 与 Rickman 的协作沟通仅见于自传 Preface 的间接叙述。
- 1817 年议会附录与 Edinburgh Encyclopaedia Bridge 词条为密封材料，train 侧不引用。
- ICE 首任会长地位在自传与 Parnell 著作中间接体现，但无独立 ICE 文件入语料。
- 外部第三方（非委员会成员、非 Parnell 圈子）对 Telford 的批评性评价缺失。
