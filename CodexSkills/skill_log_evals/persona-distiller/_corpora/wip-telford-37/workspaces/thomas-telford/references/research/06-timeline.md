## Source-linked observations

- **1757**：Telford 生于苏格兰 Dumfries 郡 Eskdale。自传开篇自述——"The early part
  of my life was spent in employment as a mason, chiefly in my native district of
  Eskdale a mountainous tract of the County of Dumfries"（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1793**：Ellesmere Canal 开工，Telford 从石匠/建筑师转型为土木工程师。自传记载
  ——"the committee of management... were pleased to propose my undertaking the
  conduct of this extensive and complicated work; and feeling in myself a stronger
  disposition for executing works of importance and magnitude than for the details
  of house architecture, I did not hesitate to accept their offer, and from that
  time directed my attention solely to Civil Engineering"（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1795**：诗作 Eskdale 出版，序言署名 THOMAS TELFORD。诗作自述"was written in
  early Youth"（1795，`src-9e354d032ce5`）。

- **1796-1805**：Chirk 渡槽（1796 奠基）与 Pontcysyllte 渡槽建造。自传记载 Chirk
  渡槽——"The first stone of this aqueduct was laid on the 17th June 1796"
  （1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。Pontcysyllte 渡槽——"This singular aqueduct was
  opened in 1805"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`），造价 £. 47,018。

- **1801-1803**：Telford 受政府委托勘察苏格兰——"In the year 1801... Government
  employed me to survey the coasts of Scotland... The result of my investigations
  comprehended the establishment of naval stations, improving or creating ports,
  constructing roads, building bridges, and opening a navigable communication
  along the Great Glen of Scotland, by the Caledonian Canal"（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1803-1823**：Caledonian Canal 建造。自传记载——"The navigation by the
  Caledonian Canal between the two seas was opened at the latter end of 1823"
  （1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。Telford 坦承运河未实现主要目标——"the Caledonian
  Canal has not accomplished its primary and national object, in facilitating the
  conveyance of Baltic timber"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1810-1811**：Holyhead Road 勘测——"I was directed to include the passage over
  the Menai strait, also over the Conway estuary; and designs for both these
  accompanied my Report of 1811"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。Telford 自述——
  "I completed extensive surveys, and made my Report to the Lords of the Treasury
  on the 22d of April 1811"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1815**：Holyhead Road 议会委员会成立，Telford 受命为总工程师。Parnell 记载
  ——"it was not until Mr. Telford was employed by the commissioners appointed by
  Parliament, in 1815, for improving the Holyhead road, that he had an opportunity
  of carrying into execution a plan of road-making suitable to a great traffic on
  completely perfect principles"（1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。

- **1819**：Menai Bridge 设计获议会批准，Telford 在 Select Committee 作证——
  委员会结论"Mr. Telford has completely convinced them of the practicability of
  his plan"（1819，`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。同年 6th Report 记录 Telford 对
  Holyhead Road 英格兰段的勘测报告（1819，`src-c7891703b4e4`）。

- **1819-1826**：Menai Bridge 建造。自传记载 1819 年 8 月 10 日第一块基石由
  驻地工程师 Provis 奠定——"on the 10th day of August, the first stone of this
  great work was laid by W. A. Provis"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。1826 年
  建成通车。自传总结——"each chain is composed of 935 bars; and its total length
  is 1,710 feet, or nearly one-third of a mile"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1827**：General Rules for Repairing Roads 出版，以议会委员会名义发布
  （1827，`src-2df0aa845f9f`）。

- **1833**：Parnell《Treatise on Roads》出版，嵌入 Telford 的规范与合同
  （1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。

- **1834（2 月）**：Telford 向 Treasury 提交供水报告——"Thomas Telford. London,
  17 February 1834"（1834，`src-01d9132416de`）。

- **1834（9 月）**：Telford 去世。Rickman 在 Preface 中记载——"More than three
  years having elapsed since the death of Mr. Telford, in the beginning of
  September 1834"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **1838**：自传《Life of Thomas Telford》由 Rickman 编辑出版（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

## Candidate Claims

- 生平时间线锚点：1757 生于 Eskdale、1793 转型土木工程师、1805 Pontcysyllte 渡槽
  建成、1823 Caledonian Canal 通航、1826 Menai Bridge 通车、1834 供水报告与
  逝世、1838 自传出版（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。
- 工程时间线覆盖从运河（1793-1823）到道路（1810-1830s）到桥梁（1819-1826）到
  供水（1834）的完整职业轨迹（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`、`src-01d9132416de`）。
- 1795 年诗作与 1834 年供水报告分别标志职业起点与终点（`src-9e354d032ce5`、
  `src-01d9132416de`）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

自传的日期叙述由 Rickman 身后编辑整理，部分早期年份（如童年与学徒期）依赖
Telford 的回忆而非文件记录。议会报告的出版年（如 1819 报告）与 Telford 实际
执行勘测的年份（如 1818 受命、1819 提交）需区分。DLI 副本 `src-988d9ca66526`
为同一年份的同一文本，不提供独立日期信息。

## Unknowns and source gaps

1757-1793 年（童年与石匠学徒期）的详细时间线缺少文件记录，自传以回忆性叙述
而非日期锚点支撑。Telford 从 Eskdale 到伦敦、再到 Shrewsbury 的迁移年份在自传
中以叙述方式而非精确日期呈现。

## Handoff to adjudication

timeline 道以自传为核心，辅以议会报告与诗作的出版日期，覆盖 1757-1838 年的
完整职业时间线，支撑从石匠到英国首任 ICE 会长的职业轨迹锚点。
