## Source-linked observations

- **Holyhead Road 路线选择基于地形勘察与距离对比**（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）：
  Telford 自述 1810 年受命勘测后做出路线选择——"I completed extensive surveys,
  and made my Report to the Lords of the Treasury on the 22d of April 1811"
  （1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。他在选择 Coventry 路而非 Oxford 路时给出理由——
  "the Commissioners preferred the Coventry road to that by Oxford, because it was
  ten miles shorter, and because the Liverpool mail-coach travelled a hundred
  miles upon the same line"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。在穿越 Welsh 山区时，
  他做了三次尝试——"I made three attempts to cross the Borewyn ridge... but
  found the lowest pass 1,000 feet above Corwen Bridge"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **Menai Bridge 采用悬索原理的决策基于 1814 年 Runcorn 桥实验**（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）：
  Telford 叙述决策由来——"in the year 1814 I had been called upon to consider
  of the best mode of crossing the river Mersey at Runcorn... I recommended a
  bridge of wrought iron, upon the suspension principle; to prove which, I tried
  several hundred experiments upon malleable iron"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。
  他选择了 Ynys-y-moch 而非 Swillies 作为桥位——"I fixed on that of Ynys-y-moch,
  where the breadth of the estuary, at high-water, is 306 yards"（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

- **Menai Bridge 强度决策以实验数据为依据**（`src-7d31bbb6da22`）：Telford 在证词
  中陈述其安全余量计算——"the weight to be suspended is 342 tons" 且 "I have taken
  a section of 192 square inches, which at 5½ tons to each square inch, will
  support 1,008 tons being a surplus of 666 tons above the real weight of the
  bridge, and there would be required a further weight of 1,008 tons to break
  down the bridge"（1819，`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。他总结——"this I conceive is
  making ample provision against any probable trial to which such a bridge can
  be exposed"（1819，`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。

- **供水方案决策排除 Thames 水源，选择 Verulam 河与 Wandle 河**
  （`src-01d9132416de`）：Telford 先否定旧案——"The Water of the river Thames
  being strongly objected to by the inhabitants of this great city, and also
  condemned in the Report of the Commissioners of Water Inquiry"（1834，
  `src-01d9132416de`）。他在勘察后选定方案——"I found an abundance of pure,
  transparent water, within the distance of 16 miles on the North" 以及
  "within 10 miles on the South"（1834，`src-01d9132416de`）。总造价估算为
  "about 1,177,840l. 16s. 5d."（1834，`src-01d9132416de`）。

- **路面维修制度以合同制取代计日工为关键决策**（`src-2df0aa845f9f`、
  `src-d2402f96bb68`）：General Rules 明确——"All labour by day wages ought, as
  far as possible, to be discontinued. The Surveyors should make out
  specifications of the work, of every kind, that is to be performed in a given
  time. This should be let by contract"（1827，`src-2df0aa845f9f`）。1820 年
  委员会报告确认 Telford 推行此制度的成效——"the success of Mr. Telford's method
  of executing road-work in Wales, fully proves the superior system by which he
  controls the application of the money"（1820，`src-d2402f96bb68`）。

- **Pontcysyllte 渡槽以铸铁水槽取代夯土的创新决策**（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）：
  Telford 叙述传统夯土渡槽的失败教训——"the frosts, by swelling the moist
  puddle, frequently created fissures, burst the masonry, and suffered the water
  to escape"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。他的替代方案——"I had about that time
  carried the Shrewsbury canal by a cast-iron trough at about 16 feet above the
  level of the ground; and finding this practicable, it occurred to me... that
  no very serious difficulty could occur in building a number of square pillars,
  of sufficient dimensions to support a cast-iron trough"（1838，
  `src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。渡槽造价为"£. 47,018"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

## Candidate Claims

- 路线选择基于地形勘测数据（三次穿岭尝试）与距离对比（Coventry 比 Oxford 短 10
  英里）（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。
- 悬索桥设计决策源于 1814 年 Runcorn 桥实验，桥位选择基于河宽与地质
  （`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。
- 桥梁安全余量以实验数据计算——192 平方英寸截面、1008 吨支撑力、666 吨余量
  （`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。
- 供水方案排除 Thames、选择 Verulam 与 Wandle 河，总造价约 117 万英镑
  （`src-01d9132416de`）。
- 路面维修以合同制取代计日工为核心制度决策（`src-2df0aa845f9f`、`src-d2402f96bb68`）。
- Pontcysyllte 渡槽以铸铁水槽取代夯土，造价 47,018 英镑（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

Telford 的自述决策过程有自我辩护色彩——如 Holyhead Road 路线选择可能受政治因素
（Irish Members 的压力）影响，但 Telford 仅以地形与距离为理由。Rennie 在
`src-7d31bbb6da22` 中提出更保守的强度建议（四倍自重），暗示 Telford 原始设计
可能偏于激进。Menai Bridge 的实际造价可能超过 Telford 的初始估算。

## Unknowns and source gaps

Caledonian Canal 的造价超支与"未实现主要国家目标"在自传中有坦承——"the
Caledonian Canal has not accomplished its primary and national object"（1838，
`src-659c3ee6ac1a`），但具体超支数额的议会文件为 1824 年报告（自传引用但未入语料
正文）。Dunmore 港报告为密封材料，train 侧不引用。
