## Source-linked observations

- **Parnell《Treatise on Roads》以二手壳收录 Telford 的规范、图纸与合同**
  （`src-a47f326e6786`）：该书在 "PLANS, SPECIFICATIONS, AND CONTRACTS MADE USE
  OF BY THOMAS TELFORD, ESQ. ON THE HOLYHEAD ROAD" 部分直接嵌入 Telford 的工程
  文件（1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。Parnell 以第二人称叙述 Telford 的成就——
  "The operations were conducted by Mr. Telford; and the result has been the
  constructing of 875 miles of road in every respect suitable to the country, and
  the building of 1117 bridges"（1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。

- **Parnell 评价 Telford 的路面计划为"完全成功"**（`src-a47f326e6786`）：
  "Mr. Telford's plan, which has completely succeeded on the Holyhead Road, the
  Glasgow and Carlisle Road, and several other roads in Scotland, of making a
  regular bottoming of rough, close-set pavement, is a plan that secures the
  greatest degree of hardness that can be given to a road"（1833，
  `src-a47f326e6786`）。Parnell 进一步解释 Telford 方法的技术原理——"By laying
  the stones... with their broadest face downwards, and filling up the interstices
  closely with stone chips well driven in, the earthy bed of the road cannot be
  pressed up so as to be mixed with the coating of broken stones"（1833，
  `src-a47f326e6786`）。

- **1819 年委员会高度评价 Telford 的能力与判断力**（`src-c7891703b4e4`）：
  委员会在报告中称——"Without such skill and experience as Mr. Telford has by long
  practice and superior talents been able to attain, it would have been impossible
  to have made a road without a single inconvenient hill upon it, through such a
  rugged and mountainous country as North Wales"（1819，`src-c7891703b4e4`）。
  委员会还肯定其方法——"Mr. Telford's Report on the Welsh road improvements
  deserves to be read with considerable attention; it gives a very accurate
  description of a method of road-making, which must be quite new to all those
  who have not paid that close attention to the subject"（1819，`src-c7891703b4e4`）。

- **1819 年 Menai Bridge 听证中 Rennie 对 Telford 方案的公开背书**
  （`src-7d31bbb6da22`）：Rennie 被问及是否怀疑可行性时回答——"I have no doubt
  of the practicability of constructing such a bridge, and I presume that Mr.
  Telford has taken care to have all the parts sufficiently strong"（1819，
  `src-7d31bbb6da22`）。委员会的结论也赞扬 Telford——"Mr. Telford has completely
  convinced them of the practicability of his plan. The numerous instances which
  he has already given to the public of his talents as a civil Engineer, fully
  prove that the House may place great confidence in his opinion"（1819，
  `src-7d31bbb6da22`）。

- **Rickman 在自传 Preface 中以编辑者身份呈现 Telford 的晚年状态**
  （`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）：Rickman 叙述 Telford 因听力衰退而退出公共生活——
  "from a growing infirmity of deafness, felt himself uncomfortable in any mixed
  company"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。Rickman 还提到 Telford 对自己写作能力的
  不自信——"he felt himself out of the habit of composing accurately for public
  perusal"（1838，`src-659c3ee6ac1a`），但最终接受编辑协助。

- **Parnell 将 Telford 定位为"Holyhead Road 工程权威"**（`src-a47f326e6786`）：
  "it was not until Mr. Telford was employed by the commissioners appointed by
  Parliament, in 1815, for improving the Holyhead road, that he had an opportunity
  of carrying into execution a plan of road-making suitable to a great traffic on
  completely perfect principles"（1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。
  Parnell 署名为"HONORARY MEMBER OF THE INSTITUTION OF CIVIL ENGINEERS, LONDON"，
  从 ICE 会员视角评价 Telford（1833，`src-a47f326e6786`）。

## Candidate Claims

- Parnell 以二手壳完整收录 Telford 的规范与合同（`src-a47f326e6786`）。
- 委员会报告将 Telford 定位为"兼具才能与实践经验的工程师权威"
  （`src-c7891703b4e4`、`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。
- Rennie 在议会听证中公开背书 Telford 的悬索桥方案（`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。
- Rickman 以编辑者视角呈现 Telford 的晚年状态与写作困境（`src-659c3ee6ac1a`）。
- Parnell 以 ICE 会员身份评价 Telford 的路面方法为"完全成功"（`src-a47f326e6786`）。

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

委员会报告对 Telford 的评价可能因委托关系而偏向正面；Parnell 作为 Holyhead Road
议会委员会主席（Sir Henry Parnell），其评价天然倾向支持 Telford 的工作。Rennie
在证词中也提出更保守的建议——认为桥梁应建为四倍自重强度而非 Telford 原始设计
（`src-7d31bbb6da22`）。Rickman 的 Preface 作为身后编辑文本，可能有美化倾向。

## Unknowns and source gaps

语料中无独立第三方（非委员会成员、非 Parnell 圈子）对 Telford 的批评性评价。
ICE 首任会长的地位在自传与 Parnell 著作中间接体现，但无独立 ICE 文件入语料。
1817 年议会附录为密封材料，train 侧不引用。

## Handoff to adjudication

external 道以 Parnell 著作为核心二手材料，辅以委员会报告与 Rickman 编辑评价，
覆盖 Telford 作为"被议会与同行认可的工程师权威"的外部形象，支撑其"兼具才能
与实践经验"的第三方评价维度。
