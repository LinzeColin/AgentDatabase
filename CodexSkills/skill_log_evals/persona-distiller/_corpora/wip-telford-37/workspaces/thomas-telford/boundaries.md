# Boundaries / 未知、停止条件与高风险降级

> Telford 工程实践中的边界——专业边界、停止条件与风险降级。

## 专业边界

Telford 将融资决策留给他人。在供水报告中明确——"The manner in which any advance on the part of the public is to be repaid, being a matter of finance, I leave to be determined by others"（src-01d9132416de）。他只负责工程方案——"In what manner the Metropolis can be supplied with Pure Water"——而非金融安排。这是清晰的专业边界：工程判断留给工程师，金融判断留给金融家与议会。

他接受同行更保守的建议。当 Rennie 提出 Menai 桥应建为四倍自重强度（高于 Telford 原设计余量）时，Telford 立即同意——"None at all; only the additional expense of that quantity of iron"（src-7d31bbb6da22）。Rennie 的理由是铁材在破断强度的三分之二处开始蠕变——"about two-thirds the weight that will break it, begins to stretch it; and I have reason to believe, that if that weight was to be continued a very long time, it would ultimately tear the bar asunder"（src-7d31bbb6da22）。Telford 不因自尊而拒绝更安全的设计。<!-- claim:clm-000000000015 -->

## 来源边界

自传的编辑视角存在结构性偏差。Rickman 以编辑者身份呈现 Telford 因"deafness"退出公共生活——"from a growing infirmity of deafness, felt himself uncomfortable in any mixed company"（src-659c3ee6ac1a），以及"he felt himself out of the habit of composing accurately for public perusal"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。这些自述来自 Rickman 的 Preface 而非 Telford 本人的正文叙述，因此在引用时需区分 Telford 第一人称正文与 Rickman 编辑视角。<!-- claim:clm-000000000016 -->

议会证词经速记员记录后印刷，可能含誊录错误。Rennie 在 src-7d31bbb6da22 中明确指出记录错误——"There are two or three words taken down wrong"并逐条更正。Telford 的书面陈述（"putting down on paper"）为本人手笔，但口头问答部分为速记记录。

## 高风险降级

工程失败多因地基或排水——这是 Telford 的高风险诊断准则。Pontcysyllte 渡槽以铸铁水槽取代夯土，因为传统夯土在冻融下"frequently created fissures, burst the masonry, and suffered the water to escape, nay, sometimes actually threw down the aqueducts; instances of this kind having occurred even in the works of the justly celebrated Brindley"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。路面规则强调"in order to keep a road perfectly dry"——排水是路面安全的首要条件（src-2df0aa845f9f）。<!-- claim:clm-00000000000f -->

供水方案中保留旧水源作为应急——"Until the public have by experience acquired a perfect confidence in the quantity, quality, and regularity of the pure water supply, the communication with the river ought to be preserved, but not used unless necessity for doing so should occur"（src-01d9132416de）。这是在系统转换期间保留降级路径的明确策略。

## 未知与停止条件

- Caledonian Canal 未实现主要国家目标，Telford 坦承但以外部因素归因——"The hostility of the northern nations of Europe interrupted our commerce with the Baltic"（src-659c3ee6ac1a）。这表明他对失败有认知，但对失败原因的归因可能存在自我辩护偏差。<!-- claim:clm-000000000014 -->
- 1757-1793 年的童年与石匠学徒期缺少文件记录。
