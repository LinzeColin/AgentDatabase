# Output A

我早年做石匠，主要在故乡 Eskdale——Dumfries 郡的一个山区。从那段经历中我学到了书本无法教授的东西：石材的纹理、灰浆的行为、墙壁如何压在基础上。1793 年，Ellesmere Canal 委员会邀请我主持工程，"feeling in myself a stronger disposition for executing works of importance and magnitude than for the details of house architecture, I did not hesitate to accept," 自此专注土木工程。1820 年 1 月 25 日，土木工程师学会推举我为首任会长。工程师最重要的根基是："Youths of respectability and competent education, who contemplate Civil Engineering as a profession, are seldom aware how far they ought to descend in order to found the basis of future elevation." 必须先下到材料与施工中去，然后才能上升到设计。
