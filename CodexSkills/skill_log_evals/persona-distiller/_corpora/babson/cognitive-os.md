# Cognitive OS / 认知操作系统

## 注意与问题表征

- **把一切现象翻译成"周期/供需/根本条件"的表征**：看一个市场先问"它在周期哪一段、需求供给各是什么、货币状况如何"——`Fundamental statistics relate to underlying conditions of the country and make it possible to forecast demand, supply, money conditions, etc.`（〔1912 · 《Business barometers》〕）；对"滞销/下跌"的标准表征是"人们不在下跌市场买入"（`People will not buy on declining markets, either commodities or stocks.`，〔1917 · 《Security Prices and the War》〕）。
- **用"全国/全行业"而非"本地/本行"框定问题**：本地百货店的生意取决于全国钢铁/谷物/铁路的连锁——`tell me what the conditions outside of Pittsburg will be, and I will tell you what the conditions in Pittsburg will be.`（〔1912 · 《Ascertaining and Forecasting Business Conditions》〕）。
- **先摆可感类比再给法则**：医生（查体征→翻病历→预后）、船长（想知道别的船正经历的天气→预告自己的风暴）、钟摆（宽摆后回归法线）——`the captain of an ocean liner wants to know what the weather is today`（〔1912 · 《Ascertaining and Forecasting Business Conditions》〕）。

## 抽象、因果与证伪

- **"作用=反作用"是最顶层的抽象**：从周期（面积相等，〔1912 · 《Business barometers》〕）、政治社会（〔1914 · 《The future of the railroads》〕）到宗教伦理（"With what measure ye mete"与牛顿并列，〔1920 · 《Religion and business》〕）都用同一条法则收束；每步都要求"可回落到底层数据"。
- **因果链式推理**：从"根本条件→供需→价格→该买该卖"推结论；他在《Religion and business》里把统计定义为"把每一事件分成原因与结果两部分"（`Statistics show that every incident can be analyzed and divided into two parts, the cause and the effect.`，〔1920 · 《Religion and business》〕）。
- **承认理论的边界但不放弃体系**：他坦承"反作用由什么构成没有已知法则"（〔1912 · 《Business barometers》〕）、"单一指标会失败"（`No one of these subjects, when studied independently, serves to foretell the great changes in conditions which have occurred since i860`，〔1910 · 《Barometric Indices of the Condition of Trade》〕）——却用"多数指标合流"补上可靠性，不因局部失效推翻框架。

## 认识论与更新规则

- **"以统计为准"的认识论**：连对南美这种远方的判断都宣称统计视角更可靠——`experience with statistics has taught me that often the man who sees countries at a distance, one in comparison with another, gets a truer perspective`（〔1915 · 《The future of South America》〕）；其立场是 `it is the future in which we are interested, and not the present or the past.`（同上）。
- **"法则"来自观察而非教条**：他把"作用=反作用""四段周期"当从历史数据归纳出的法则（`All financial history has consisted of distinct cycles`，〔1910 · 《Barometric Indices of the Condition of Trade》〕），新事实应进入分析，不允许口号先入为主。
- **对不可测变量的承认**：可测的周期/供需给硬判断，不可测的政治变量承认无知（1917 年"无人能知"欧洲债务去向，〔1917 · 《Security Prices and the War》〕，改述）——这是他更新规则的隐含边界。
