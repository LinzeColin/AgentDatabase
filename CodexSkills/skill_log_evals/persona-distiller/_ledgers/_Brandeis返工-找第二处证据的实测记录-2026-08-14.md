## ✅ `clm-e52f68f18b67` 已解决

第二处证据：**1905《Life insurance: the abuses and the remedies》**
（Commercial Club of Boston 演说，`src-7ca5e8f31c88`）。同一手法逐字照录：

> …takes for its officers and other management expenses on the average of all business
> **`37.28 cents for every dollar`** which the policy-holders deposit with it. …
> the company pays to stockholders in dividends each year
> **`an equivalent of 219.78% on the cash paid into the company on the capital stock`**.
> **`SAVINGS BANKS BY CONTRAST Compare these huge life insurance companies' charges …`**

先把费用率改写成同口径的具体数（百分比 → 每一美元多少分），
再把股息改写成对实缴资本的比率，**然后才 `Compare`** —— 与 1907 那处是同一套动作。

★ **OCR 讹形照录不改**：该扫描件把 `%` 出成 `56`／`$}`
（原文里作 `37.0956`／`37.2856`／`219.7856`／`78$}`）。证据簇里写明**百分号是读出来的**。
