## Frontier evaluation/evolution research

- SkillHone: persistent decision history across sessions; store structured decisions and evidence, not private reasoning.
- SkillRevise, SkillGrad and SkillMOO: diagnosis, feedback-driven revision, candidate portfolios and multi-objective selection.
- SkillAxe / SkillLens family: fault attribution, trigger compliance, solution-path and behavior coverage.
- Skill Coverage research: measure whether declared constraints are exercised; coverage is not correctness.
- Skill Shadowing research: library retrieval and activation can degrade an otherwise useful Skill.
- SkillReducer / SkillRet: reduce instruction burden and retrieve only relevant Skill knowledge.
- AgentAssay / SkillsBench / SWE-Skills-Bench: real-task and negative-transfer evaluation; more Skill text can increase cost without increasing success.
- AgentSkillOS and related lifecycle work: modular registries, routing, observability and replaceable runtime adapters.

## Adoption rule

For every adopted mechanism record source, date, version/commit when available, license, adopted abstraction, rejected part, local tests and residual unknowns. No source qualifies as one of five real peers without provenance and product-role evidence. Closed/private systems remain `UNKNOWN`.
