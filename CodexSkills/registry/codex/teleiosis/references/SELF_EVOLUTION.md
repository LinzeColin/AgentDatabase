# Self-Evolution

Teleiosis receives no exemptions.

1. The currently installed stable version is the control plane.
2. It freezes itself as target baseline and creates separate optimizer candidates.
3. Genesis, research, evaluation contract, holdout and reviewer packets remain outside every candidate.
4. Candidates may rewrite the whole implementation, not just prompts, but cannot modify their exam.
5. The same ten perspectives, raw evaluation, competitor research, 2x6 review and thirteenth verifier apply.
6. The stable version cannot promote its own candidate; only the independent verifier can authorize a registry pointer change.

Cross-run evidence may update replaceable meta-strategy defaults. A single task success cannot silently rewrite global optimizer policy.
