1. Run real equal-budget Track A/B/C benchmarks on at least six targets and multiple stochastic trials.
2. Bind provider token/cost receipts and human-minute evidence.
3. Execute library-scale shadowing tests in the actual registry.
4. Deploy the review attestor outside Candidate-controlled storage and collect real 2×6+1 evidence.
5. Validate at least two runtimes and two model families.
6. Reheat immediately on lease expiry, stronger peers, model/runtime releases, security changes or real regressions.

These are event-triggered reheat conditions, not promises of automatic background work.
