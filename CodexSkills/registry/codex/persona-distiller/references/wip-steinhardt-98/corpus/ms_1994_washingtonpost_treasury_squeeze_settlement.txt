TWO INVESTMENT FUNDS SETTLE TREASURY NOTE 'SQUEEZE' CASE
FIRMS TO PAY $76 MILLION OVER 1991 SCANDAL
December 17, 1994
More than
31 years ago
Make us preferred on Google
By Sharon Walsh
Two Wall Street investment funds yesterday agreed to pay $76 million to settle fraud and price-fixing charges in connection with a 1991 auction of U.S. Treasury notes.
Steinhardt Management Co. and Caxton Corp. agreed to settle civil complaints brought simultaneously by the Justice Department and the Securities and Exchange Commission following a 3 1/2-year investigation.
