WINTER 2006 3
Priming the Pump 
of Jewish 
Philanthropy
by MICHAEL H. STEINHARDT
I
have a dream in which Jewish early
childhood educators, who presently
receive an average salary of $9.66
per hour, can raise their own chil-
dren without having to take out loans or
marry rich. I have a dream in which
birthright israel does not have to keep
tens of thousands of potential partici-
pants on waiting lists for lack of funds. I 
have a dream in which non-Orthodox
day schools truly rival the best private 
schools and the Jewish socioeconomic
elite clamor to enter them. 
While these dreams are, alas, remote
and quixotic, American Jews have
achieved levels of wealth unprecedented
in our history . The problem is that we
no longer give much to Jewish causes.
We are donors to universities, museums,
orchestras and hospitals, but when it 
comes to Jewish philanthropy , we fall
short. Today , perhaps 20 percent or less
of Jewish giving goes to Jewish causes.
