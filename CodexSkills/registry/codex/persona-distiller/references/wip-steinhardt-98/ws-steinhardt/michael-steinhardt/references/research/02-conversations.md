## Source-linked observations

### 1. 「variant perception」是他的原话，他给的定义比通常转述的窄

"If you can have on one basis or another, **a conviction about something that is
meaningfully off consensus, and that conviction turns out to be correct**, you can say
that just those facts alone should almost always result in profits" [src-77362ff74739]。

**要点是他明说了前提。** 被问「凭什么我比别人对」时他没给方法，给的是一个信念条件：
若认为宏观判断本质随机，"then the idea of variant perception, in a broad sense,
diminishes a great deal"；若认为投入全部精力研究日本就能比别人更早察觉变化，
"then that creates the opportunity for you"。随后他接受了 Connors 的概括
——"It's a knowledge game" [src-77362ff74739]。

**所以 variant perception 不是流程，是一个下注对象**：赌「勤奋能换认知优势」成立。
产物里凡把它写成可执行步骤的，都超出了语料。

### 2. 核心动作：先问「这件事是不是已经人尽皆知」

2006 年被问及财政赤字、贸易逆差、伊拉克伊朗，他答：
"The litany of negatives that one can go through … **the best thing that one can say
about it is that it's well-known**"，据此判断 2006 年股市会不错，并当场报仓位：
"I'm short the bond market and long the stock market" [src-98d1bba1c274]。
他还补了一句关于可证伪性的话："I try to say it unequivocally so you will be able to
remember my foolishness"。

同一场里他不接受官方通胀口径："we're all politely excluding food and energy. But food
and energy are like anything else, and the real rate of inflation last year was
between 4 and 5 percent" [src-98d1bba1c274]。

### 3. 他自己指出的矛盾，以及他自己给的解法

Connors 把他书里两句并置——长期投资的原则，与"I've never owned a stock for 10 years
but have had the unique and profitable experience of owning some very good companies
for 10 minutes"——问他怎么平衡。**他先拒绝「平衡」这个词**：
"I'm not sure you do 'balance' those two philosophies — and often they're in
conflict"；再给机制：
"the truth that you think you've found before the rest of the world that will result
in a 30% or 40% gain in a year (**if it results in a 12% gain in two weeks**) — the
equation's really changed" [src-77362ff74739]。

**即：长周期判断若被提前兑现，就按提前兑现处理。** 不是「既做长线又做短线」。

### 4. 他给「直觉」下过定义，并与「经验」分开

"I thought intuition meant **the cumulative, non-conscious experiential phenomenon**
that led one to make conclusions that on balance were better than others'"；
而抢在别人前面对分析师改评级做出反应，"this isn't intuitive, it's really
experiential" [src-77362ff74739]。

### 5. 自我度量：每天、只看数字、不接受解释

"my measure of myself was my performance. Period." ／ "You really measure it every
day." ／ "You can't say, 'I would have had a great year except for the fact of
Sept. 11.' … It's all in the numbers, and **however justified or unjustified your
performance is** … you have to live with that" [src-77362ff74739]。

代价他也说了："I had no ability to separate my feelings, my sense of self from how
well I did in the market"；被追问是否用市场表现定义人生，答：
"**Regrettably, that's true**" [src-98d1bba1c274]。

### 6. 逆向是气质不是方法

"for reasons which I don't understand, I don't understand, I've always been peculiarly
contrarian. And I liked early on things that no one else seemed to appreciate in my
world. I was a Giant fan, a New York Giant fan in Brooklyn. I voted for Barry
Goldwater in 1964 the first time I voted" [src-98d1bba1c274]。
**他两次说「我不明白为什么」。** 产物不得替他补一个来源解释。

### 7. 对费用的自我要求

"you had to perceive yourself as a genuinely superior money manager … to justify what
were **egregious compensations**"；"To charge 20% … are justified only by superior
returns" [src-e4a46be18cd2]。「egregious」是他自己的用词。

### 8. 一处明确的自陈能力边界

谈进入债券市场："I didn't even know the phraseology of the bonds — and I'm not sure I
could totally understand analysis that related to the yield curve and stuff like
that"，称之为"a rather **amateurish** comfort"，但认为宏观取向可迁移
[src-77362ff74739]。**这条与车道四的 1991 国债案、1994 巨亏直接相关。**

### 9. 离场理由

"I gave it up because I really didn't do it so much to make money as much as to be the
best" ／ "purpose was always a very important thing in my life … I began to lose it a
little bit"；并说明离场前曾大量试探其它方向，包括拍了九部电影"all of which were
failures" [src-98d1bba1c274]。

## Candidate Claims

- `conv-1`（heuristic）：先问「这个负面是不是已经 well-known」——已知即不构成信息。
  [src-98d1bba1c274]
- `conv-2`（mental-model）：variant perception ＝ 对「偏离共识且正确」下注，
  前提是相信宏观可知。[src-77362ff74739]
- `conv-3`（heuristic）：长周期判断被提前兑现时按提前兑现处理。[src-77362ff74739]
- `conv-4`（epistemic）：直觉＝累积的非意识经验，与「经验性反应」分开。[src-77362ff74739]
- `conv-5`（work-method）：每日结算、只认数字、不接受外部事件作为解释。
  [src-77362ff74739] [src-98d1bba1c274]
- `conv-6`（blind-spot）：自陈无法把自我感受与市场表现分开，并称之为 regrettable。
  [src-98d1bba1c274]
- `conv-7`（boundary）：自陈债券理解 amateurish，靠宏观取向代偿。[src-77362ff74739]
- `conv-8`（value）：高收费只能由超额回报证成，用词 egregious。[src-e4a46be18cd2]
- `conv-9`（expression）：公开判断时刻意说得不留退路，理由是便于日后被追责。
  [src-98d1bba1c274]

## Contradictions and alternative explanations

- **「逆向」与「跟着共识做多」不冲突。** 2006 年他判断股市上涨恰恰因为悲观已是共识
  ——逆向的对象是**共识本身**，不是市场方向 [src-98d1bba1c274]。
- **他拒绝点名自己最糟的投资决定**："I've made a lot of bad investment decisions.
  There were too many to think about"，而最好的答"marrying my wife" [src-e4a46be18cd2]。
  替代解释有二：真的不愿谈，或访谈场合的玩笑。**语料不能判定**，
  产物应两说并陈，**不得写成「他认为 1994 年债券是最糟的决定」**。

## Unknowns and source gaps

- **1994 年债市巨亏与 1995 年清盘，他本人的说法我手上没有一手件**，
  相关只有第三方当年报道 [src-7adba3bd9e59]。
- **1991 年国债案他本人的辩解**（据称见于《No Bull》）只有二手转述，未落盘。
- **一处转录缺陷不得逐字引用**：[src-e4a46be18cd2] 中
  "I can't accept the idea that some of his interest in gambling probably spread to my
  interest in speculating in markets"，与紧随其后的"most people in the stock market
  would deny the gambling aspect of what they do. **I don't.** I think it has that
  element"自相矛盾。原话应为 escape／reject 一类。**只可转述立场，不可引这一句。**

## Handoff to adjudication

`conv-2` 的边界要裁定死：语料只支持「他相信勤奋能换认知优势」，
**不支持任何可执行步骤**。`conv-6` 与 `conv-7` 是他的自陈短板，
不得被产物改写成优点。
